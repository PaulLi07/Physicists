# Archive audit and bilingual review

Reviewed: 2026-10-26. The archive expanded from 531 to 721 people, adding 190 contributors and retaining every original record. Each language edition contains 721 independent dossiers ordered by birth date.

## Research and factual checks

The new search compared AIP history resources, MacTutor, the National MagLab pioneers directory, official Nobel data, Wolf Foundation and ICTP entry points, followed by individual university, laboratory, national archive, academy and paper sources. Access failures, conflicting dates and unresolved historical questions remain documented. No single directory was treated as infallible.

All original records were reviewed for identity, life dates and precision, biography, scientific contribution, collaborator credit, images and sources. The official Nobel Physics cohort contains 230 distinct people; multiple awards are merged by person. Of 66 entries read from the MagLab directory, 63 are included; three were excluded under the archive's scope for general engineering and medical-device inventors.

Corrections distinguish birth from baptism and death from burial, retain historical calendar differences, and address disagreements between biographical summaries and original memorials. Examples include Papin, George Green, Halley, Reynolds, Duhem, Terada, von Laue, Gabor, Giacconi and Grünberg. Source disagreements involving Siegbahn and Yang remain visible. Scientific wording was refined for stationary optical paths, the usual Stokes shift, the Cherenkov threshold in a medium, Haldane's antiferromagnetic chains, collaborator credit and experimental conditions.

Compared with the baseline, 150 original records have editorial, source or image-metadata changes; 15 involve a date value, precision, display or life-status change. These totals include clarification and formatting, and are not counts of factual errors.

## Independent English review

After drafting, a different reviewer read all 721 English records against the audited Chinese facts: 138 early-history, 125 nineteenth-century, 230 Nobel, 204 modern, 10 experimental additions and 14 field-theory additions. The review compared biographies, every contribution, terminology, collaborator credit, qualifications, life-date displays, historical caveats, image descriptions and sources. Shared Nobel text was reviewed in 120 prize-year units and 103 individual supplementary units, with its application checked for every laureate.

An additional publication check removed leaked HTML entities, stylesheet text and duplicated author-template labels, untranslated descriptive credits and spacing defects. Licence links pointing to image downloads were redirected to the image's rights statement. Original-script personal names and legal institutional attributions are intentionally retained. This is semantic review against the source-checked Chinese archive, not a claim that every historical dispute was independently resolved a second time.

## Integrity checks and limitations

Every Chinese–English pair was checked for stable ID, chronological order, date values and precision, life status, contribution and note alignment, source URLs, image type, image hashes and local links. All local images were decoded. Each edition contains 721 dossiers and 721 local image references.

All 190 added images were visually inspected, and 12 caption, classification or provenance findings were corrected. Of 154 fresh Commons source-page requests, 124 succeeded and 30 were rate-limited; existing caches and complementary evidence remain recorded for the latter. The final full English-output check reports no current findings or broken local links.

There are 672 portraits or photographs containing the subject, 29 posthumous or commemorative depictions, and 20 contextual illustrations. The latter two categories are explicitly labelled and must not be presented as authenticated lifetime likenesses. Website republication permission remains unverified for 62 image records.

The archive remains expandable. Historical source conflicts, approximate dates, scope decisions and unavailable authenticated digital portraits remain explicit; worldwide completeness or absolute freedom from error is not claimed.
