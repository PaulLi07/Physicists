# Ernst Chladni

**Born:** 30 November 1756

**Died / status:** 3 April 1827

**Fields:** Acoustics, Vibrations, Meteoritics

## Biography

Chladni was a German physicist initially trained in law who turned to acoustics and supported his research through travelling lectures and demonstrations. He used sand to reveal nodal lines on vibrating plates and also investigated sound speed, instruments and meteorites, becoming a pioneer of experimental acoustics and meteoritics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Made modes and nodal lines of vibrating plates visible through Chladni figures.
- Investigated sound propagation in solids and gases and argued for an extraterrestrial origin of meteorites.
