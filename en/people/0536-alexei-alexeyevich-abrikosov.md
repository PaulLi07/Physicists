## Biography

Alexei Alexeyevich Abrikosov was born in Moscow, USSR (now Russia) and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and superconductivity. Nobel records list Argonne National Laboratory as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed the vortex theory of type-II superconductors. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2003.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the vortex theory of type-II superconductors
- Explained the mixed state in which magnetic flux enters as quantized vortices

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2003
