# Bruno Rossi

**Born:** 13 April 1905

**Died / status:** 21 November 1993

**Fields:** Cosmic rays, Astrophysics

## Biography

An Italian experimental physicist forced abroad by fascist racial laws, Rossi later worked at MIT. He applied fast electronics to cosmic-ray detection and helped establish X-ray astronomy in the space age.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed fast electronic coincidence circuits for cosmic-ray measurements.
- Investigated extensive air showers and meson lifetimes.
- Advanced early space-based cosmic X-ray experiments.
