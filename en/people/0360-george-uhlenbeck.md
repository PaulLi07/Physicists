# George Uhlenbeck

**Born:** 6 December 1900

**Died / status:** 31 October 1988

**Fields:** Quantum mechanics, Statistical physics

## Biography

A Dutch-born theoretical physicist educated at Leiden, Uhlenbeck spent much of his career teaching in the United States. He proposed electron spin with Goudsmit and investigated Brownian motion, transport, and the foundations of statistical physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed electron spin with Samuel Goudsmit.
- Developed the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process with Leonard Ornstein to describe damped stochastic motion.
- Investigated quantum gases and transport theory.
