# Louis Eugène Félix Néel

**Born:** 22 November 1904

**Died / status:** 17 November 2000

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Magnetism

## Biography

Louis Eugène Félix Néel was born in Lyon, France and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and magnetism. Nobel records list University of Grenoble as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed theories of antiferromagnetism and ferrimagnetism. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1970.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed theories of antiferromagnetism and ferrimagnetism
- Explained macroscopic magnetic behaviour through interacting magnetic sublattices

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1970
