**Born:** 21 April 1882

## Biography

Percy Williams Bridgman was born in Cambridge, MA, USA and conducted research in high-pressure physics and experimental methods. Nobel records list Harvard University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed apparatus for producing very high pressures. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1946.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed apparatus for producing very high pressures
- Investigated material properties and phase transitions under extreme pressure

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1946
