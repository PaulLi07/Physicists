# Cecilia Payne-Gaposchkin

**Born:** 10 May 1900

**Died / status:** 7 December 1979

**Fields:** Stellar physics, Astronomical spectroscopy

## Biography

A British-born astrophysicist, Payne-Gaposchkin moved to Harvard to study stellar spectra and complete her doctorate. She later became one of Harvard's first women full professors and used quantum atomic physics to interpret stellar composition.

## Major contributions to physics

- Applied Saha ionization theory to the sequence of stellar spectra.
- Established that stars consist predominantly of hydrogen and helium.
- Conducted extensive research on variable stars and stellar evolution.
