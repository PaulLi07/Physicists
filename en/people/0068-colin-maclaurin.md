## Biography

Maclaurin was a Scottish mathematician who taught in Aberdeen and Edinburgh and became an important expositor of Newtonian methods. He connected analysis and geometry with rotating-fluid equilibria, gravitation and tides, helping disseminate Newtonian mathematical traditions.

## Major contributions to physics

- Studied ellipsoidal equilibria of uniformly rotating fluids, known as Maclaurin spheroids.
- Developed analytical and series methods and applied them to gravitation, tides and mechanics.
