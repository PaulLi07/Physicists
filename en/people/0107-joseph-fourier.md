# Joseph Fourier

**Born:** 21 March 1768

**Died / status:** 16 May 1830

**Fields:** Heat conduction, Mathematical physics

## Biography

Fourier was a French mathematician and administrator who joined the Egyptian expedition, served as prefect of Isère and later worked at the Paris Academy. His study of heat conduction produced a trigonometric-series method that connected physical processes to partial differential equations and later influenced wave and quantum theories.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established the heat-conduction equation and systematically investigated the diffusion of temperature fields.
- Developed Fourier-series methods as tools for boundary-value problems, waves and signal analysis.
