# Friedrich Bessel

**Born:** 22 July 1784

**Died / status:** 17 March 1846

**Fields:** Astrometry, Mathematical physics

## Biography

Bessel was a German astronomer who moved from commerce into astronomy and directed the Königsberg Observatory for many years. Combining precision instruments, error analysis and mathematics, he measured the parallax of 61 Cygni in 1838 and developed methods involving the functions that later bore his name.

## Major contributions to physics

- Published the first widely accepted reliable stellar-parallax measurement, determining the distance of 61 Cygni.
- Investigated celestial positions, observational errors and Bessel functions, providing tools for astronomy and mathematical physics.
