# Heinrich Wilhelm Dove

**Born:** 6 October 1803

**Died / status:** 4 April 1879

**Fields:** Physics of meteorology, Acoustics, Optics

![Portrait of Heinrich Wilhelm Dove](../portraits/5786dc76d259b27b1431.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Dove was a German physicist and meteorologist who worked in Berlin. He combined comparisons of weather observations with physical explanations, studying winds, temperature distributions and acoustics. His organizational work helped meteorology develop into a coordinated observational science.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated atmospheric circulation and changes in wind direction
- Discovered binaural beats produced by slightly different tones presented to the two ears
- Studied optical polarization and related experimental apparatus
