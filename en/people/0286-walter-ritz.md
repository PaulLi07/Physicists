# Walter Ritz

**Born:** 22 February 1878

**Died / status:** 7 July 1909

**Fields:** Spectroscopy, Variational methods, Mathematical physics

## Biography

Ritz was a Swiss theoretical physicist who studied and worked in Zürich and Göttingen before dying young from illness. His mathematical approximation methods and empirical organization of spectra both became enduring tools, despite the brevity of his career.

## Major contributions to physics

- Formulated the combination principle expressing spectral wavenumbers as differences of terms
- Developed variational approximation methods for vibration and boundary-value problems
- Investigated theoretical questions in spectroscopy and electrodynamics
