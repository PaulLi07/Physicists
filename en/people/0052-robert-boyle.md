# Robert Boyle

**Born:** 25 January 1627

**Died / status:** 30 or 31 December 1691 (sources differ)

**Fields:** Gas physics, Experimental methods, History of theories of matter

![Robert Boyle — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/f0443ce41c4610828462.jpg)

The creator, date and form of this likeness are documented at the image source.

## Biography

Boyle was an Irish-born experimental scientist active mainly in England and an early member of the Royal Society. With Hooke and others, he used improved air pumps to investigate gases. He promoted repeatable experiments and mechanical explanations of nature and strongly influenced experimental traditions in chemistry and physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established the approximate inverse relationship between pressure and volume for a fixed quantity of gas at constant temperature.
- Used air pumps to investigate combustion, respiration and sound propagation, advancing gas and vacuum experiments.

## Historical and source notes

- Institutional and scholarly sources give both 30 and 31 December 1691 for his death; neither is represented as an undisputed exact day. Birth is usually given as 25 January, although individual Birkbeck and Science Museum pages give 27 January; the Royal Society date is retained with this discrepancy noted.
