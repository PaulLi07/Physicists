## Biography

Adam G. Riess was born in Washington, D.C., USA and conducted research in cosmology and observational astronomy. Nobel records list Johns Hopkins University and Space Telescope Science Institute as the affiliations at the time of the award. They discovered the accelerating expansion of the universe through distant Type Ia supernovae. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2011.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the accelerating expansion of the universe through distant Type Ia supernovae
- Provided crucial observational evidence motivating dark-energy research

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2011
