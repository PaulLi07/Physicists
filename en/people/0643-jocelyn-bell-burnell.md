# Jocelyn Bell Burnell

**Born:** 15 July 1943

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Radio astronomy, Neutron stars

## Biography

Born in Northern Ireland, Bell Burnell helped build a radio telescope as a Cambridge graduate student and identified regular pulses in its records in 1967. She later worked at British universities and supported science education and equitable research opportunities.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the first pulsar radio signals.
- Helped establish the observational study of rapidly rotating neutron stars.
- Advanced radio and high-energy astronomy.
