# Rolf Widerøe

**Born:** 11 July 1902

**Died / status:** 11 October 1996

**Fields:** Accelerator physics

## Biography

A Norwegian physicist and engineer educated in Germany, Widerøe developed accelerator concepts and experiments that inspired several later high-energy machines. He also promoted the development of accelerators for radiation therapy.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed and experimentally demonstrated early radio-frequency linear acceleration.
- Formulated the basic condition for electron induction acceleration and investigated betatron technology.
- Advanced accelerator applications in medical radiotherapy.
