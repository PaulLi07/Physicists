# Artur Ekert

**Born:** 19 September 1961

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Quantum information, Quantum foundations

## Biography

A Polish-born theorist who researched at Oxford and later led Singapore’s Centre for Quantum Technologies, Ekert connected entanglement and Bell tests with cryptographic security and advanced quantum communication research.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed entanglement-based quantum key distribution using Bell tests.
- Investigated quantum computing and information processing.
- Built quantum-technology research and training programmes.
