# David J. Thouless

**Born:** 21 September 1934

**Died / status:** 6 April 2019

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Topological matter

## Biography

David J. Thouless was born in Bearsden, United Kingdom and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and topological matter. Nobel records list University of Washington as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed the theory of topological phase transitions in two-dimensional systems with Michael Kosterlitz. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2016.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the theory of topological phase transitions in two-dimensional systems with Michael Kosterlitz
- Made major contributions to the topological understanding of the quantum Hall effect

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2016
