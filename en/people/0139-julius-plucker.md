# Julius Plücker

**Born:** 16 July 1801

**Died / status:** 22 May 1868

**Fields:** Gas discharges, Spectroscopy, Mathematical physics

## Biography

A German mathematician and experimental physicist, Plücker worked for many years at the University of Bonn. He moved from analytical geometry into magnetism, gas discharges and spectroscopy, combining mathematical insight with experiments that helped prepare the discovery of the electron.

## Major contributions to physics

- Studied low-pressure gas discharges and the magnetic deflection of cathode rays
- Worked with Hittorf on gas spectra, contributing to the foundations of spectroscopy
- Developed line geometry, an important mathematical framework later used in physics
