## Biography

An American theorist at Cornell, Mermin worked in condensed-matter physics and quantum foundations and became known for clear exposition. His solid-state textbook with Ashcroft trained generations of physicists.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proved, with Wagner, the absence of finite-temperature long-range order in low-dimensional continuous-symmetry systems under specified conditions.
- Investigated crystals and topological defects.
- Developed concise arguments about quantum nonlocality and contextuality.
