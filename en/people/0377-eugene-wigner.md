# Eugene Paul Wigner

**Born:** 17 November 1902

**Died / status:** 1 January 1995

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Symmetry, Mathematical physics

## Biography

Eugene Paul Wigner was born in Budapest, Austria-Hungary (now Hungary) and conducted research in nuclear physics, symmetry and mathematical physics. Nobel records list Princeton University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They systematically applied group theory and fundamental symmetries to quantum mechanics. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1963.

## Major contributions to physics

- Systematically applied group theory and fundamental symmetries to quantum mechanics
- Investigated nuclear structure and symmetry principles of elementary particles

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1963
