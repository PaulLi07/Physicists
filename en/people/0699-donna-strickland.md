**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

## Biography

Donna Strickland was born in Guelph, Canada and conducted research in laser physics and ultrafast optics. Nobel records list University of Waterloo as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed chirped-pulse amplification with Gérard Mourou. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2018.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed chirped-pulse amplification with Gérard Mourou
- Addressed optical damage during the direct amplification of ultrashort laser pulses

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2018
