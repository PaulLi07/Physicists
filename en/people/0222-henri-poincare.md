# Henri Poincaré

**Born:** 29 April 1854

**Died / status:** 17 July 1912

**Fields:** Mathematical physics, Celestial mechanics, Prehistory of relativity

## Biography

Poincaré was a French mathematician and theoretical physicist based in Paris. He studied celestial mechanics, topology, differential equations and electrodynamics, making foundational contributions to dynamical systems and the development of relativity while examining the structure of scientific theories.

## Major contributions to physics

- Revealed the complexity of the three-body problem and helped found qualitative dynamics
- Investigated the group properties of Lorentz transformations and the relativity principle, contributing to the prehistory of special relativity
- Developed mathematical methods in electrodynamics and celestial mechanics
