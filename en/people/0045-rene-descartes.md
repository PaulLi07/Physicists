# René Descartes

**Born:** 31 March 1596

**Died / status:** 11 February 1650

**Fields:** Geometrical optics, History of theoretical physics

## Biography

Descartes was a French philosopher and mathematician who lived for many years in the Dutch Republic and moved to Sweden late in life. He sought unified mathematical and mechanical explanations of nature. His optics and analytic geometry were influential, although specific vortex and collision theories were later corrected or superseded.

## Major contributions to physics

- Presented the law of refraction in the Dioptrics and explained the rainbow through reflection and refraction.
- Developed a mechanical account of nature and a conservation concept for motion, promoting mathematical approaches to physical problems.
