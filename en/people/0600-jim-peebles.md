## Biography

James Peebles was born in Winnipeg, Canada and conducted research in physical cosmology. Nobel records list Princeton University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They investigated the cosmic microwave background, structure formation and dark matter. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2019.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated the cosmic microwave background, structure formation and dark matter
- Established important theoretical foundations of modern physical cosmology

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2019
