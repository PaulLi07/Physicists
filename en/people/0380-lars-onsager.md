# Lars Onsager

**Born:** 27 November 1903

**Died / status:** 5 October 1976

**Fields:** Statistical physics, Thermodynamics

## Biography

A Norwegian-born theoretical physical chemist at Yale, Onsager used mathematics to investigate irreversible processes and phase transitions. His reciprocal relations in irreversible thermodynamics earned the 1968 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established the Onsager reciprocal relations.
- Solved the zero-field, two-dimensional square-lattice Ising model exactly.
- Investigated quantized vortices and turbulence statistics.
