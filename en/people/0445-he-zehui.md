# He Zehui

**Born:** 5 March 1914

**Died / status:** 20 June 2011

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Particle detection

## Biography

Born in Suzhou, He studied at Tsinghua and in Germany before conducting fission experiments in France. She returned to China with Qian Sanqiang in 1948 and worked on nuclear physics, cosmic rays, and particle detectors at the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

## Major contributions to physics

- Studied and helped discover multibody uranium-fission processes with Qian Sanqiang and other collaborators.
- Developed nuclear emulsions and particle-detection techniques.
- Advanced Chinese cosmic-ray, high-energy, and space experiments.
