# Serge Haroche

**Born:** 11 September 1944

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Quantum optics, Quantum information

## Biography

Serge Haroche was born in Casablanca, Morocco and conducted research in quantum optics and quantum information. Nobel records list Collège de France and École Normale Supérieure as the affiliations at the time of the award. They controlled atom-photon interactions using cavity quantum electrodynamics. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2012.

## Major contributions to physics

- Controlled atom-photon interactions using cavity quantum electrodynamics
- Developed non-destructive measurements of individual photon states and studies of quantum coherence

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2012
