# Henry Augustus Rowland

**Born:** 27 November 1848

**Died / status:** 16 April 1901

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Spectroscopy, Metrology

## Biography

Rowland was an American experimental physicist at Johns Hopkins who helped professionalize research in the United States. He investigated electromagnetic effects and thermal measurement, becoming especially known for precision diffraction gratings and measurements of the solar spectrum.

## Major contributions to physics

- Demonstrated the magnetic effect of moving electric charge using a rotating charged disk
- Developed concave diffraction gratings and high-precision grating ruling
- Measured the mechanical equivalent of heat and solar spectral wavelengths
