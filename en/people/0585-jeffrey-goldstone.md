# Jeffrey Goldstone

**Born:** 3 September 1933

**Died / status:** 12 October 2026

**Fields:** Quantum field theory, Quantum many-body physics

## Biography

A British theorist who worked at Cambridge and MIT, Goldstone investigated many-fermion systems, spontaneous symmetry breaking, and quantum computation. He died in October 2026.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established Goldstone’s theorem connecting spontaneous breaking of continuous symmetries with massless modes.
- Developed diagrammatic methods for many-fermion systems.
- Investigated quantum computing and algorithms.
