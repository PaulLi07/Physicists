# John Cardy

**Born:** 19 March 1947

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Statistical physics, Quantum field theory

## Biography

A British theorist who worked at UC Santa Barbara and Oxford, Cardy applies conformal field theory and probability to low-dimensional critical phenomena and universality.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed boundary conformal field theory and critical-scaling methods.
- Derived a crossing-probability formula for two-dimensional percolation.
- Investigated conformal state counting and statistical universality.
