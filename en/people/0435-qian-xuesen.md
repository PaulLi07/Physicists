# Qian Xuesen

**Born:** 11 December 1911

**Died / status:** 31 October 2009

**Fields:** Aerodynamics, Engineering control

![Portrait of Qian Xuesen](../portraits/8df38bf84640cb4efe60.png)

## Biography

Born in Shanghai, Qian studied and worked at MIT and Caltech under von Kármán. After returning to China in 1955, he helped establish the country's aerospace programme and developed systematic approaches to engineering problems.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated high-speed aerodynamics, compressible flow, and rocket-flight theory.
- Developed a systematic formulation of engineering cybernetics.
- Advanced the theory and organization of Chinese rocket, missile, and space technology.
