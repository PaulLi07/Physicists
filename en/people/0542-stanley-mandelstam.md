# Stanley Mandelstam

**Born:** 12 December 1928

**Died / status:** 11 June 2016

**Fields:** Quantum field theory, String theory

![Portrait of Stanley Mandelstam](../portraits/386de7404d4ecca163af-ef81a24e6e.jpg)

## Biography

A South African-born theorist educated in Britain, Mandelstam taught at Berkeley. Beginning with analytic scattering amplitudes, he investigated strong interactions, gauge theories, and strings.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced Mandelstam variables and double-dispersion representations.
- Developed gauge-theory and low-dimensional bosonization methods.
- Investigated ultraviolet properties of supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory and string amplitudes.
