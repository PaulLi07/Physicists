# George Gabriel Stokes

**Born:** 13 August 1819

**Died / status:** 1 February 1903

**Fields:** Fluid mechanics, Optics, Mathematical physics

![Portrait of George Gabriel Stokes](../portraits/d01a1dc98f157ea8a6de.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Stokes was an Irish-born British mathematician and physicist who held Cambridge's Lucasian chair and served as President of the Royal Society. He applied mathematical analysis to fluids, light and vibration, helping establish continuum mechanics and physical optics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed equations for viscous fluid motion and the drag law for a sphere at low Reynolds number
- Investigated fluorescence and the usual shift of its emitted light to wavelengths longer than those of the exciting light
- Introduced parameters that describe the polarization state of light
