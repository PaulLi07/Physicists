# Albert Fert

**Born:** 7 March 1938

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Spintronics

## Biography

Albert Fert was born in Carcassonne, France and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and spintronics. Nobel records list Université Paris-Sud and Unité Mixte de Physique CNRS/THALES as the affiliations at the time of the award. They independently discovered giant magnetoresistance. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2007.

## Major contributions to physics

- Independently discovered giant magnetoresistance
- Revealed the influence of electron spin on electrical transport in magnetic multilayers

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2007
