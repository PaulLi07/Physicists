# Thomas Edison

**Born:** 11 February 1847

**Died / status:** 18 October 1931

**Fields:** Electrical technology, Thermionic emission, Applied physics

![Portrait of Thomas Edison](../portraits/37c67f7f5d793cb12fd3.jpg)

## Biography

Edison was an American inventor and organizer of industrial research. His laboratories developed practical electrical lighting, power systems, sound recording and other technologies through sustained collaborative experimentation. His observed thermionic effect also became important in the history of electronic devices.

## Major contributions to physics

- Systematically improved incandescent lighting and associated direct-current distribution systems with his laboratory teams
- Observed one-way conduction in an evacuated incandescent lamp, later called the Edison effect, an early thermionic-emission phenomenon
- Established an industrial research model organized around team experiments and iterative improvement of devices

## Historical and source notes

- His principal roles were inventor and organizer of engineering research.
- Electric lighting, motion pictures and related technologies had multiple pioneers; this entry does not assign sole invention to Edison.
