## Biography

An American-born theorist who taught at Princeton, Bohm left the United States under political persecution and worked in Brazil, Israel, and Britain. He studied plasmas and interpretations of quantum mechanics and helped develop debates about nonlocality.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed a quantum theory with definite particle trajectories.
- Predicted the observable electromagnetic-potential effect with Aharonov.
- Investigated collective plasma motion and quantum many-body theory.
