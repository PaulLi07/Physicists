## Biography

Klaus Hasselmann was born in Hamburg, Germany and conducted research in climate physics and statistical physics. Nobel records list Max Planck Institute for Meteorology as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed stochastic climate models connecting short-term weather fluctuations with long-term climate variation. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2021.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed stochastic climate models connecting short-term weather fluctuations with long-term climate variation
- Developed methods for identifying human influences in climate observations

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2021
