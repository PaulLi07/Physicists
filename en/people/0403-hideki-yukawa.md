# Hideki Yukawa

**Born:** 23 January 1907

**Died / status:** 8 September 1981

**Fields:** Particle physics, Nuclear forces

![Portrait of Hideki Yukawa](../portraits/347563d7909fd666a889.jpg)

For the photographer or artist, date and type of image, consult the original image record.

## Biography

Hideki Yukawa was born in Tokyo, Japan and conducted research in particle physics and nuclear forces. Nobel records list Columbia University and Kyoto University as the affiliations at the time of the award. They proposed that meson exchange explains the interaction between nucleons. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1949.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed that meson exchange explains the interaction between nucleons
- Predicted a new particle associated with the transmission of the nuclear force

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1949
