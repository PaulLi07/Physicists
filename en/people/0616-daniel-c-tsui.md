**Born:** 28 February 1939

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

## Biography

Daniel C. Tsui was born in Henan, China and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and fractional quantum Hall effect. Nobel records list Princeton University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They investigated the fractional quantum Hall effect in two-dimensional electron systems. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1998.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated the fractional quantum Hall effect in two-dimensional electron systems
- Revealed strongly correlated quantum states with fractionalized excitations

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1998
