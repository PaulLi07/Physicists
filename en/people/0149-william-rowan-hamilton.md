# William Rowan Hamilton

**Born:** 4 August 1805

**Died / status:** 2 September 1865

**Fields:** Classical mechanics, Optics, Mathematical physics

## Biography

Hamilton was an Irish mathematician, astronomer and mathematical physicist. Appointed to Trinity College Dublin's Andrews professorship while still young, he also directed Dunsink Observatory. He expressed optics and mechanics through a shared variational framework, laying foundations for modern analytical mechanics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established Hamiltonian mechanics in terms of generalized coordinates and conjugate momenta
- Developed formal connections between optics and mechanics and predicted conical refraction
- Invented quaternions, later important for describing rotations and theoretical physics
