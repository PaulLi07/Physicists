# Heinrich Gustav Magnus

**Born:** 2 May 1802

**Died / status:** 4 April 1870

**Fields:** Fluid mechanics, Thermal physics, Experimental physics

![Portrait of Heinrich Gustav Magnus](../portraits/d81ae3d40e9647ad319d.jpg)

## Biography

Magnus was a German experimental physicist originally trained in chemistry. At Berlin he investigated gases, heat and fluid motion while creating opportunities for younger scientists to learn experimental methods. His name is associated with the transverse force on a rotating body in a flowing fluid.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated the transverse force on rotating objects in fluids, now called the Magnus effect
- Studied gas expansion, vapour pressure and thermal properties of matter
- Helped establish experimental physics teaching and laboratory research in Berlin
