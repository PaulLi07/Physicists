# Albert Einstein

**Born:** 14 March 1879

**Died / status:** 18 April 1955

**Fields:** Relativity, Quantum theory, Statistical physics

## Biography

Albert Einstein was born in Ulm, Germany and conducted research in relativity, quantum theory and statistical physics. Nobel records list Kaiser-Wilhelm-Institut (now Max-Planck-Institut) für Physik as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed special and general relativity, transforming the description of spacetime and gravity. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed special and general relativity, transforming the description of spacetime and gravity
- Explained the photoelectric effect with light quanta and investigated Brownian motion

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1921
