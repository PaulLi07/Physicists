## Biography

Sham is a Hong Kong-born American theoretical physicist educated in Britain who worked for many years at UC San Diego. With Kohn he developed a practical density-functional framework. His later research on many-body effects, optical properties and electron-spin control in semiconductors connects electronic-structure theory with quantum-information research.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed self-consistent auxiliary one-electron equations for ground-state density-functional calculations with Kohn
- Investigated many-body effects, excitons and nonlinear optical processes in semiconductors
- Studied electron spins and their optical control in semiconductor nanostructures
