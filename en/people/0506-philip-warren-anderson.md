# Philip Warren Anderson

**Born:** 13 December 1923

**Died / status:** 29 March 2020

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Disordered systems

## Biography

Philip Warren Anderson was born in Indianapolis, IN, USA and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and disordered systems. Nobel records list Bell Telephone Laboratories as the affiliation at the time of the award. They proposed Anderson localization, explaining how disorder can inhibit electron propagation. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1977.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed Anderson localization, explaining how disorder can inhibit electron propagation
- Made major contributions to magnetism, superconductivity and many-body theory

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1977
