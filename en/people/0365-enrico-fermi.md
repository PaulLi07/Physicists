## Biography

Enrico Fermi was born in Rome, Italy and conducted research in nuclear physics and neutron physics. Nobel records list Rome University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They investigated nuclear reactions and induced radioactivity using neutron bombardment. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1938.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated nuclear reactions and induced radioactivity using neutron bombardment
- Showed that slow neutrons are particularly effective in producing certain nuclear reactions

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1938
