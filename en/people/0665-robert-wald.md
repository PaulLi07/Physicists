# Robert Wald

**Born:** 29 June 1947

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** General relativity, Quantum field theory

## Biography

An American theorist at Chicago, Wald investigates black-hole thermodynamics, quantum fields in curved spacetime, and the mathematical foundations of gravitation. His general-relativity textbook has been widely influential.

## Major contributions to physics

- Related black-hole entropy to Noether charge.
- Developed rigorous quantum-field methods in curved spacetime.
- Investigated black-hole thermodynamics and gravitational stability.
