# Aristotle

**Born:** c. 384 BCE

**Died / status:** c. 322 BCE

**Fields:** Natural philosophy, History of kinematics, History of cosmology

## Biography

The Greek philosopher Aristotle studied in Athens and later founded the Lyceum. He wrote extensively on nature, animals and logic. His physics treated motion, change and causes within a systematic framework that shaped ancient and medieval scholarship, while also becoming a central target of early modern scientific criticism.

## Major contributions to physics

- Systematically discussed motion, time, place and causal explanation in the Physics.
- Developed a distinction between terrestrial and celestial nature that influenced natural philosophy in Europe and the Islamic world for centuries.
