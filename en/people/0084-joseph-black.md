# Joseph Black

**Born:** 16 April 1728

**Died / status:** 6 December 1799

**Fields:** Thermal physics, Calorimetry

![Joseph Black — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/5c02e2c54ff98b1468ce.jpg)

## Biography

Black was a Scottish physician, chemist and investigator of heat, born in Bordeaux and later a teacher in Glasgow and Edinburgh. His precise weighing supported studies of gases. Experiments on phase changes distinguished temperature from heat and influenced steam technology and the development of calorimetry.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the concept of latent heat, showing that a phase change can absorb heat without a corresponding temperature rise.
- Investigated specific heat and heat capacity and advanced quantitative calorimetry.
