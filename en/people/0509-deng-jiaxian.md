# Deng Jiaxian

**Born:** 25 June 1924

**Died / status:** 29 July 1986

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Nuclear-weapons theory

## Biography

Born in Huaining, Anhui, Deng graduated from the National Southwestern Associated University, received his doctorate at Purdue, and returned to China in 1950. He became a major organizer of nuclear-weapons theory under highly classified working conditions.

## Major contributions to physics

- Organized and contributed to the physical design and calculations for China's first atomic bomb.
- Investigated hydrogen-bomb principles and nuclear-weapons theory.
- Built research teams linking theoretical calculation with experimental verification.
