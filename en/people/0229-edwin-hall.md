# Edwin Hall

**Born:** 7 November 1855

**Died / status:** 20 November 1938

**Fields:** Electronic transport, Electromagnetism, Condensed-matter physics

## Biography

Hall was an American experimental physicist who discovered the Hall effect as a graduate student at Johns Hopkins and later taught at Harvard. His investigations of metallic transport under electrical, magnetic and thermal influences linked precision experiments with developing electron theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the transverse voltage produced in a current-carrying conductor by a magnetic field
- Investigated electrical, thermoelectric and thermomagnetic transport in metals
- Provided an experimental effect later central to carrier measurements and quantum transport
