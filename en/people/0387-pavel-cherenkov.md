# Pavel Alekseyevich Cherenkov

**Born:** 28 July 1904

**Died / status:** 6 January 1990

**Fields:** Radiation physics, Particle detection

## Biography

Pavel Alekseyevich Cherenkov was born in Novaya Chigla, Russia and conducted research in radiation physics and particle detection. Nobel records list P.N. Lebedev Physical Institute as the affiliation at the time of the award. They experimentally discovered radiation emitted when charged particles exceed the phase velocity of light in a medium, now called Cherenkov radiation. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1958.

## Major contributions to physics

- Experimentally discovered radiation emitted when charged particles exceed the phase velocity of light in a medium, now called Cherenkov radiation
- Opened ways to detect particles using that radiation

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1958
