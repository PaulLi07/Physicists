# Guido Altarelli

**Born:** 12 July 1941

**Died / status:** 30 September 2015

**Fields:** Particle physics, Quantum chromodynamics

![Portrait of Guido Altarelli](../portraits/00f8974566948d260d49.jpg)

## Biography

An Italian theorist who worked in Rome, the United States, and CERN, Altarelli developed testable predictions of quantum chromodynamics. His work helped make detailed comparisons between collider theory and data possible.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed parton-distribution evolution equations with Parisi.
- Advanced perturbative quantum chromodynamics and phenomenology.
- Investigated precision electroweak tests and theories beyond the standard model.
