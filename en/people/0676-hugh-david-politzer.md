## Biography

H. David Politzer was born in New York, NY, USA and conducted research in particle physics and quantum chromodynamics. Nobel records list California Institute of Technology (Caltech) as the affiliation at the time of the award. They discovered asymptotic freedom in non-Abelian gauge theory. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2004.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered asymptotic freedom in non-Abelian gauge theory
- Explained why quark interactions become weaker at short distances and high energies

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2004
