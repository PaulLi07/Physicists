## Biography

Nicole Oresme was a French medieval scholar and Parisian university teacher who later became bishop of Lisieux. He worked on mathematics, economics and natural philosophy and translated Aristotle. His geometrical representations of changing intensities and motion were important intellectual resources before early modern kinematics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Used diagrams to represent quantities varying with position or time and investigated uniformly changing motion and the mean-speed relation.
- Considered the possibility of Earth's rotation and investigated light and vision.
