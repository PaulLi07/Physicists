## Biography

Geoffrey Hinton was born in London, United Kingdom and conducted research in statistical learning and neural networks. Nobel records list University of Toronto as the affiliation at the time of the award. They helped develop Boltzmann machines and other learning models drawing on statistical physics. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2024.

## Major contributions to physics

- Helped develop Boltzmann machines and other learning models drawing on statistical physics
- Advanced representation-learning methods for multilayer neural networks

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2024
