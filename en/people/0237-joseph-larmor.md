# Joseph Larmor

**Born:** 11 July 1857

**Died / status:** 19 May 1942

**Fields:** Classical electrodynamics, Magnetism, Prehistory of relativity

![Portrait of Joseph Larmor](../portraits/5d9338c5dd18c35f4422-15c84492ce.jpeg)

## Biography

Larmor was an Irish-born theoretical physicist at Cambridge, where he held the Lucasian chair. His work on electrons, electromagnetic radiation and motion in magnetic fields contributed important results to classical electrodynamics and the historical development of relativity.

## Major contributions to physics

- Derived the radiation-power formula for an accelerating charge
- Developed the account of Larmor precession in a magnetic field
- Investigated time transformations and moving systems within electron theory
