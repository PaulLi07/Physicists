# Johannes Kepler

**Born:** 27 December 1571

**Died / status:** 15 November 1630

**Fields:** Celestial mechanics, Geometrical optics

## Biography

Kepler was a German astronomer who assisted Tycho Brahe and inherited his observational data. Working amid religious and political turmoil, he insisted on testing planetary models against precise observations. He replaced combinations of circles with elliptical orbits and also made major contributions to vision and telescope optics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Formulated the three laws of planetary motion, relating orbital shape, areal velocity and orbital periods.
- Explained retinal imaging and investigated lenses and telescope design, advancing geometrical optics.
