# Charles-Augustin de Coulomb

**Born:** 14 June 1736

**Died / status:** 23 August 1806

**Fields:** Electrostatics, Experimental mechanics, Tribology

## Biography

Coulomb was a French military engineer and experimental physicist who worked on fortifications overseas and in France. He moved from civil and mechanical questions to precision measurements of electrical and magnetic forces. His torsion instruments helped quantify weak forces; the unit of electric charge bears his name.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established the inverse-square law of electrostatic force through torsion-balance experiments.
- Investigated friction, torsion and the stability of soil, providing quantitative results for applied mechanics and instrumentation.
