# Hans Geiger

**Born:** 30 September 1882

**Died / status:** 24 September 1945

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Experimental physics

![Portrait of Hans Geiger](../portraits/3bfce62b65b467be1a66-260fef4e1d.jpg)

## Biography

A German experimental physicist, Geiger worked with Rutherford in Manchester before pursuing radioactivity and particle detection in Germany. He developed the counting of individual ionizing particles into a precise experimental tool and helped perform experiments central to the nuclear model of the atom.

## Major contributions to physics

- Performed large-angle alpha-particle scattering experiments with Marsden, providing evidence for Rutherford's nuclear atom.
- Developed early particle counters and, with Walther Müller, the Geiger–Müller tube.
- Established the Geiger–Nuttall relation with John Mitchell Nuttall, connecting alpha-particle range with radioactive half-life.
