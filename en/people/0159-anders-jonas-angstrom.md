# Anders Jonas Ångström

**Born:** 13 August 1814

**Died / status:** 21 June 1874

**Fields:** Spectroscopy, Astrophysics, Metrology

## Biography

Ångström was a Swedish physicist and astronomer at Uppsala. He brought precise wavelength measurements to solar and laboratory spectroscopy and produced detailed spectral atlases, helping make spectral analysis a reliable means of investigating atomic radiation and stellar composition.

## Major contributions to physics

- Precisely measured and mapped lines in the solar spectrum
- Investigated emission and absorption spectra and identified hydrogen in the solar atmosphere
- Helped standardize spectroscopic wavelengths, with the ångström unit later named in his honour
