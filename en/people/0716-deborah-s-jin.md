# Deborah S. Jin

**Born:** 15 November 1968

**Died / status:** 15 September 2016

**Fields:** Ultracold atoms, Quantum many-body physics

![Portrait of Deborah S. Jin](../portraits/444577f1c802a3e53b8f.jpg)

## Biography

An American experimental physicist educated at Chicago, Jin worked at JILA and NIST. By cooling fermionic atoms into quantum degeneracy she opened experimental approaches to strongly interacting many-body systems.

## Major contributions to physics

- Produced an early quantum-degenerate Fermi gas.
- Created a condensate of paired fermionic atoms with her team.
- Advanced experiments on the BEC–BCS crossover and strongly correlated Fermi gases.
