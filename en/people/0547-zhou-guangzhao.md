# Zhou Guangzhao

**Born:** 15 May 1929

**Died / status:** 17 August 2024

**Fields:** Particle physics, Nuclear physics

## Biography

Born in Changsha, Zhou studied and worked at Tsinghua, Peking University, and JINR in Dubna. He contributed to Chinese nuclear-weapons theory and later served as president of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, promoting basic research and international exchange.

## Major contributions to physics

- Contributed to the partially conserved axial-current idea.
- Investigated relativistic scattering amplitudes and helicity methods.
- Organized and contributed to nuclear-weapons theory and radiation hydrodynamics in China.
