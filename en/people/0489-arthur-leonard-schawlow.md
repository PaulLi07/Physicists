# Arthur Leonard Schawlow

**Born:** 5 May 1921

**Died / status:** 28 April 1999

**Fields:** Laser physics, Precision spectroscopy

## Biography

Arthur Leonard Schawlow was born in Mount Vernon, NY, USA and conducted research in laser physics and precision spectroscopy. Nobel records list Stanford University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed laser spectroscopy and precise measurements of narrow spectral lines. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1981.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed laser spectroscopy and precise measurements of narrow spectral lines
- Proposed an important theoretical scheme for an optical maser with Charles Townes

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1981
