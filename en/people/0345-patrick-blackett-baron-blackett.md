# Patrick Maynard Stuart Blackett

**Born:** 18 November 1897

**Died / status:** 13 July 1974

**Fields:** Particle physics, Cosmic rays

## Biography

Patrick Maynard Stuart Blackett was born in London, United Kingdom and conducted research in particle physics and cosmic rays. Nobel records list Victoria University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They improved cloud chambers and automatic triggering techniques. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1948.

## Major contributions to physics

- Improved cloud chambers and automatic triggering techniques
- Used cosmic-ray experiments to investigate positrons and particle showers

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1948
