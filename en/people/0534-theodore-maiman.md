# Theodore Maiman

**Born:** 11 July 1927

**Died / status:** 5 May 2007

**Fields:** Laser physics, Quantum electronics

![Portrait of Theodore Maiman](../portraits/4a87405ca29ef4222a28-cecd351c69.jpg)

Sourced portrait or photograph; see the image record for provenance and reuse conditions.

## Biography

An American experimental physicist trained by Lamb at Stanford, Maiman worked at Hughes Research Laboratories. He constructed the first operating laser using ruby and advanced practical laser technology.

## Major contributions to physics

- Demonstrated the first operating ruby laser in 1960.
- Investigated solid-state laser media and optical pumping.
- Advanced laser engineering and applications.
