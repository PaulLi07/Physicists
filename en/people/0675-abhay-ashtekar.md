# Abhay Ashtekar

**Born:** 5 July 1949

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Quantum gravity, General relativity

## Biography

An Indian-born theorist based at Pennsylvania State University, Ashtekar reformulated general relativity in variables resembling those of gauge theory. His work helped establish loop quantum gravity and quantum cosmology.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced Ashtekar variables for general relativity.
- Developed mathematical foundations of loop quantum gravity.
- Investigated isolated horizons, quantum black holes, and loop quantum cosmology.
