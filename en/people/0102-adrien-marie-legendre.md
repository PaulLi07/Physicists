# Adrien-Marie Legendre

**Born:** 18 September 1752

**Died / status:** 9 January 1833

**Fields:** Potential theory, Mathematical physics, Measurement errors

![Image associated with Adrien-Marie Legendre](../portraits/a73c4682efd4bc024376-11a811d824.jpg)

A contemporary caricature of the mathematician by Julien-Léopold Boilly, circa 1820, reidentified in 2009. The frequently reproduced formal painted portrait depicts the politician Louis Legendre and must not be assigned to this mathematician.

## Biography

Legendre was a French mathematician who taught at the Paris military school and worked with the academy and geodetic surveys. He investigated planetary attraction, elliptic integrals and observational errors and developed functions and transformations widely used in field theory and mechanics. His portrait was long misidentified.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed Legendre polynomials and related potential-theory methods for gravitation and boundary-value problems.
- Investigated elliptic integrals, least squares and the transformation bearing his name, providing tools for mechanics and mathematical physics.

## Historical and source notes

- Death follows Inventaire Condorcet, citing a Paris death record: 9 January 1833; MacTutor gives 10 January, also the date of the funeral.
- The familiar formal portrait was commonly of the politician Louis Legendre; the authenticated contemporary depiction of this mathematician is Boilly’s caricature of 1820.
