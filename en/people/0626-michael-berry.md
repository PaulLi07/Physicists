# Michael Berry

**Born:** 14 March 1941

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Mathematical physics, Wave theory

## Biography

A British theorist at Bristol, Berry uses geometry and asymptotic analysis to understand quantum and classical waves. His work revealed geometric structure in quantum evolution and illuminated many everyday wave phenomena.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the geometric phase of adiabatic quantum evolution.
- Investigated wave singularities, optical catastrophes, and semiclassical theory.
- Advanced quantum-chaos and wave-asymptotic methods.
