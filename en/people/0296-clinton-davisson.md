# Clinton Joseph Davisson

**Born:** 22 October 1881

**Died / status:** 1 February 1958

**Fields:** Quantum mechanics, Electron diffraction

## Biography

Clinton Joseph Davisson was born in Bloomington, IL, USA and conducted research in quantum mechanics and electron diffraction. Nobel records list Bell Telephone Laboratories as the affiliation at the time of the award. They observed the diffraction of electrons by crystals. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1937.

## Major contributions to physics

- Observed the diffraction of electrons by crystals
- Confirmed the wave properties of electrons predicted by matter-wave theory

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1937
