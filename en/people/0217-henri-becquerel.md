# Antoine Henri Becquerel

**Born:** 15 December 1852

**Died / status:** 25 August 1908

**Fields:** Radioactivity, Nuclear physics

## Biography

Antoine Henri Becquerel was born in Paris, France and conducted research in radioactivity and nuclear physics. Nobel records list École Polytechnique as the affiliation at the time of the award. They discovered that uranium salts emit penetrating radiation without external illumination. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1903.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered that uranium salts emit penetrating radiation without external illumination
- Revealed the spontaneous radioactivity of matter

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1903
