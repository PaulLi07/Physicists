# Friedrich Paschen

**Born:** 22 January 1865

**Died / status:** 25 February 1947

**Fields:** Atomic spectroscopy, Gas discharges, Precision measurement

## Biography

Paschen was a German experimental physicist specializing in precision spectroscopy and gas discharges. He taught in Tübingen and headed the Physikalisch-Technische Reichsanstalt. His spectral and external-field experiments provided important evidence for atomic physics and early quantum theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established the pressure–distance dependence of gas breakdown associated with Paschen's law
- Discovered the infrared Paschen series of hydrogen
- Investigated strong-magnetic-field spectral behaviour with Back, now known as the Paschen–Back effect
