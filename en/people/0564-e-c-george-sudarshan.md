# E. C. George Sudarshan

**Born:** 16 September 1931

**Died / status:** 13 May 2018

**Fields:** Particle physics, Quantum optics

## Biography

An Indian-born theorist who worked at Rochester, Harvard, and Texas, Sudarshan contributed to weak interactions, quantum optics, and open quantum systems. He also maintained an important role in Indian scientific institutions.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed the V−A theory of weak interactions with Marshak.
- Established the diagonal coherent-state representation in quantum optics.
- Contributed to dynamical-map descriptions of open quantum systems.
