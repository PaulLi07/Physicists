# Hans G. Dehmelt

**Born:** 9 September 1922

**Died / status:** 7 March 2017

**Fields:** Atomic physics, Particle trapping

## Biography

Hans G. Dehmelt was born in Görlitz, Prussia (now Germany) and conducted research in atomic physics and particle trapping. Nobel records list University of Washington as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed techniques for trapping charged particles in electromagnetic fields. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1989.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed techniques for trapping charged particles in electromagnetic fields
- Advanced precision measurements of systems including individual electrons

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1989
