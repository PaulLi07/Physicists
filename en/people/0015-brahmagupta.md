## Biography

Brahmagupta was an Indian mathematician and astronomer associated with the Ujjain tradition who composed the Brahmasphutasiddhanta in 628. Alongside numerical methods, he treated celestial calculation, eclipses and instruments, applying mathematics to practical astronomical questions. His death year is only approximate.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed calculations for celestial longitudes, eclipses and conjunctions.
- Investigated spherical astronomy and observational instruments, extending Indian quantitative astronomy.

## Historical and source notes

- Birth is usually dated 598; death is variously estimated around 668 or 670.
