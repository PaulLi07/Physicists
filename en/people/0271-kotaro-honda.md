# Kotaro Honda

**Born:** 23 February 1870

**Died / status:** 12 February 1954

**Fields:** Magnetism, Condensed-matter physics, Materials physics

## Biography

Honda was a Japanese physicist and metallurgical researcher at Tohoku Imperial University. He connected precise measurements of magnetic and thermal properties with the design of steels and alloys, while building research institutions devoted to metallic materials.

## Major contributions to physics

- Systematically investigated magnetic and thermal properties of metals and alloys
- Helped develop high-performance KS permanent-magnet steel
- Established a measurement-based approach to magnetic-material design
