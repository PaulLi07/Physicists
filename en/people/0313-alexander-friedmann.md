## Biography

Friedmann was a Russian mathematician and physicist who worked on meteorology and fluid mechanics before pursuing cosmology in Petrograd. His evolving homogeneous solutions of Einstein's equations established a central framework of dynamical cosmology before his early death.

## Major contributions to physics

- Derived the Friedmann equations governing homogeneous, isotropic cosmic evolution.
- Showed that general relativity permits expanding or contracting universes.
- Investigated turbulence and atmospheric dynamics.
