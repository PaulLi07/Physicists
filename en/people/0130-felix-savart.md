# Félix Savart

**Born:** 30 June 1791

**Died / status:** 16 March 1841

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Acoustics

![Image associated with Félix Savart](../portraits/9e1db00813c3f1b43145-fad82676e2.jpg)

A modern photograph of the named bust of Félix Savart at the Institut de France in Paris. This is a sculptural portrait, not a historical photograph of Savart; whether it was modelled during his lifetime has not been verified.

## Biography

Savart was a French physician and physicist who initially served as an army surgeon and later investigated acoustics, electromagnetism and instruments in Paris. With Biot he measured the magnetic action of current. His studies of vibrations, sound and materials connected instrument making to quantitative physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established the quantitative magnetic-field relation associated with Biot and Savart.
- Investigated vibrations of strings, plates and liquids and acoustic interference, advancing experimental acoustics.
