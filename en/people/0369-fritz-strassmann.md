# Fritz Strassmann

**Born:** 22 February 1902

**Died / status:** 22 April 1980

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Nuclear chemistry

![Portrait of Fritz Strassmann](../portraits/949af6868ba6a3263830-a1604a9396.jpg)

## Biography

A German nuclear chemist, Strassmann worked with Otto Hahn and Lise Meitner at the Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Chemistry in Berlin. In late 1938 he and Hahn obtained chemical evidence of barium in neutron-irradiated uranium. After the war he developed chemistry and nuclear chemistry at Mainz.

## Major contributions to physics

- Identified barium in neutron-irradiated uranium with Hahn through radiochemical analysis, supplying crucial experimental evidence for nuclear fission.
- Developed chemical methods for separating and identifying radionuclides and investigating fission products.
- Helped establish chemistry and nuclear-chemistry research institutions at Mainz.

## Historical and source notes

- Hahn and Strassmann supplied the chemical experimental evidence; Meitner and Otto Robert Frisch subsequently provided the physical interpretation as nuclear fission.
