# Francesco Maria Grimaldi

**Born:** 2 April 1618

**Died / status:** 28 December 1663

**Fields:** Wave optics, Experimental mechanics

## Biography

Grimaldi was an Italian Jesuit scholar in Bologna who investigated astronomy, mechanics and optics and collaborated with Riccioli on lunar observations and falling bodies. His posthumously published optical work described complex light distributions near obstacles, opening the study of diffraction.

## Major contributions to physics

- Observed and named diffraction, studying bright and dark structures near apertures and obstacles.
- Collaborated with Riccioli on timing falling bodies and observing the Moon, advancing precision astronomical and mechanical experiments.
