# Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar

**Born:** 19 October 1910

**Died / status:** 21 August 1995

**Fields:** Astrophysics, Stellar structure

## Biography

Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar was born in Lahore, India (now Pakistan) and conducted research in astrophysics and stellar structure. Nobel records list University of Chicago as the affiliation at the time of the award. They established the theory of the limiting mass of white dwarfs. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1983.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established the theory of the limiting mass of white dwarfs
- Investigated stellar structure, stellar dynamics and compact-object evolution

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1983
