## Biography

Roger Penrose was born in Colchester, United Kingdom and conducted research in general relativity, black holes and mathematical physics. Nobel records list University of Oxford as the affiliation at the time of the award. They proved that gravitational collapse produces a spacetime singularity under specified physical conditions. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2020.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proved that gravitational collapse produces a spacetime singularity under specified physical conditions
- Applied rigorous geometrical methods to the theory of black-hole formation

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2020
