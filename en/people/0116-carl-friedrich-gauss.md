# Carl Friedrich Gauss

**Born:** 30 April 1777

**Died / status:** 23 February 1855

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Potential theory, Physical measurement

## Biography

Gauss was a German mathematician, astronomer and physical investigator who directed the Göttingen Observatory for many years. He applied rigorous mathematics to orbit calculation, geodesy and geomagnetism and collaborated with Weber on magnetic measurements, closely joining mathematical methods and precision experiments.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed potential theory and the divergence relation associated with Gauss, providing tools for gravitational and electrostatic fields.
- Established absolute measurements of magnetic intensity and promoted international geomagnetic observations.
- Advanced orbit determination and error analysis, improving astronomical and geodetic precision.
