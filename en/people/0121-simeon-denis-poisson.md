# Siméon Denis Poisson

**Born:** 21 June 1781

**Died / status:** 25 April 1840

**Fields:** Mathematical physics, Potential theory, Elasticity

## Biography

Poisson was a French mathematician and physicist educated at the École Polytechnique, where he later taught, and a professor of mechanics in Paris. He used analytical methods for gravitation, electricity, elasticity, heat and sound, extending partial differential equations as tools for continuous fields and material properties.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established Poisson's equation with a source term, used in electrostatics, gravitation and potential theory.
- Advanced elasticity theory, including the relation between transverse and longitudinal deformation.
- Extended analytical methods in heat conduction, acoustics and mechanics.
