# Makoto Kobayashi

**Born:** 7 April 1944

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Particle physics, Quark mixing

## Biography

Makoto Kobayashi was born in Nagoya, Japan and conducted research in particle physics and quark mixing. Nobel records list High Energy Accelerator Research Organization (KEK) as the affiliation at the time of the award. They proposed a six-quark mixing framework with Toshihide Maskawa. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2008.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed a six-quark mixing framework with Toshihide Maskawa
- Explained how weak interactions could violate CP symmetry

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2008
