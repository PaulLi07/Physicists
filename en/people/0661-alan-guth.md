## Biography

An American theorist educated at MIT, Guth worked at Cornell and SLAC before returning to MIT. He applied vacuum phase-transition ideas from particle physics to the evolution of the early universe.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed inflation to address the horizon and flatness problems through rapid accelerated expansion.
- Advanced the idea that quantum fluctuations seed cosmic large-scale structure.
