# Democritus

**Born:** c. 460 BCE

**Died / status:** c. 370 BCE

**Fields:** History of atomism, Natural philosophy

![Democritus — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/ed9bf929f6fd628f7a0f.jpg)

A later painting, sculpture or traditional representation; it is not a verified contemporary likeness.

## Biography

A thinker from Abdera in ancient Greece, Democritus developed atomism with Leucippus. Most of his writings are lost, and his views must be reconstructed from later quotations. His account of atoms and void was a natural-philosophical hypothesis, rather than the experimentally established atomic physics of modern science.

## Major contributions to physics

- Explained matter and its changes in terms of atoms and void.
- Related observable properties to the arrangement and structure of microscopic constituents, supplying a historical resource for later corpuscular theories.

## Historical and source notes

- The dates are conventional estimates; ancient atomism differs from modern atomic physics.
