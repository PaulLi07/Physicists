# Aleksandr Mikhailovich Prokhorov

**Born:** 11 July 1916

**Died / status:** 8 January 2002

**Fields:** Quantum electronics, Lasers

## Biography

Aleksandr Mikhailovich Prokhorov was born in Atherton, Australia and conducted research in quantum electronics and lasers. Nobel records list P.N. Lebedev Physical Institute as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed microwave amplification and oscillation based on stimulated emission. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1964.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed microwave amplification and oscillation based on stimulated emission
- Established important theoretical and experimental foundations for masers and lasers

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1964
