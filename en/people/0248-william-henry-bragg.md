# Sir William Henry Bragg

**Born:** 2 July 1862

**Died / status:** 12 March 1942

**Fields:** Crystallography, X-rays

## Biography

Sir William Henry Bragg was born in Wigton, United Kingdom and conducted research in crystallography and X-rays. Nobel records list University College as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed an X-ray spectrometer and investigated crystal structures with Lawrence Bragg. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1915.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed an X-ray spectrometer and investigated crystal structures with Lawrence Bragg
- Established experimental methods for determining crystal structures using X-rays

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1915
