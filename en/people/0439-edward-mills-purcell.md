# Edward Mills Purcell

**Born:** 30 August 1912

**Died / status:** 7 March 1997

**Fields:** Nuclear magnetic resonance, Atomic physics

## Biography

Edward Mills Purcell was born in Taylorville, IL, USA and conducted research in nuclear magnetic resonance and atomic physics. Nobel records list Harvard University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They independently developed methods for detecting nuclear magnetic resonance in bulk matter. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1952.

## Major contributions to physics

- Independently developed methods for detecting nuclear magnetic resonance in bulk matter
- Provided experimental foundations for condensed-matter research and later magnetic-resonance technologies

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1952
