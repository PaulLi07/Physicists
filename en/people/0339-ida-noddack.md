# Ida Noddack

**Born:** 25 February 1896

**Died / status:** 24 September 1978

**Fields:** Nuclear science, Analytical chemistry

## Biography

Noddack was a German chemist who worked at the boundary of chemistry and nuclear physics. With Walter Noddack and Otto Berg she discovered rhenium. Her suggestion that neutron-bombarded heavy nuclei might break into substantial fragments anticipated an essential idea of nuclear fission.

## Major contributions to physics

- Co-discovered rhenium, element 75.
- Suggested in 1934 that heavy nuclei under neutron bombardment might split into large fragments.
- Developed trace-element analysis.
