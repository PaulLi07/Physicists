# Hendrik Antoon Lorentz

**Born:** 18 July 1853

**Died / status:** 4 February 1928

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Relativity

## Biography

Hendrik Antoon Lorentz was born in Arnhem, the Netherlands and conducted research in electromagnetism and relativity. Nobel records list Leiden University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed an electron theory of matter that explained magnetic influences on radiation. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1902.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed an electron theory of matter that explained magnetic influences on radiation
- Developed the Lorentz transformations, an important mathematical precursor to special relativity

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1902
