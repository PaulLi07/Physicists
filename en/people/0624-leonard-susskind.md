**Born:** 16 June 1940

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

## Biography

An American theorist educated at Cornell and based at Stanford, Susskind helped develop string descriptions of strong interactions. His later work connected black-hole quantum information, holography, and cosmology.

## Major contributions to physics

- Helped formulate string models of particles.
- Developed black-hole complementarity and the holographic principle.
- Investigated links among black-hole information, quantum complexity, and quantum gravity.
