# Georges Lemaître

**Born:** 17 July 1894

## Biography

A Belgian physicist and Catholic priest, Lemaître studied in Cambridge and at MIT before teaching at Louvain. He connected relativistic cosmological solutions with galaxy observations and proposed evolution from a dense early state.

## Major contributions to physics

- Independently proposed an expanding universe and derived a distance–recession-velocity relation.
- Proposed the primeval-atom hypothesis, an important precursor of Big Bang cosmology.
- Developed relativistic cosmological models.
