# Anthony J. Leggett

**Born:** 26 March 1938

**Died / status:** 8 March 2026

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Superfluidity

## Biography

Anthony J. Leggett was born in London, United Kingdom and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and superfluidity. Nobel records list University of Illinois as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed a theoretical explanation of superfluid helium-3. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2003.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed a theoretical explanation of superfluid helium-3
- Investigated unconventional pairing and macroscopic quantum phenomena

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2003
