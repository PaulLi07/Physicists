# Samuel Goudsmit

**Born:** 11 July 1902

**Died / status:** 4 December 1978

**Fields:** Atomic physics, Quantum mechanics

## Biography

A Dutch-born physicist, Goudsmit studied atomic spectra at Leiden before moving to the United States and working at Michigan and Brookhaven. He also served for many years as a physics-journal editor, supporting postwar scientific communication.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed electron spin with Uhlenbeck.
- Investigated atomic spectral structure and quantum numbers.
- Advanced atomic and nuclear research and scientific publishing.
