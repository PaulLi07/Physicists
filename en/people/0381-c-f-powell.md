# Cecil Frank Powell

**Born:** 5 December 1903

**Died / status:** 9 August 1969

**Fields:** Particle physics, Detection methods

## Biography

Cecil Frank Powell was born in Tonbridge, United Kingdom and conducted research in particle physics and detection methods. Nobel records list Bristol University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed photographic-emulsion methods for recording high-energy particle tracks. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1950.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed photographic-emulsion methods for recording high-energy particle tracks
- Contributed to the discovery and study of the pion

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1950
