# Giuseppe Occhialini

**Born:** 5 December 1907

**Died / status:** 30 December 1993

**Fields:** Cosmic rays, Particle physics

![Portrait of Giuseppe Occhialini](../portraits/88ff46411fbe975063e4-1a034b9e31.jpg)

## Biography

An Italian experimentalist, Occhialini worked with Blackett and Powell in Britain before building cosmic-ray and space research in Milan. He combined track detectors with electronics and participated in new-particle discoveries.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed coincidence-triggered cloud chambers with Blackett.
- Participated in the nuclear-emulsion discovery of the pion.
- Advanced Italian space physics and X-ray astronomy.
