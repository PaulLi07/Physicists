# Carl David Anderson

**Born:** 3 September 1905

**Died / status:** 11 January 1991

**Fields:** Particle physics, Cosmic rays

## Biography

Carl David Anderson was born in New York, NY, USA and conducted research in particle physics and cosmic rays. Nobel records list California Institute of Technology (Caltech) as the affiliation at the time of the award. They discovered the positron in cosmic-ray cloud-chamber tracks. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1936.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the positron in cosmic-ray cloud-chamber tracks
- Co-discovered the particle later identified as the muon

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1936
