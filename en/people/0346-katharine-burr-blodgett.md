# Katharine Burr Blodgett

**Born:** 10 January 1898

**Died / status:** 12 October 1979

**Fields:** Surface physics, Optical thin films

## Biography

An American experimental physicist with a Cambridge doctorate, Blodgett worked with Langmuir at General Electric. She transformed molecular-monolayer research into controlled thin-film techniques and applied them to optical surfaces.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed Langmuir–Blodgett film-transfer techniques with Langmuir.
- Used thin-film interference to produce low-reflection glass.
- Investigated film-thickness measurement and interfacial properties.
