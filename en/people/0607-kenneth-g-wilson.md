## Biography

Kenneth G. Wilson was born in Waltham, MA, USA and conducted research in statistical physics and renormalization group. Nobel records list Cornell University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed renormalization-group methods for critical phenomena at continuous phase transitions. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1982.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed renormalization-group methods for critical phenomena at continuous phase transitions
- Explained the universality of critical behaviour across different physical systems

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1982
