## Biography

A French-born American theorist, Hohenberg worked at Bell Laboratories and later at New York University. His applications of statistical physics to quantum many-body and nonequilibrium systems included foundational work on density-functional theory and critical dynamics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proved the Hohenberg–Kohn theorems with Kohn.
- Established restrictions on low-dimensional long-range order from thermal fluctuations.
- Classified critical dynamics systematically with Halperin.
