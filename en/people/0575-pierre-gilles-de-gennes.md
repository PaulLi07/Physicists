# Pierre-Gilles de Gennes

**Born:** 24 October 1932

**Died / status:** 18 May 2007

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Soft matter

## Biography

Pierre-Gilles de Gennes was born in Paris, France and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and soft matter. Nobel records list Collège de France as the affiliation at the time of the award. They extended theoretical methods for ordering phenomena to liquid crystals and polymers. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1991.

## Major contributions to physics

- Extended theoretical methods for ordering phenomena to liquid crystals and polymers
- Connected the behaviour of different soft-matter systems

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1991
