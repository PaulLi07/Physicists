## Biography

A Greek-born theorist, Iliopoulos worked at CERN and Harvard before joining the École normale supérieure in Paris. His studies of weak interactions and gauge theory contributed to the establishment of the standard model.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed the GIM mechanism and the role of charm with Glashow and Maiani.
- Investigated gauge theory, renormalization, and supersymmetry.
- Advanced the theoretical development of the standard model.
