# Al-Biruni

**Born:** c. 973 CE

**Died / status:** c. 1048 or after 1050

**Fields:** Physical measurement, Hydrostatics, Astronomy

![Al-Biruni — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/e3a3b25a5bc888dc35c8.jpg)

A later painting, sculpture or traditional representation; it is not a verified contemporary likeness.

## Biography

Al-Biruni was a polymath from Khwarazm whose studies encompassed astronomy, geography, mathematics and natural phenomena. Later active in Ghazna, he also wrote extensively on Indian learning and culture. He combined observation, instruments and mathematics to investigate the Earth and the properties of materials through measurement.

## Major contributions to physics

- Estimated Earth's radius through geometry and angular measurements.
- Measured the specific gravities of minerals and liquids and developed methods of hydrostatic weighing and precision measurement.

## Historical and source notes

- Birth is commonly dated 4 September 973, whereas MacTutor gives 15 September; specialist research notes limited evidence. Death is variously placed in 1048, around 1050 or after 1050, so no exact day is treated as undisputed.
