## Biography

Johann Bernoulli was a Swiss mathematician, younger brother of Jacob, who taught in Groningen before taking the Basel chair. He developed and disseminated Leibnizian calculus and applied variational reasoning to optics and mechanics. He also participated in debates on vis viva and fluid motion.

## Major contributions to physics

- Advanced variational methods through the brachistochrone problem.
- Investigated reflection, refraction, vibrating strings and fluid motion, extending applications of calculus in physics.
