# Roy Kerr

**Born:** 16 May 1934

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** General relativity, Black-hole physics

![Portrait of Roy Kerr](../portraits/bfaf87eb715175e80158-f48de6055b.jpg)

## Biography

A New Zealand mathematician and relativist, Kerr worked in Cambridge and Texas before teaching at Canterbury. His exact rotating-black-hole solution became central to modern black-hole astrophysics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the Kerr metric for rotating vacuum black holes.
- Investigated exact solutions of Einstein's equations.
- Established foundations for rotating-black-hole astrophysical models.
