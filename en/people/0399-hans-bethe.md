**Born:** 2 July 1906

## Biography

Hans Albrecht Bethe was born in Strasbourg, Germany (now France) and conducted research in nuclear astrophysics and nuclear physics. Nobel records list Cornell University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They explained nuclear reactions responsible for stellar energy production. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1967.

## Major contributions to physics

- Explained nuclear reactions responsible for stellar energy production
- Investigated the proton-proton chain and carbon-nitrogen-oxygen cycle

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1967
