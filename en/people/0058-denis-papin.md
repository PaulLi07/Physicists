# Denis Papin

**Born:** 1647 (baptized 22 August; exact birth date unknown)

**Died / status:** 1713 (buried 26 August; exact death date unknown)

**Fields:** History of thermal physics, Pressure technology, Mechanical engineering

![Denis Papin — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/1e98ce771c59218477df.jpg)

The creator, date and form of this likeness are documented at the image source.

## Biography

Papin was a French physician-turned-inventor who collaborated with Huygens and Boyle and later worked in Germany. He investigated steam, pressure and mechanical power and designed a pressure digester with a safety valve and piston devices, linking vacuum experiments to early steam technology.

## Major contributions to physics

- Built a high-pressure digester with a safety valve, demonstrating the effects of confined steam.
- Proposed using steam condensation to create a pressure difference that drives a piston, contributing to early steam-power technology.

## Historical and source notes
