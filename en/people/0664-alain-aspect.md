# Alain Aspect

**Born:** 15 June 1947

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Quantum foundations, Quantum information

## Biography

Alain Aspect was born in Agen, France and conducted research in quantum foundations and quantum information. Nobel records list Institut d’Optique Graduate School – Université Paris-Saclay and École Polytechnique as the affiliations at the time of the award. They performed entangled-photon experiments and tested Bell inequalities. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2022.

## Major contributions to physics

- Performed entangled-photon experiments and tested Bell inequalities
- Established important experimental foundations for quantum information science

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2022
