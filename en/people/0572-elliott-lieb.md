**Born:** 31 July 1932

## Biography

An American mathematical physicist at Princeton, Lieb uses rigorous inequalities and exact solutions to study matter stability, quantum many-body systems, and statistical mechanics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated stability of matter and many-body energy bounds.
- Established bounds on quantum-lattice propagation speeds with Robinson.
- Solved important statistical models and developed mathematical inequalities.
