# Efim Fradkin

**Born:** 30 November 1924

**Died / status:** 25 May 1999

**Fields:** Quantum field theory, Statistical physics

![Portrait of Efim Fradkin](../portraits/d2e95509f5b00d5c100e-578a126bf5.jpg)

Portrait extracted unchanged from page 1 of the 1999 Physics–Uspekhi memorial; its published caption identifies Fradkin and his life dates.

## Biography

A Soviet and Russian theorist at the Lebedev Institute, Fradkin applied functional methods to interacting fields, quantum statistics, and gauge theory, developing quantization methods for constrained systems.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed functional methods in field theory and quantum statistics.
- Developed, with collaborators, the Hamiltonian BFV formulation of BRST quantization for constrained systems.
- Investigated higher-spin and conformal field theories.
