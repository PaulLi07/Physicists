# Henrietta Swan Leavitt

**Born:** 4 July 1868

**Died / status:** 12 December 1921

**Fields:** Stellar physics, Cosmic distance scale, Observational astronomy

![Portrait of Henrietta Swan Leavitt](../portraits/a729bc657b3281946455.jpg)

## Biography

Leavitt was an American astronomer at Harvard who compared photographic plates and measured variable-star brightnesses. Her relation between Cepheid periods and luminosities in the Magellanic Clouds supplied a crucial tool for measuring distances beyond the Milky Way and developing observational cosmology.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the period–luminosity relation for Cepheid variables
- Used approximately equidistant stars in the Magellanic Clouds to establish the relation
- Provided a foundation for calibrating Galactic and extragalactic distance measurements
