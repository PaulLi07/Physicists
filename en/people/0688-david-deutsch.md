# David Deutsch

**Born:** 18 May 1953

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Quantum information, Quantum foundations

![Portrait of David Deutsch](../portraits/efe753da9cd2aa6627a8-fded4d25dd.jpg)

## Biography

An Israeli-born British theorist associated with Oxford, Deutsch connected quantum physics with computational universality. His research includes quantum computers, algorithms, and interpretations of quantum theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed a universal quantum-computer model.
- Developed quantum-advantage algorithms with Jozsa.
- Investigated many-worlds quantum theory and constructor theory.
