# Williamina Fleming

**Born:** 15 May 1857

**Died / status:** 21 May 1911

**Fields:** Stellar physics, Spectroscopy, Observational astronomy

## Biography

Fleming was a Scottish-born American astronomer at Harvard College Observatory. She classified photographic spectra and later supervised the observatory's women computers. Her systematic examination of plates supported stellar classification and the discovery of variable stars, novae and unusual spectra.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed and applied an early Harvard stellar spectral classification and compiled extensive stellar catalogues
- Discovered many variable stars, novae and other unusual objects on plates
- Helped recognize anomalous white-dwarf spectra
