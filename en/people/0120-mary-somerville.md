# Mary Somerville

**Born:** 26 December 1780

**Died / status:** 29 November 1872

**Fields:** Mathematical physics, Celestial mechanics, Science communication

## Biography

Somerville was a Scottish mathematical and scientific writer who entered European scholarly networks largely through self-education and later lived in Italy. Her Mechanism of the Heavens expounded Laplace's mathematical astronomy. On the Connexion of the Physical Sciences brought together knowledge of light, heat, electricity and astronomy.

## Major contributions to physics

- Explained Laplacian celestial mechanics mathematically and advanced the dissemination of mathematical physics.
- Examined connections among physical sciences and undertook experiments concerning magnetism and sunlight.

## Historical and source notes

- Some results of her experiments on magnetization by sunlight were not reliably reproduced later; they are part of her historical research, not established modern physical results.
