# Luigi Galvani

**Born:** 9 September 1737

**Died / status:** 4 December 1798

**Fields:** Bioelectricity, History of electricity

## Biography

Galvani was an Italian physician, anatomist and teacher at Bologna who investigated nerves, muscles and electrical stimulation. Frog-leg experiments led him to the idea of animal electricity. His debate with Volta encouraged distinctions between electrical activity in living tissue and sources associated with dissimilar metals.

## Major contributions to physics

- Demonstrated connections between electrical stimulation and muscular contraction, advancing experimental electrophysiology.
- Stimulated further experiments on electricity, nerve function and the mechanisms of electrical sources through his animal-electricity research.
