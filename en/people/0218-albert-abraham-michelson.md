# Albert Abraham Michelson

**Born:** 19 December 1852

**Died / status:** 9 May 1931

**Fields:** Optics, Precision measurement

## Biography

Albert Abraham Michelson was born in Strelno, Prussia (now Strzelno, Poland) and conducted research in optics and precision measurement. Nobel records list University of Chicago as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed high-precision interferometric instruments and methods. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1907.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed high-precision interferometric instruments and methods
- Applied optical precision measurements to the speed of light and spectroscopy

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1907
