# Hans Christian Ørsted

**Born:** 14 August 1777

**Died / status:** 9 March 1851

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Experimental physics

![Hans Christian Ørsted — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/367cba5cf5fa818a5f9c.jpg)

The creator, date and form of this likeness are documented at the image source.

## Biography

Ørsted was a Danish physicist and chemist who taught in Copenhagen and promoted scientific education and technical institutions. Interested in connections among natural effects, he discovered that a current deflects a magnetic needle. This observation directly stimulated the emergence of electromagnetism.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the magnetic effect of an electric current, clearly demonstrating a connection between electricity and magnetism.
- Investigated materials and electrical phenomena and promoted experimental teaching and the dissemination of electromagnetic research.
