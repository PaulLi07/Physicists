# Robert Sanderson Mulliken

**Born:** 7 June 1896

**Died / status:** 31 October 1986

**Fields:** Chemical physics, Molecular orbitals, Spectroscopy

## Biography

Mulliken was an American chemical physicist based for much of his career at the University of Chicago. He applied quantum mechanics to molecular electrons, developed the molecular-orbital method and built an influential research school. His work on bonding and molecular electronic structure earned the 1966 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed molecular-orbital methods for understanding chemical bonding through quantum electronic states
- Investigated molecular electronic structure and spectra and developed associated nomenclature and classifications
- Introduced interpretive tools for electronic structure, including Mulliken population analysis
