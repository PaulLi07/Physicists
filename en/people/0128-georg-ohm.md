# Georg Ohm

**Born:** 16 March 1789

**Died / status:** 6 July 1854

**Fields:** Circuit theory, Electrical conduction

## Biography

Ohm was a German physicist who initially taught mathematics in schools and used teaching laboratories to investigate circuits, later holding posts in Nuremberg and Munich. Drawing on the mathematics of heat conduction, he connected current, voltage and conductor properties. His work was not immediately widely accepted.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established Ohm's law relating current, voltage and resistance under appropriate physical conditions.
- Developed a mathematical account of conduction that helped establish quantitative circuit theory.
