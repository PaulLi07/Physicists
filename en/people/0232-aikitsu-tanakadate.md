# Aikitsu Tanakadate

**Born:** 18 September 1856

**Died / status:** 21 May 1952

**Fields:** Geophysics, Metrology, Experimental physics

## Biography

Tanakadate was a Japanese physicist and geophysicist associated with Tokyo University. After studying in Europe and North America, he organized geomagnetic and gravity surveys in Japan and promoted precision observation, international scientific cooperation, physics education and aeronautical research.

## Major contributions to physics

- Organized systematic surveys of Japan's magnetic field
- Investigated gravity measurements and geophysical instruments
- Promoted experimental physics, aeronautical research and international metrological cooperation
