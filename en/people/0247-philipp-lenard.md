# Philipp Eduard Anton von Lenard

**Born:** 7 June 1862

**Died / status:** 20 May 1947

**Fields:** Electron physics, Experimental physics

## Biography

Philipp Eduard Anton von Lenard was born in Pressburg, Hungary (now Bratislava, Slovakia) and conducted research in electron physics and experimental physics. Nobel records list Kiel University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They investigated the penetration, absorption and propagation of cathode rays. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1905.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated the penetration, absorption and propagation of cathode rays
- Provided important experimental knowledge about electron beams

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1905
