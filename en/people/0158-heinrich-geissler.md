# Heinrich Geissler

**Born:** 26 May 1814

**Died / status:** 24 January 1879

**Fields:** Experimental techniques, Gas discharges, Vacuum physics

## Biography

Geissler was a German glass instrument maker and experimental pioneer based in Bonn, where he collaborated with Plücker and others. His glasswork and vacuum techniques made low-pressure electrical discharges accessible to reproducible laboratory investigation.

## Major contributions to physics

- Improved mercury vacuum pumps for low-pressure experiments
- Developed Geissler discharge tubes used in gas-discharge and cathode-ray research
- Advanced experimental physics through precision glass apparatus
