# Rudolf Ludwig Mössbauer

**Born:** 31 January 1929

**Died / status:** 14 September 2011

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Precision spectroscopy

## Biography

Rudolf Ludwig Mössbauer was born in Munich, Germany and conducted research in nuclear physics and precision spectroscopy. Nobel records list Technical University and California Institute of Technology (Caltech) as the affiliations at the time of the award. They discovered recoil-free resonant emission and absorption of gamma rays, the Mössbauer effect. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1961.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered recoil-free resonant emission and absorption of gamma rays, the Mössbauer effect
- Established a spectroscopic method for precise energy measurements and material studies

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1961
