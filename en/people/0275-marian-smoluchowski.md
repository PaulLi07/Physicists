# Marian Smoluchowski

**Born:** 28 May 1872

**Died / status:** 5 September 1917

**Fields:** Statistical physics, Brownian motion, Non-equilibrium physics

![Portrait of Marian Smoluchowski](../portraits/9875fe8d28d056ef3bd1-c6fd4345b6.jpg)

## Biography

Smoluchowski was a Polish physicist educated in Vienna who taught in Lviv and Kraków. He used molecular and probabilistic reasoning to explain Brownian motion, fluctuations and colloids, independently contributing alongside Einstein to tests of atomism and the foundations of statistical physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Independently developed a theory of Brownian motion
- Advanced statistical descriptions of diffusion, fluctuations and non-equilibrium random processes
- Formulated a kinetic equation for colloidal coagulation
