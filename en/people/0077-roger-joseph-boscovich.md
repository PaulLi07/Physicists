# Roger Joseph Boscovich

**Born:** 18 May 1711

**Died / status:** 13 February 1787

**Fields:** History of theories of matter, Theoretical mechanics, Astronomy

## Biography

Boscovich was a Jesuit scholar born in Ragusa who taught in Rome and later worked in Italy and France. His activities included astronomy, geodesy and diplomacy. He sought a unified account of nature based on point-like matter and distance-dependent forces, making a distinctive contribution to eighteenth-century theories of matter.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed describing matter through point particles and forces varying with separation.
- Investigated orbit determination and geodesy and sought common mechanical principles behind material properties.
