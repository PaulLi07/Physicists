## Biography

Wolfgang Paul was born in Lorenzkirch, Germany and conducted research in atomic physics and ion traps. Nobel records list University of Bonn as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed the Paul trap, using alternating electric fields to confine charged particles stably. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1989.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the Paul trap, using alternating electric fields to confine charged particles stably
- Provided foundational tools for precision ion studies and quantum control

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1989
