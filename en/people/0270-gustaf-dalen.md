# Nils Gustaf Dalén

**Born:** 30 November 1869

**Died / status:** 9 December 1937

**Fields:** Applied physics, Optical engineering

## Biography

Nils Gustaf Dalén was born in Stenstorp, Sweden and conducted research in applied physics and optical engineering. Nobel records list Swedish Gas-Accumulator Co. as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed automatic gas regulators and daylight-control devices for lighthouse and buoy illumination. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1912.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed automatic gas regulators and daylight-control devices for lighthouse and buoy illumination
- Improved the reliability and fuel efficiency of navigational lighting

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1912
