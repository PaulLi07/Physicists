## Biography

Val Logsdon Fitch was born in Merriman, NE, USA and conducted research in particle physics and CP symmetry. Nobel records list Princeton University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They discovered CP violation in neutral-kaon decays. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1980.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered CP violation in neutral-kaon decays
- Showed that some processes violate the combined symmetry of charge conjugation and spatial reflection

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1980
