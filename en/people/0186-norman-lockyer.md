# Norman Lockyer

**Born:** 17 May 1836

**Died / status:** 16 August 1920

**Fields:** Solar physics, Spectroscopy, Astrophysics

![Portrait of Norman Lockyer](../portraits/7d3d1d48443b48e0bf07.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Lockyer was a British astronomer and scientific organizer who initially worked as a civil servant. He concentrated on solar spectroscopy, founded Nature and later established observatories, helping to develop astrophysical observation and scientific communication.

## Major contributions to physics

- Used spectroscopy to study solar prominences and the solar atmosphere
- Independently observed the unfamiliar yellow solar line associated with the discovery of helium
- Developed methods of astrophysical spectroscopy and solar observation

## Historical and source notes

- The solar-spectral discovery of helium involved independent work by Janssen, Lockyer and others; it should not be attributed to a single discoverer.
