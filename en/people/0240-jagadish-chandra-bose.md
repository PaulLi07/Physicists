# Jagadish Chandra Bose

**Born:** 30 November 1858

**Died / status:** 23 November 1937

**Fields:** Microwave physics, Solid-state detection, Biophysics

![Portrait of Jagadish Chandra Bose](../portraits/882ec2dabeb2831725d6.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Bose was an Indian physicist and interdisciplinary researcher who taught at Presidency College in Calcutta and founded the Bose Institute. Working under colonial constraints, he designed sensitive detectors for millimetre-wave experiments and later applied precision instruments to plant responses.

## Major contributions to physics

- Conducted experiments on the generation, polarization, refraction and detection of millimetre waves
- Developed semiconductor contact detectors as an early contributor to solid-state detection
- Built sensitive instruments to investigate electrical and mechanical responses in plants
