# Leon Neil Cooper

**Born:** 28 February 1930

**Died / status:** 23 October 2024

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Superconductivity

## Biography

Leon Neil Cooper was born in New York, NY, USA and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and superconductivity. Nobel records list Brown University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed the BCS microscopic theory of superconductivity. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1972.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the BCS microscopic theory of superconductivity
- Explained conventional superconductivity through electron pairing and condensation

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1972
