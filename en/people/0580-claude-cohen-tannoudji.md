# Claude Cohen-Tannoudji

**Born:** 1 April 1933

**Died / status:** 22 October 2026

**Fields:** Atomic physics, Laser cooling

![Portrait of Claude Cohen-Tannoudji](../portraits/1c6b125c2ce8c4f30af5.jpg)

For the photographer or artist, date and type of image, consult the original image record.

## Biography

Claude Cohen-Tannoudji was born in Constantine, French Algeria (now Algeria) and conducted research in atomic physics and laser cooling. Nobel records list Collège de France and École Normale Supérieure as the affiliations at the time of the award. They developed methods to cool and trap atoms with laser light. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1997.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed methods to cool and trap atoms with laser light
- Provided foundations for ultracold-atom experiments, precision measurements and quantum gases

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1997

## Historical and source notes

- As of 26 October 2026, the Nobel structured record had not yet included his death. Announcements issued by Laboratoire Kastler Brossel and the Bibliothèque nationale de France on 23 October confirm that he died on 22 October 2026. The announcement date is not the date of death.
