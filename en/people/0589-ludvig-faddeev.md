# Ludvig Faddeev

**Born:** 23 March 1934

**Died / status:** 26 February 2017

**Fields:** Mathematical physics, Quantum field theory

![Portrait of Ludvig Faddeev](../portraits/72471b8fd9b199204a38-4cc943e6e4.jpg)

Sourced portrait or photograph; see the image record for provenance and reuse conditions.

## Biography

A Russian mathematical physicist based in Leningrad and St Petersburg, Faddeev connected scattering theory, gauge-field quantization, and integrable systems through rigorous mathematical methods.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established Faddeev equations for quantum three-body scattering.
- Developed gauge fixing in path integrals with Popov.
- Advanced quantum inverse scattering and integrable-model theory.
