# Paul Adrien Maurice Dirac

**Born:** 8 August 1902

**Died / status:** 20 October 1984

**Fields:** Quantum mechanics, Quantum field theory

## Biography

Paul Adrien Maurice Dirac was born in Bristol, United Kingdom and conducted research in quantum mechanics and quantum field theory. Nobel records list University of Cambridge as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed the Dirac equation combining quantum mechanics with special relativity. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1933.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the Dirac equation combining quantum mechanics with special relativity
- Predicted antimatter and developed foundational tools of quantum field theory

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1933
