# Clifford G. Shull

**Born:** 23 September 1915

**Died / status:** 31 March 2001

**Fields:** Neutron scattering, Condensed-matter physics

## Biography

Clifford G. Shull was born in Pittsburgh, PA, USA and conducted research in neutron scattering and condensed-matter physics. Nobel records list Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed neutron diffraction. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1994.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed neutron diffraction
- Used neutrons to determine atomic arrangements and magnetic structures in matter

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1994
