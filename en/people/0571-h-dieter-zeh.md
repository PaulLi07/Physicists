# H. Dieter Zeh

**Born:** 8 May 1932

**Died / status:** 15 April 2018

**Fields:** Quantum foundations, Quantum gravity

## Biography

A German theorist at Heidelberg, Zeh moved from nuclear physics to measurement, the arrow of time, and cosmology. His emphasis on environmental interactions helped establish decoherence theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Formulated an environmental approach to quantum decoherence.
- Investigated decoherence, measurement, and the emergence of classical behaviour.
- Studied the arrow of time and quantum cosmology.
