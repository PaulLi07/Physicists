# Richard E. Taylor

**Born:** 2 November 1929

**Died / status:** 22 February 2018

**Fields:** Particle physics, Deep inelastic scattering

## Biography

Richard E. Taylor was born in Medicine Hat, Alberta, Canada and conducted research in particle physics and deep inelastic scattering. Nobel records list Stanford University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They revealed nucleon substructure through high-energy electron scattering. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1990.

## Major contributions to physics

- Revealed nucleon substructure through high-energy electron scattering
- Provided crucial evidence for quarks as constituents of protons and neutrons

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1990
