# Felix Bloch

**Born:** 23 October 1905

**Died / status:** 10 September 1983

**Fields:** Nuclear magnetic resonance, Atomic physics

## Biography

Felix Bloch was born in Zurich, Switzerland and conducted research in nuclear magnetic resonance and atomic physics. Nobel records list Stanford University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They independently developed methods for detecting nuclear magnetic resonance in bulk matter. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1952.

## Major contributions to physics

- Independently developed methods for detecting nuclear magnetic resonance in bulk matter
- Provided experimental foundations for condensed-matter research and later magnetic-resonance technologies

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1952
