# Francis Hauksbee

**Born:** c. 1660

**Died / status:** April 1713

**Fields:** Electrostatics, Gas discharge, Experimental instruments

![Related historical document for Francis Hauksbee; not a portrait](../portraits/da59f5aaa0842cdfd8eb-c5a4d83a22.jpg)

An air pump made by Francis Hauksbee in 1708–1709, exhibited at the Science Museum, London. This is a related instrument, not a portrait.

## Biography

Hauksbee was an English instrument maker and experimental investigator from Colchester who performed numerous demonstrations for the Royal Society. He improved air pumps and frictional electrical devices and systematically studied luminescence in rarefied gases and static electricity, publishing his experiments in 1709.

## Major contributions to physics

- Improved double-barrel air pumps and rotating-glass electrical machines.
- Investigated electrical luminescence, static electricity and capillarity, helping establish eighteenth-century electrical experimentation.

## Historical and source notes

- 27 May 1660 is baptism and 29 April 1713 burial, not securely established birth and death days; he must also be distinguished from his nephew of the same name.
