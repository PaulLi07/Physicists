# James Bjorken

**Born:** 22 June 1934

**Died / status:** 6 August 2024

**Fields:** Particle physics

## Biography

An American theorist trained at Stanford, Bjorken worked mainly at SLAC and also at Fermilab. He connected field theory with high-energy scattering and helped establish the experimental interpretation of quark structure.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed Bjorken scaling in deep-inelastic scattering.
- Advanced parton descriptions of nucleon structure.
- Coauthored influential relativistic quantum-theory textbooks with Drell.
