# Charles Thomson Rees Wilson

**Born:** 14 February 1869

**Died / status:** 15 November 1959

**Fields:** Particle detection, Experimental physics

## Biography

Charles Thomson Rees Wilson was born in Glencorse, Scotland and conducted research in particle detection and experimental physics. Nobel records list University of Cambridge as the affiliation at the time of the award. They invented the cloud chamber, using supersaturated vapour to make charged-particle tracks visible. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1927.

## Major contributions to physics

- Invented the cloud chamber, using supersaturated vapour to make charged-particle tracks visible
- Opened direct visual observation of the paths of individual microscopic particles

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1927
