# Ibn Sahl

**Born:** c. 940 CE

**Died / status:** c. 1000

**Fields:** Geometrical optics, Refraction

## Biography

Ibn Sahl was a tenth-century mathematician and optical investigator active in Baghdad. Little is known securely about his life, birthplace or cultural identity. Around 984 he wrote on burning instruments, applying geometrical constructions to mirrors and lenses. Modern historians recovered the significance of this work from surviving manuscripts.

## Major contributions to physics

- Used a geometrical relation equivalent to the sine law of refraction in lens design.
- Analyzed focusing by curved mirrors and lenses and constructed lens shapes intended to reduce geometrical aberration.
