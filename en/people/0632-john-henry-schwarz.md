# John Henry Schwarz

## Biography

An American theorist who worked at Princeton before joining Caltech, Schwarz pursued superstrings as a framework for fundamental interactions. His collaboration with Green helped initiate a major revival of string theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the Green–Schwarz cancellation of gauge and gravitational anomalies.
- Developed superstring and supersymmetry methods.
- Investigated string dualities and unified-interaction frameworks.
