# Georgy Flerov

**Born:** 2 March 1913

**Died / status:** 19 November 1990

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Experimental physics

![Portrait of Georgy Flerov](../portraits/4c0a77ce20dc77e78caa-06f42ab705.jpg)

A Russian postage stamp issued in 2013 for the centenary of Flerov’s birth; this is a posthumous commemorative depiction. Retain the whole stamp rather than extracting the portrait alone.

## Biography

A Soviet experimental nuclear physicist, Flerov began nuclear-reaction research in Leningrad and later participated in the Soviet nuclear programme. A founding scientist of the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research in Dubna, he led its Laboratory of Nuclear Reactions and advanced heavy-ion and superheavy-element research.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered spontaneous fission of uranium with Petrzhak in 1940.
- Developed heavy-ion nuclear-reaction experiments to investigate heavy elements and nuclear stability.
- Founded and directed the Dubna Laboratory of Nuclear Reactions, organizing superheavy-element research.

## Historical and source notes

- The birth date is 2 March 1913 in the Gregorian calendar, corresponding to 17 February in the Julian calendar then used in Russia. His surname is also transliterated as Flyorov.
