# Joseph-Louis Lagrange

**Born:** 25 January 1736

**Died / status:** 10 April 1813

**Fields:** Analytical mechanics, Calculus of variations, Celestial mechanics

## Biography

Lagrange was a mathematician born in Turin who worked in Turin, Berlin and Paris. He combined variational methods with Newtonian mechanics. His Analytical Mechanics treated complicated systems by general methods rather than individual geometrical constructions and became a foundation of modern analytical mechanics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed descriptions of mechanical systems through generalized coordinates and Lagrange's equations.
- Advanced variational methods and the treatment of constraints in statics and dynamics.
- Investigated the three-body problem and orbital stability and identified special equilibrium solutions.
