# Oliver Heaviside

**Born:** 18 May 1850

**Died / status:** 3 February 1925

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Electrical physics, Mathematical physics

## Biography

Heaviside was a British electrical theorist who worked in telegraphy before becoming an independent researcher. He reformulated electromagnetic theory in vector language and studied cables, impedance and operational methods. His work profoundly influenced electromagnetic engineering despite uneven recognition during his lifetime.

## Major contributions to physics

- Helped express Maxwell's electromagnetic theory in its familiar vector form
- Developed transmission-line theory, the distortionless condition and impedance methods
- Proposed a reflecting ionized atmospheric layer and advanced operational calculus
