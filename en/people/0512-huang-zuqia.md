# Huang Zuqia

**Born:** 2 October 1924

**Died / status:** 7 September 2014

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Reactor theory

## Biography

Born in Changsha, Huang graduated from Tsinghua and worked on Chinese nuclear-science theory before teaching at Beijing Normal University. His career connected fundamental theory, nuclear-energy applications, and advanced education.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated neutron transport, reactor theory, and nuclear-energy applications.
- Contributed to Chinese nuclear-weapons theoretical research and calculations.
- Advanced teaching and research in nuclear physics and nonequilibrium statistical problems.
