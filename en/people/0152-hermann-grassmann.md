# Hermann Grassmann

**Born:** 15 April 1809

**Died / status:** 26 September 1877

**Fields:** Mathematical physics, Vector and exterior algebra

![Portrait of Hermann Grassmann](../portraits/d5184b7530b31d60183b.jpg)

## Biography

Grassmann was a German mathematician and linguist who spent much of his working life as a schoolteacher in Stettin. His abstract theory of extension received limited recognition initially, but became an important source for vector algebra, exterior algebra and the mathematical language of physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed vector operations and exterior algebra in general-dimensional spaces
- Created an extension theory that contributed to the later language of differential forms
- Provided algebraic structures later used for fermionic variables, without himself developing fermion theory

## Historical and source notes

- His principal professional identity was that of a mathematician.
- His inclusion concerns mathematics later foundational to physics; he did not himself discover fermion theory.
