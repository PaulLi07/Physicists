# Grete Hermann

**Born:** 2 March 1901

**Died / status:** 15 April 1984

**Fields:** Quantum foundations, Mathematical physics

## Biography

A German mathematician and philosopher of physics, Hermann studied with Noether in Göttingen. She critically examined logic and causality in early quantum mechanics, opposed Nazism, and later worked in education. Her contributions to quantum foundations long remained underrecognized.

## Major contributions to physics

- Identified limitations in the assumptions of von Neumann's argument against hidden variables.
- Analyzed causality, measurement, and the conditions of knowledge in quantum theory.
- Advanced philosophical and logical analysis of quantum mechanics.
