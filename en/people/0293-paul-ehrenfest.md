# Paul Ehrenfest

**Born:** 18 January 1880

**Died / status:** 25 September 1933

**Fields:** Statistical physics, Quantum mechanics

## Biography

An Austrian-born theorist, Ehrenfest studied with Boltzmann and succeeded Lorentz at Leiden. His thought experiments clarified the conceptual foundations of physics, and his teaching and exchanges with Einstein and Bohr helped shape the emerging quantum community.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established the Ehrenfest theorem relating quantum expectation values to classical equations of motion.
- Developed adiabatic-invariant ideas during the transition from old quantum theory to quantum mechanics.
- Classified phase transitions through discontinuities in thermodynamic derivatives.
