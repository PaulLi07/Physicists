# Nasir al-Din al-Tusi

**Born:** 18 February 1201

**Died / status:** 26 June 1274

**Fields:** Mathematical astronomy, Models of celestial motion

## Biography

Al-Tusi was a thirteenth-century Iranian mathematician and astronomer who worked under successive patrons and organized the Maragha observatory. He contributed to observations and astronomical tables and used geometrical devices to revise Ptolemaic astronomy, becoming a central figure in medieval models of celestial motion.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced the Tusi couple, a construction producing linear oscillation from two uniform circular motions.
- Organized observations and tables at Maragha and advanced applications of spherical trigonometry in astronomy.
