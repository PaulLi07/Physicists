# Karl Schwarzschild

**Born:** 9 October 1873

**Died / status:** 11 May 1916

**Fields:** General relativity, Astrophysics, Observational methods

![Portrait of Karl Schwarzschild](../portraits/5b721a91b6bf42023266.jpg)

## Biography

Schwarzschild was a German astronomer and physicist who directed observatories in Göttingen and Potsdam. He investigated stellar photometry and theory before deriving a major exact solution of Einstein's field equations while serving in the First World War; he died shortly afterwards.

## Major contributions to physics

- Derived the spherically symmetric vacuum solution of general relativity
- Advanced stellar photometry and astronomical radiation studies
- Investigated celestial mechanics and the theory of observational instruments
