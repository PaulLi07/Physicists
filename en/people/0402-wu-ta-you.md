# Wu Ta-You

**Born:** 1907

**Died / status:** 4 March 2000

**Fields:** Atomic and molecular physics, Theoretical physics

## Biography

Born in Guangdong, Wu received his doctorate at Michigan and taught at Peking University and the National Southwestern Associated University before working in Canada, the United States, and Taiwan. His molecular-spectroscopy research accompanied the training of students including Yang and Lee.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated vibrations and structures of polyatomic molecules.
- Wrote influential treatments of theoretical physics and quantum scattering.
- Developed physics education and research institutions.
