# Bryce DeWitt

**Born:** 8 January 1923

**Died / status:** 23 September 2004

**Fields:** Quantum gravity, Quantum field theory

![Portrait of Bryce DeWitt](../portraits/56e6f3f16c94b5ed1045-7146c6ba19.png)

Bryce DeWitt stands at right; Cécile DeWitt-Morette is seated at left. This is a group photograph.

## Biography

An American theorist trained by Schwinger, DeWitt worked at North Carolina and Texas. He extended quantum-field methods to gravity and curved spacetime and helped build research and teaching in quantum gravity.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed canonical quantum-gravity equations with Wheeler.
- Advanced background-field methods and quantum fields in curved spacetime.
- Developed and disseminated relative-state approaches to quantum theory.
