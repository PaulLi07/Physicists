**Born:** 6 January 1655

**Died / status:** 16 August 1705

## Biography

Jacob Bernoulli was a Swiss mathematician and elder brother of Johann who taught in Basel. An early investigator of calculus and variational questions, he connected curves, probability and mechanics. His studies of elastic curves and hanging chains contributed to the development of analytical mechanics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated the catenary, isochronous curves and brachistochrone-related problems in mathematical physics.
- Studied the equilibrium shapes of bent elastic rods, advancing the mathematical theory of elastic curves.
