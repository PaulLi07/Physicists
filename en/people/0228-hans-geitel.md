# Hans Geitel

**Born:** 16 July 1855

**Died / status:** 15 August 1923

**Fields:** Photoelectric effects, Atmospheric electricity, Atomic physics

![Julius Elster and Hans Geitel conducting an experiment](../portraits/75ee4d650cacb8da0b88-854111e37f.jpg)

A historical photograph of Julius Elster and Hans Geitel conducting an experiment, extracted unchanged from Figure 1 of Fricke and Schlegel (2017), credited to Archiv Fricke. This is a two-person photograph rather than a solo headshot.

## Biography

Geitel was a German physicist and teacher who worked closely with Elster in Wolfenbüttel. Combining physical interpretation with careful experiments on photocurrents, air ionization and radioactivity, their collaboration linked nineteenth-century electricity with emerging atomic physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed photoelectric cells and low-light electrical detection with Elster
- Investigated atmospheric electricity and spontaneous air ionization
- Contributed to early investigations of radioactivity and atomic-energy concepts

## Historical and source notes

- The death date follows Neue Deutsche Biographie: 15 August 1923. A 2017 historical article gives 23 August; this source discrepancy is retained.
