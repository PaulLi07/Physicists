# Ernst Abbe

**Born:** 23 January 1840

**Died / status:** 14 January 1905

**Fields:** Physical optics, Microscopy, Metrology

![Portrait of Ernst Abbe](../portraits/8082e58bc52322f4c113.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Abbe was a German physicist and optical instrument designer who worked with Carl Zeiss and Otto Schott in Jena. He connected diffraction theory, optical glass and lens manufacture, putting microscope design on a quantitative physical foundation. He also participated in reforms of industrial and research organization.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established the diffraction-based account of microscope resolution
- Developed aberration-control criteria, including the Abbe sine condition, to improve high-performance microscope objectives
- Designed precision optical instruments, including apparatus for measuring refractive index
