# Shen Kuo

**Born:** 1031

**Died / status:** 1095

**Fields:** Early Chinese physical science, Magnetism, Optics

## Biography

Shen Kuo was a Northern Song scientist and official involved in astronomy, calendars, engineering and military affairs. Late in life he compiled the Dream Pool Essays, recording observations, experiments and technical knowledge. Its discussions of astronomy, magnetism, optics and geology are major evidence for the history of Song science.

## Major contributions to physics

- Recorded the preparation and use of magnetized needles and described the difference between magnetic north and true north.
- Discussed pinhole imaging and concave mirrors, combining observation with geometrical explanation.
