# Sidney Coleman

**Born:** 7 March 1937

**Died / status:** 18 November 2007

**Fields:** Quantum field theory, Particle physics

## Biography

An American theorist educated at Caltech and based at Harvard, Coleman combined influential teaching with research on symmetry, vacuum structure, and nonperturbative field theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated radiative symmetry breaking with Weinberg.
- Proved restrictions on continuous-symmetry breaking in two-dimensional quantum field theory.
- Investigated false-vacuum decay, solitons, and field-theory dualities.
