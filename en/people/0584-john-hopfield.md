# John J. Hopfield

**Born:** 15 July 1933

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Statistical physics, Neural networks

## Biography

John J. Hopfield was born in Chicago, IL, USA and conducted research in statistical physics and neural networks. Nobel records list Princeton University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They proposed the Hopfield network as a model of associative memory. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2024.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed the Hopfield network as a model of associative memory
- Used energy functions and collective dynamics to describe information storage in neural networks

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2024
