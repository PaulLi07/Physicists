# Asher Peres

**Born:** 30 January 1934

**Died / status:** 1 January 2005

**Fields:** Quantum information, Quantum foundations

![Portrait of Asher Peres](../portraits/7e18ef8c4db606673f50-9c1906b89a.jpg)

Sourced portrait or photograph; see the image record for provenance and reuse conditions.

## Biography

An Israeli theorist at the Technion, Peres studied measurement, entanglement, and information processing through precise operational arguments, helping establish quantum information theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced the partial-transpose entanglement criterion.
- Co-proposed quantum teleportation.
- Investigated quantum contextuality, measurement, and relativistic quantum information.
