# Ludwig Prandtl

**Born:** 4 February 1875

**Died / status:** 15 August 1953

**Fields:** Fluid mechanics, Aerodynamics, Turbulence

![Portrait of Ludwig Prandtl](../portraits/2edc7fbd98d47e7ad6f3.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Prandtl was a German physicist and engineer who built a leading research school in Göttingen. Combining theory, wind-tunnel experiments and practical problems, he explained how thin viscous regions influence an otherwise nearly ideal flow and helped found modern aerodynamics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced boundary-layer theory
- Developed lifting-line theory and wind-tunnel methods
- Investigated turbulence, mixing-length models and compressible flow
