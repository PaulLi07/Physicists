# Geoffrey Burbidge

**Born:** 24 September 1925

**Died / status:** 26 January 2010

**Fields:** Nuclear astrophysics, Cosmology

![Portrait of Geoffrey Burbidge](../portraits/754f5ab857bc5085018a-c44ea61dfd.jpg)

## Biography

A British-born theoretical astrophysicist at UC San Diego and former Kitt Peak director, Burbidge studied stellar nucleosynthesis, radio galaxies, and cosmology. He also explored alternatives to standard cosmological models.

## Major contributions to physics

- Coauthored the B²FH account of stellar nucleosynthesis.
- Investigated energy sources in radio galaxies, quasars, and energetic objects.
- Developed alternative cosmological models that did not become the mainstream account.
