# John William Draper

**Born:** 5 May 1811

**Died / status:** 4 January 1882

**Fields:** Photochemistry, Thermal radiation, Scientific photography

![Portrait of John William Draper](../portraits/fd5633480d115fb37b07-f008ec31fe.jpg)

For the image’s date, creator, form and attribution, see its source record.

## Biography

Draper was a British-born American chemist, physicist and photographic pioneer at New York University. He connected chemical action, spectra and photography, produced early photographs of the Moon and investigated thermal emission and photochemical processes.

## Major contributions to physics

- Helped establish the principle that photochemical action requires absorbed light
- Investigated the onset of visible incandescence associated with the Draper point
- Conducted early astronomical photography and solar-spectrum experiments
