# Peter van Nieuwenhuizen

**Born:** 26 October 1938

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Supergravity, Quantum field theory

![Portrait of Peter van Nieuwenhuizen](../portraits/cae98ea738cc6ce7eb0f-b11b49b901.jpg)

Photograph of van Nieuwenhuizen working at a blackboard, from the Stony Brook departmental profile.

## Biography

A Dutch-born theorist at Stony Brook, van Nieuwenhuizen established supergravity with Freedman and Ferrara. His work also addresses quantum gravity, renormalization, and gauge-system quantization.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established supergravity with Freedman and Ferrara.
- Investigated ultraviolet properties of quantum gravity and supergravity.
- Developed BRST and gauge-field quantization methods.
