## Biography

John C. Mather was born in Roanoke, VA, USA and conducted research in cosmology and cosmic microwave background. Nobel records list NASA Goddard Space Flight Center as the affiliation at the time of the award. They led scientific work on COBE and the FIRAS experiment. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2006.

## Major contributions to physics

- Led scientific work on COBE and the FIRAS experiment
- Precisely established the nearly ideal black-body spectrum of the cosmic microwave background

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2006
