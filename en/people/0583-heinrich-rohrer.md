# Heinrich Rohrer

**Born:** 6 June 1933

**Died / status:** 16 May 2013

**Fields:** Surface physics, Scanning probes

## Biography

Heinrich Rohrer was born in Buchs, Switzerland and conducted research in surface physics and scanning probes. Nobel records list IBM Zurich Research Laboratory as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed the scanning tunnelling microscope with Gerd Binnig. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1986.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the scanning tunnelling microscope with Gerd Binnig
- Established a scanning-probe method for investigating atomic-scale surface structure

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1986
