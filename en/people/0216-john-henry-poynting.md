# John Henry Poynting

**Born:** 9 September 1852

**Died / status:** 30 March 1914

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Radiation physics, Gravitational experiments

![Portrait of John Henry Poynting](../portraits/c3be30a2393998b190f1.jpg)

## Biography

Poynting was a British physicist who taught at Mason College and the University of Birmingham. Combining theoretical work with precision measurement, he investigated electromagnetic energy transport, radiation pressure and gravity, helping clarify how fields carry energy.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced the electromagnetic energy-flux vector and associated energy-conservation theorem
- Investigated radiation pressure and its effects on small celestial bodies
- Performed precision gravitational experiments and measured Earth's mean density
