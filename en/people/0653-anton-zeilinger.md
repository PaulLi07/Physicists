# Anton Zeilinger

**Born:** 20 May 1945

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Quantum foundations, Quantum information

## Biography

Anton Zeilinger was born in Ried im Innkreis, Austria and conducted research in quantum foundations and quantum information. Nobel records list University of Vienna and Institute for Quantum Optics and Quantum Information, Austrian Academy of Sciences as the affiliations at the time of the award. They performed entangled-photon experiments and tested Bell inequalities. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2022.

## Major contributions to physics

- Performed entangled-photon experiments and tested Bell inequalities
- Established important experimental foundations for quantum information science

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2022
