# Friedrich Pockels

**Born:** 18 June 1865

**Died / status:** 29 August 1913

**Fields:** Crystal optics, Electro-optic effects, Condensed-matter physics

![Portrait of Friedrich Pockels](../portraits/8fdc2db91c183fc74a49-f85fa27c3e.jpg)

## Biography

Friedrich Pockels was a German physicist who worked in Göttingen and Heidelberg. He investigated the optical, electrical and thermal behaviour of crystals and used symmetry to analyse propagation under external fields. His sister Agnes independently became known for surface experiments.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated the linear electro-optic change of crystal refractive indices known as the Pockels effect
- Developed crystal-optical and photoelastic theory
- Applied crystal symmetry to electro-optic and other anisotropic properties
