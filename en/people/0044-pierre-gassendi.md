# Pierre Gassendi

**Born:** 22 January 1592

**Died / status:** 24 October 1655

**Fields:** History of atomism, Astronomy, History of kinematics

## Biography

Gassendi was a French philosopher, astronomer and priest who taught in Aix and Paris. He reinterpreted ancient atomism for early modern natural inquiry and took part in astronomical observations and debates about motion. His observation of the 1631 transit of Mercury is a celebrated achievement.

## Major contributions to physics

- Revived and modified atomism, contributing to seventeenth-century corpuscular accounts of nature.
- Practised observational astronomy and empirical investigation, including the 1631 transit of Mercury.
