**Born:** 1578

**Died / status:** 9 April 1643

## Biography

Castelli was an Italian Benedictine monk, mathematician and pupil of Galileo who taught in Pisa and Rome. He connected mathematics with hydraulic engineering and investigated flow in rivers and channels. His On the Measurement of Running Waters was an important early work of quantitative hydraulics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discussed continuity of flow through relationships between velocity and cross-sectional area.
- Applied mathematical measurement to rivers, channels and hydraulic engineering, advancing quantitative studies of fluids.
