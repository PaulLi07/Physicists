# George Atwood

## Biography

Atwood was an English mathematician and teacher of experimental physics who taught at Cambridge and became a Royal Society fellow. His low-friction pulley and weighted-body apparatus used small accelerations to investigate falling motion. He also studied pendulums, mechanical education and ship stability.

## Major contributions to physics

- Invented Atwood's machine, making accelerated motion under gravity easier to demonstrate and measure quantitatively.
- Advanced experimental teaching of Newtonian mechanics and investigated pendulums and mechanical equilibrium.
