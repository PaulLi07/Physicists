# John Clarke Slater

**Born:** 22 December 1900

**Died / status:** 25 July 1976

**Fields:** Atomic physics, Electronic structure, Condensed-matter theory

## Biography

Slater was an American theoretical physicist trained at Harvard who led physics research at MIT and later worked at the University of Florida. He applied quantum mechanics to the electronic structure of atoms, molecules and solids, emphasizing practical calculations while developing influential research groups and textbooks.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed and popularized Slater-determinant representations of antisymmetric many-electron wavefunctions
- Introduced Slater-type orbitals and screening rules for atomic electronic calculations
- Developed practical local-exchange approximations and other methods for molecular and solid-state calculations
