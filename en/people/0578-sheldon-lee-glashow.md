## Biography

Sheldon Lee Glashow was born in New York, NY, USA and conducted research in particle physics and electroweak theory. Nobel records list Harvard University, Lyman Laboratory as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed a unified theory of electromagnetic and weak interactions. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1979.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed a unified theory of electromagnetic and weak interactions
- Predicted and explained phenomena including weak neutral currents

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1979
