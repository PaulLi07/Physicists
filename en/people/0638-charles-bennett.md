## Biography

An American physicist at IBM, Bennett investigates the physical foundations of information processing. He connects reversible computation, entanglement, and communication and helped establish quantum information science.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed reversible computing and information thermodynamics.
- Proposed the BB84 quantum key-distribution protocol with Brassard.
- Co-developed quantum teleportation, entanglement distillation, and superdense coding.
