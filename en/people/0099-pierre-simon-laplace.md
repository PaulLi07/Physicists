# Pierre-Simon Laplace

**Born:** 23 March 1749

**Died / status:** 5 March 1827

**Fields:** Celestial mechanics, Potential theory, Mathematical physics

## Biography

Laplace was a French mathematician and astronomer associated with Parisian scientific institutions and public administration. He used calculus and probability to investigate gravitational systems, celestial motion and other physical processes. His Celestial Mechanics systematically advanced analytical treatments of Newtonian gravitation.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed perturbation theory and investigated long-term variations in planetary and satellite motion.
- Established the potential-theory equation bearing his name, used in gravitation, electrostatics and fluid problems.
- Collaborated with Lavoisier on calorimetry and investigated sound speed and capillarity.
