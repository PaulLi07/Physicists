# Anne L’Huillier

**Born:** 16 August 1958

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Attosecond physics, Ultrafast optics

## Biography

Anne L’Huillier was born in Paris, France and conducted research in attosecond physics and ultrafast optics. Nobel records list Lund University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed experimental methods for producing attosecond light pulses. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2023.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed experimental methods for producing attosecond light pulses
- Made it possible to investigate ultrafast electron dynamics in matter

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2023
