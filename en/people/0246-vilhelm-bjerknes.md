# Vilhelm Bjerknes

**Born:** 14 March 1862

**Died / status:** 9 April 1951

**Fields:** Atmospheric physics, Fluid mechanics, Dynamical meteorology

## Biography

Vilhelm Bjerknes was a Norwegian physicist and meteorologist who began by assisting his father's hydrodynamic research. Working in Stockholm, Leipzig and Bergen, he applied thermodynamics and fluid dynamics to the atmosphere and founded the Bergen school of meteorology.

## Major contributions to physics

- Formulated weather prediction as an initial-value problem for physical equations
- Developed physical foundations for atmospheric circulation, air-mass and frontal studies
- Established the Bergen school and helped found dynamical meteorology
