# Subir Sachdev

**Born:** 2 December 1961

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Quantum many-body physics

## Biography

An Indian-born theorist who worked at Yale and Harvard, Sachdev studies quantum criticality, correlated electrons, and strange metals, including connections with holography.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed theories of quantum phase transitions and criticality.
- Introduced the Sachdev–Ye many-body model with Jinwu Ye.
- Investigated correlated electrons, strange metals, and holographic connections.
