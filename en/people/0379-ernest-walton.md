# Ernest Thomas Sinton Walton

**Born:** 6 October 1903

**Died / status:** 25 June 1995

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Accelerator physics

## Biography

Ernest Thomas Sinton Walton was born in Dungarvan, Ireland and conducted research in nuclear physics and accelerator physics. Nobel records list Trinity College as the affiliation at the time of the award. They produced nuclear transformations using artificially accelerated charged particles. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1951.

## Major contributions to physics

- Produced nuclear transformations using artificially accelerated charged particles
- Developed early high-voltage particle-accelerator experiments

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1951
