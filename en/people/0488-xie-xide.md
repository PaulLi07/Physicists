# Xie Xide

**Born:** 19 March 1921

**Died / status:** 4 March 2000

**Fields:** Solid-state physics, Surface physics

## Biography

Born in Fujian, Xie studied and conducted research at Smith College, MIT, and Stanford. Returning to China in 1952, she taught at Fudan and later served as its president, building surface-physics research and international scientific exchanges.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated semiconductor bands, crystal defects, and surface electronic structure.
- Helped establish Fudan’s surface-physics research programme.
- Developed training in solid-state and semiconductor physics in China.
