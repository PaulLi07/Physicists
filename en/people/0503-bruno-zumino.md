# Bruno Zumino

**Born:** 28 April 1923

**Died / status:** 21 June 2014

**Fields:** Supergravity, Quantum field theory

![Portrait of Bruno Zumino](../portraits/c3cba465a9e501f2caf7-7d4054ca1c.jpg)

## Biography

An Italian-born theorist at CERN and Berkeley, Zumino helped turn supersymmetry into concrete four-dimensional field theories. He also developed supergravity and the study of quantum anomalies.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed four-dimensional supersymmetric field models with Wess.
- Independently constructed supergravity with Deser.
- Developed consistency conditions for gauge anomalies.
