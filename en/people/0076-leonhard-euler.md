# Leonhard Euler

**Born:** 15 April 1707

**Died / status:** 18 September 1783

**Fields:** Fluid mechanics, Rigid-body mechanics, Mathematical physics

## Biography

Euler was a Swiss mathematician and physicist who worked at the St Petersburg and Berlin academies. Even after losing his sight, he continued producing research. He systematically applied calculus, differential equations and variational methods to mechanics, fluids and optics and was a central builder of eighteenth-century mathematical physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established equations for the motion of ideal fluids, advancing continuum mechanics.
- Developed equations for rigid-body rotation and methods of analytical mechanics.
- Applied differential equations and variational methods to optics, vibrations and celestial motion.
