## Biography

Zweig was born in Moscow and moved to the United States as a young child. Trained with Feynman at Caltech, he proposed fundamental constituents of hadrons independently of Murray Gell-Mann. He later investigated neurobiology, cochlear mechanics and signal analysis at institutions including Los Alamos and MIT, connecting particle physics with biophysics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Independently proposed hadronic constituents later known as quarks, calling them aces
- Applied physical and mathematical methods to cochlear mechanics and auditory signals
- Developed signal-processing methods associated with the continuous wavelet transform
