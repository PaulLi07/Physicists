# John Bardeen

**Born:** 23 May 1908

**Died / status:** 30 January 1991

**Fields:** Semiconductor physics, Superconductivity

## Biography

John Bardeen was born in Madison, WI, USA and conducted research in semiconductor physics and superconductivity. Nobel records list University of Illinois as the affiliation at the time of the awards. They made pioneering transistor research contributions with Walter Brattain and William Shockley. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1956 and 1972.

## Major contributions to physics

- Made pioneering transistor research contributions with Walter Brattain and William Shockley
- Developed the BCS microscopic theory of conventional superconductivity with Leon Cooper and John Robert Schrieffer

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1956, 1972
