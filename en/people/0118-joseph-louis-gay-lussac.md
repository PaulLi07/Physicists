**Born:** 6 December 1778

## Biography

Gay-Lussac was a French chemist and physicist associated with the École Polytechnique and Parisian scientific institutions. He investigated gas volumes, temperature and chemical combination and joined high-altitude balloon observations, connecting physical measurement with quantitative chemistry.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated thermal expansion of gases and relationships between temperature and volume.
- Established simple whole-number ratios for combining gas volumes and used balloon experiments to investigate the atmosphere.
