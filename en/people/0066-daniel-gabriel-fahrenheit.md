# Daniel Gabriel Fahrenheit

**Born:** 24 May 1686

**Died / status:** 16 September 1736

**Fields:** Thermometry, Thermal physics

![Daniel Gabriel Fahrenheit — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/90cc74c979ff44d7544b.jpg)

## Biography

Fahrenheit was an instrument maker and experimental investigator born in Danzig and active mainly in the Netherlands. He travelled in Europe and exchanged knowledge with scholars including Rømer. His reproducible thermometers and temperature scale helped turn temperature into a precision measurement rather than a qualitative sensation.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed reliable alcohol and mercury thermometers and established the Fahrenheit temperature scale.
- Investigated boiling, freezing and pressure effects, improving experimental thermometry.

## Historical and source notes

- Royal Society records give 14 or 24 May 1686; this archive follows Deutsche Biographie in using 24 May.
