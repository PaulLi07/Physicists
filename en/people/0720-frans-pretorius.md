# Frans Pretorius

**Born:** 31 July 1973

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** General relativity, Computational physics

## Biography

A South African-born theorist who worked in Canada and the United States, Pretorius joined Princeton. His numerical studies of Einstein's equations overcame central obstacles to simulating merging black holes.

## Major contributions to physics

- Produced an early stable simulation of binary-black-hole inspiral and merger.
- Developed generalized-harmonic and numerical-relativity techniques.
- Investigated strong-gravity dynamics and gravitational-wave generation.
