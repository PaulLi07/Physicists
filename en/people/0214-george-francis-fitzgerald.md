# George Francis FitzGerald

**Born:** 3 August 1851

**Died / status:** 21 February 1901

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Prehistory of relativity, Theoretical physics

![Portrait of George Francis FitzGerald](../portraits/8096717609fe15a3d3b1.jpg)

## Biography

FitzGerald was an Irish theoretical physicist at Trinity College Dublin and an influential member of the Maxwellian school. He connected electromagnetic theory with experiments and proposed a contraction of moving bodies as a possible explanation of ether-drift results.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed producing electromagnetic waves with oscillating currents
- Advanced the length-contraction hypothesis in the prehistory of special relativity
- Promoted Maxwell's field theory and investigated electromagnetic radiation
