# Heinz London

**Born:** 7 November 1907

**Died / status:** 3 August 1970

**Fields:** Low-temperature physics, Superconductivity

![Portrait of Heinz London](../portraits/454ca624b21cb643b2a7-a68136893b.png)

## Biography

A German-born low-temperature physicist who moved to Britain under Nazi persecution, London worked at Oxford and other institutions. He combined superconductivity theory with experimental methods for reaching lower temperatures.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the London equations of superconducting electrodynamics with Fritz London.
- Advanced low-temperature experimental techniques.
- Proposed dilution refrigeration using mixtures of helium isotopes.
