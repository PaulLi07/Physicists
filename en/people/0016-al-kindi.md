# Al-Kindi

**Born:** c. 801 CE

**Died / status:** c. 873 CE

**Fields:** Geometrical optics, History of acoustics, Natural philosophy

## Biography

Al-Kindi was a philosopher, mathematician and investigator of nature in the Arabic scholarly tradition, active mainly in Abbasid Baghdad. He helped absorb and transform Greek learning. His writings ranged across geometry, optics, music and medicine and sought mathematical approaches to natural phenomena.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated visual rays, light propagation and burning mirrors within geometrical optics.
- Applied mathematical proportions to music and sound, bringing quantitative reasoning to natural philosophy.
