# Fred Hoyle

**Born:** 24 June 1915

**Died / status:** 20 August 2001

**Fields:** Nuclear astrophysics, Cosmology

![Portrait of Fred Hoyle](../portraits/07c93822756f83ceb2da-5c17398d08.jpg)

## Biography

A British astrophysicist at Cambridge, Hoyle helped build theoretical-astronomy institutions. He made major contributions to stellar nucleosynthesis and also proposed the steady-state model later contradicted by observations.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed stellar nucleosynthesis and coauthored the B²FH synthesis.
- Predicted the carbon-12 resonance enabling stellar carbon production.
- Proposed steady-state cosmology with Bondi and Gold.
