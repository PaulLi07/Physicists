# Donald Lynden-Bell

**Born:** 5 April 1935

**Died / status:** 6 February 2018

**Fields:** Galactic dynamics, Astrophysics

![Portrait of Donald Lynden-Bell](../portraits/37fd1a3d1e5a0acdfdc6-77d38ffa6c.jpg)

Sourced portrait or photograph; see the image record for provenance and reuse conditions.

## Biography

A British theoretical astrophysicist at Cambridge, Lynden-Bell applied dynamics and statistical mechanics to galaxies, accretion discs, and active nuclei, helping establish black holes as astrophysical engines.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed violent-relaxation theory for collisionless stellar systems.
- Proposed accreting supermassive black holes as engines of active galactic nuclei.
- Investigated accretion-disc evolution and Galactic dynamics.
