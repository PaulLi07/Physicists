# Léon Foucault

**Born:** 18 September 1819

**Died / status:** 11 February 1868

**Fields:** Classical mechanics, Optics, Electromagnetism

## Biography

Foucault was a French experimental physicist who turned from early interests in medicine and photography to precision instruments and optics. His pendulum made Earth's rotation directly visible, while his rotating-mirror experiments advanced laboratory measurements of the propagation of light.

## Major contributions to physics

- Demonstrated Earth's rotation with the Foucault pendulum and developed gyroscope experiments
- Improved rotating-mirror measurements of light speed and compared propagation in air and water
- Investigated eddy currents in conducting materials
