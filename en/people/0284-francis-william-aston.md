# Francis William Aston

**Born:** 1 September 1877

**Died / status:** 20 November 1945

**Fields:** Mass spectrometry, Nuclear physics, Isotope physics

![Portrait of Francis William Aston](../portraits/1dd4133471eb3bdf3e37.jpg)

## Biography

Aston was a British experimental physicist at Cambridge's Cavendish Laboratory and received the 1922 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. Developing mass spectrography from discharge and positive-ray research, he established many isotopic compositions and important regularities in atomic masses.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed high-resolution mass spectrographs and measured many isotopes
- Provided experimental support for the whole-number rule
- Produced precision mass data relevant to mass defects and nuclear binding
