# Knut Ångström

**Born:** 12 January 1857

**Died / status:** 4 March 1910

**Fields:** Solar physics, Atmospheric physics, Radiometry

## Biography

Knut Ångström was a Swedish experimental physicist and son of Anders Jonas Ångström. Working in Stockholm and Uppsala, he investigated solar radiation and atmospheric absorption and designed influential radiometric instruments while developing new physics facilities.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed an electrical-compensation pyrheliometer for direct solar radiation
- Measured the solar constant and solar radiation at high-altitude sites
- Investigated infrared absorption by atmospheric gases
