# Hendrik Willem Bakhuis Roozeboom

**Born:** 24 October 1854

**Died / status:** 8 February 1907

**Fields:** Thermodynamics, Phase equilibrium, Physical chemistry

## Biography

Bakhuis Roozeboom was a Dutch physical chemist who worked in Leiden and became a professor in Amsterdam. He systematically applied Gibbs's phase rule to real multicomponent systems, connecting thermodynamic theory with experimentally useful phase diagrams.

## Major contributions to physics

- Systematically applied Gibbs's phase rule to multiphase equilibrium
- Investigated solid–liquid equilibria, solid solutions and multicomponent diagrams
- Advanced applications of phase thermodynamics to chemical and materials research
