# Shirley Ann Jackson

**Born:** 5 August 1946

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Theoretical physics

![Portrait of Shirley Ann Jackson](../portraits/c1120ca49e284c89f66d-d38589fbe1.jpg)

Shirley Ann Jackson, wearing red at left, receives the National Medal of Science from President Obama. The Commons free-licence claim is disputed in a deletion nomination dated 5 May 2026; website republication permission has not been verified.

## Biography

An American theorist, Jackson was the first Black woman to earn a doctorate at MIT. She studied condensed matter at Bell Laboratories and later chaired the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission and led Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated two-dimensional electrons, semiconductors, and charge-density waves.
- Developed theoretical analyses of condensed-matter systems.
- Advanced science-based nuclear regulation and scientific education.
