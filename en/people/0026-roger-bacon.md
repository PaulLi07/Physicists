## Biography

Roger Bacon was an English Franciscan scholar who studied and taught at Oxford and Paris. Drawing extensively on Arabic and Greek learning, he stressed mathematics, languages and experience in works such as the Opus maius. His optics helped transmit medieval traditions of the study of vision.

## Major contributions to physics

- Applied geometry to vision, reflection and refraction and transmitted Ibn al-Haytham's optical tradition.
- Emphasized experience and mathematics in natural knowledge and discussed lenses and optical devices.
