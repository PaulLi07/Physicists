# Gregor Wentzel

**Born:** 17 February 1898

**Died / status:** 12 August 1978

**Fields:** Quantum mechanics, Quantum field theory

## Biography

A German-born theoretical physicist who taught in Europe and at the University of Chicago, Wentzel helped develop early quantum mechanics. He later investigated meson theory and quantum fields and influenced graduate teaching through systematic theoretical texts.

## Major contributions to physics

- Contributed to the semiclassical WKB approximation.
- Investigated quantum scattering, meson theory, and field quantization.
- Applied asymptotic expansions to quantum motion.
