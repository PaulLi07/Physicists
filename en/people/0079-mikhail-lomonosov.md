# Mikhail Lomonosov

**Born:** 19 November 1711

**Died / status:** 15 April 1765 (Gregorian)

**Fields:** History of thermal physics, Theories of matter, Experimental physics

![Mikhail Lomonosov — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/71778ecbf2351bea9b4f.jpg)

The creator, date and form of this likeness are documented at the image source.

## Biography

Lomonosov was a Russian scientist, educational reformer and writer from a northern village who studied in Moscow, St Petersburg and Germany. At the St Petersburg Academy he worked on chemistry and physics, promoted scientific education and helped establish Moscow University.

## Major contributions to physics

- Explained heat in terms of corpuscular motion and opposed treating it simply as an independent substance.
- Investigated gases, electricity and optics and promoted experimental and quantitative approaches in Russian science.
