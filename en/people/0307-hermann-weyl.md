# Hermann Weyl

**Born:** 9 November 1885

**Died / status:** 8 December 1955

**Fields:** Mathematical physics, Gauge theory

## Biography

Weyl was a German mathematician and theoretical physicist who worked in Göttingen and Zürich before joining the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton. He used geometry, analysis, and group theory to reshape the mathematical language of relativity and quantum physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced an early formulation of gauge invariance.
- Developed the group-representation approach to quantum mechanics and symmetry.
- Introduced Weyl spinors in relativistic particle theory.
