# Edward Teller

**Born:** 15 January 1908

**Died / status:** 9 September 2003

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Molecular physics

## Biography

A Hungarian-born American theorist, Teller trained in Germany and left Europe after the rise of Nazism, moving to the United States in 1935. He studied molecular quantum theory and nuclear physics, participated in the Manhattan Project and thermonuclear-weapons research, helped establish the Livermore laboratory, and became deeply involved in nuclear-policy debates.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the Gamow–Teller transitions in beta-decay theory with George Gamow.
- Formulated the Jahn–Teller effect with Hermann Jahn, explaining structural distortions associated with certain degenerate electronic states.
- Contributed to nuclear and thermonuclear research and helped establish Lawrence Livermore Laboratory.
