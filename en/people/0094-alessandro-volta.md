# Alessandro Volta

**Born:** 18 February 1745

**Died / status:** 5 March 1827

**Fields:** Electrical science, Electrical sources, Experimental physics

![Alessandro Volta — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/12fcea74a54864515476.jpeg)

## Biography

Volta was an Italian physicist from Como who held the chair of experimental physics at Pavia. He studied static electricity and gases and developed an interpretation of Galvani's experiments different from animal electricity. His voltaic pile provided sustained electrical current and opened a new period of electrical experimentation.

## Major contributions to physics

- Invented the voltaic pile, providing a practical source for sustained-current experiments.
- Investigated contact electricity and potential differences and improved the electrophorus and electrical measuring instruments.
