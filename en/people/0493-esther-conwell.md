# Esther Conwell

**Born:** 23 May 1922

**Died / status:** 16 November 2014

**Fields:** Semiconductor physics, Condensed-matter physics

![Portrait of Esther Conwell](../portraits/47ac94ca7c9065c51333-c244782243.jpg)

Sourced portrait or photograph; see the image record for provenance and reuse conditions.

## Biography

An American physicist educated at Rochester and Chicago, Conwell worked at Bell Laboratories, Xerox, and Rochester. She investigated charge transport in semiconductors and organic materials and supported women in science.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed semiconductor impurity-scattering theory with Weisskopf.
- Investigated high-field carrier transport.
- Studied charge motion in conducting polymers and organic semiconductors.
