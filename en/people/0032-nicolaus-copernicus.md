# Nicolaus Copernicus

**Born:** 19 February 1473

**Died / status:** 24 May 1543

**Fields:** Astronomy, History of cosmology

## Biography

Copernicus was a Polish astronomer educated in Krakow and Italy who later held a church office in Frombork while pursuing astronomical calculations. His On the Revolutions of the Heavenly Spheres, published in 1543, rearranged the roles of the Sun, Earth and planets and helped transform European astronomy and natural philosophy.

## Major contributions to physics

- Constructed a mathematical heliocentric planetary system using Earth's revolution and rotation to explain celestial appearances.
- Explained apparent planetary retrograde motion through Earth's motion, providing a new framework for Kepler and Newton.
