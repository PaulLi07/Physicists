# Kip S. Thorne

**Born:** 1 June 1940

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Gravitational waves, Experimental physics, Relativity

## Biography

Kip S. Thorne was born in Logan, UT, USA and conducted research in gravitational waves, experimental physics and relativity. Nobel records list LIGO/VIRGO Collaboration and California Institute of Technology (Caltech) as the affiliations at the time of the award. They made decisive contributions to the LIGO detector and the first direct observation of gravitational waves. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2017.

## Major contributions to physics

- Made decisive contributions to the LIGO detector and the first direct observation of gravitational waves
- Opened gravitational-wave astronomy through precision interferometry

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2017
