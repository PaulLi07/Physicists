# Burton Richter

**Born:** 22 March 1931

**Died / status:** 18 July 2018

**Fields:** Particle physics, Experimental physics

## Biography

Burton Richter was born in Brooklyn, NY, USA and conducted research in particle physics and experimental physics. Nobel records list Stanford Linear Accelerator Center as the affiliation at the time of the award. They led independent experiments that discovered the J/psi particle. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1976.

## Major contributions to physics

- Led independent experiments that discovered the J/psi particle
- Provided key evidence for the charm quark and the quark model

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1976
