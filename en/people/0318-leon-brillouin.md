# Léon Brillouin

**Born:** 7 August 1889

**Died / status:** 4 October 1969

**Fields:** Solid-state physics, Wave theory

![Portrait of Léon Brillouin](../portraits/edc5d19c8eaeb3b888d7.jpg)

## Biography

A French theoretical physicist from a scientific family, Brillouin worked in France and the United States. His studies of electrons and sound in crystals were followed by research connecting statistical physics, information, and thermodynamics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced Brillouin zones in reciprocal space.
- Predicted Brillouin scattering of light by acoustic excitations.
- Contributed to the WKB approximation and studied information and entropy.
