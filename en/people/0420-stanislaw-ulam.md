# Stanislaw Ulam

**Born:** 13 April 1909

**Died / status:** 13 May 1984

**Fields:** Computational physics, Mathematical physics

## Biography

A Polish-born mathematician who moved to the United States before the war, Ulam spent much of his career at Los Alamos. He applied probability, computing, and dynamical-systems ideas to complex physical problems.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed Monte Carlo computational methods with von Neumann and others.
- Participated in the Fermi–Pasta–Ulam numerical experiment on nonlinear oscillators.
- Investigated theoretical nuclear-reaction and thermonuclear-device problems.
