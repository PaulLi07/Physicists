# Vadim Berezinskii

**Born:** 15 July 1935

**Died / status:** 23 June 1980

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Statistical physics

## Biography

A Soviet theorist who worked in Leningrad and Moscow, Berezinskii studied two-dimensional and disordered systems. His short career established ideas about topological excitations and phase transitions that transformed low-dimensional physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established the possibility of quasi-long-range order in two dimensions.
- Developed vortex-unbinding ideas underlying the BKT transition.
- Investigated disorder and low-dimensional electronic systems.
