# Josiah Willard Gibbs

**Born:** 11 February 1839

**Died / status:** 28 April 1903

**Fields:** Thermodynamics, Statistical physics, Mathematical physics

## Biography

Gibbs was an American mathematical physicist whose academic career was centred at Yale. He developed abstract and powerful treatments of thermodynamics, phase equilibrium and statistical ensembles. Initially slow to circulate, these became shared foundations of physics, physical chemistry and engineering thermodynamics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Systematized chemical potential, free energy and multiphase equilibrium, including the phase rule
- Established a systematic statistical-mechanical framework using ensembles, including the canonical and grand-canonical descriptions
- Developed vector analysis and methods used in mathematical physics
