# Zhang Heng

**Born:** 78 CE

**Died / status:** 139 CE

**Fields:** Early Chinese physical science, Astronomical instruments, History of seismological observation

## Biography

Zhang Heng was a scientist, writer and official of China's Eastern Han dynasty. As an imperial astronomer he worked on calendars and astronomical instruments. He combined mechanical construction with astronomical observation. His earthquake-detecting instrument is described in historical texts, but the original is lost and its internal mechanism remains debated.

## Major contributions to physics

- Constructed a water-powered armillary sphere to demonstrate celestial motions.
- Created an instrument intended to indicate the direction of distant earthquakes through a mechanical response, an important episode in the history of seismological instruments.

## Historical and source notes

- The original earthquake-detecting instrument is lost; its internal structure and modern reconstructions are debated.
