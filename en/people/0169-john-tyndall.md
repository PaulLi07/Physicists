# John Tyndall

**Born:** 2 August 1820

**Died / status:** 4 December 1893

**Fields:** Thermal radiation, Atmospheric physics, Optics

## Biography

Tyndall was an Irish-born British experimental physicist at London's Royal Institution, also known for mountaineering and public lectures. His investigations of thermal radiation, scattering and magnetism connected laboratory physics with atmospheric processes, while his demonstrations brought science to a wide audience.

## Major contributions to physics

- Measured infrared absorption by gases including water vapour and carbon dioxide
- Investigated the scattering of light by suspended particles
- Studied diamagnetism, radiative heat transfer and sound propagation
