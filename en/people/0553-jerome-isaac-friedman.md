## Biography

Jerome I. Friedman was born in Chicago, IL, USA and conducted research in particle physics and deep inelastic scattering. Nobel records list Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) as the affiliation at the time of the award. They revealed nucleon substructure through high-energy electron scattering. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1990.

## Major contributions to physics

- Revealed nucleon substructure through high-energy electron scattering
- Provided crucial evidence for quarks as constituents of protons and neutrons

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1990
