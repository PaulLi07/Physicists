# Fang Lizhi

**Born:** 12 February 1936

**Died / status:** 6 April 2012

**Fields:** Astrophysics, Cosmology

![Portrait of Fang Lizhi](../portraits/85bd1d63dcc8a8231815-e0e1d3c68e.jpg)

## Biography

Born in Beijing, Fang taught at the University of Science and Technology of China and served as its vice-president before joining the University of Arizona. He studied cosmology and galaxy formation and participated in scientific education and public debate.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated relativistic cosmology and the early universe.
- Studied galaxy formation, quasars, and large-scale structure.
- Advanced modern astrophysics research and teaching in China.
