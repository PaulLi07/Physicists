# Homi J. Bhabha

**Born:** 30 October 1909

**Died / status:** 24 January 1966

**Fields:** Particle physics, Cosmic rays

## Biography

An Indian theorist educated at Cambridge, Bhabha returned to India to establish the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research and organize atomic-energy research. His scientific contributions concerned cosmic rays and quantum electrodynamics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Calculated electron–positron scattering, now called Bhabha scattering.
- Developed electromagnetic cosmic-ray shower theory with Heitler.
- Built Indian nuclear-science and high-energy-physics institutions.
