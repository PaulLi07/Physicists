# Polykarp Kusch

**Born:** 26 January 1911

**Died / status:** 20 March 1993

**Fields:** Atomic physics, Precision measurement

## Biography

Polykarp Kusch was born in Blankenburg, Germany and conducted research in atomic physics and precision measurement. Nobel records list Columbia University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They precisely measured the electron's magnetic moment and established its small departure from the simple Dirac value. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1955.

## Major contributions to physics

- Precisely measured the electron's magnetic moment and established its small departure from the simple Dirac value
- Provided experimental evidence for the anomalous magnetic moment and quantum-electrodynamic corrections

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1955
