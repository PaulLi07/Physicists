**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

## Biography

An American theorist at UC Santa Barbara, Horowitz studies singularities, black holes in string theory, and holography. His work connects classical gravitation, quantum microstates, and strongly coupled matter.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated string-theoretic black holes, black branes, and microscopic structure.
- Helped develop Calabi–Yau superstring compactification.
- Developed holographic-superconductor models.
