# William Crookes

**Born:** 17 June 1832

**Died / status:** 4 April 1919

**Fields:** Gas discharges, Vacuum physics, Spectroscopy

![Portrait of William Crookes](../portraits/359b829fb4888acaf273.jpg)

## Biography

Crookes was a British chemist and physicist who worked mainly in a private laboratory and founded scientific periodicals. His vacuum and discharge experiments influenced later cathode-ray research. He also pursued spiritualism, a controversial activity distinct from his established laboratory contributions.

## Major contributions to physics

- Improved high-vacuum discharge tubes and investigated cathode rays
- Invented the Crookes radiometer, stimulating research on rarefied gases
- Discovered thallium through spectral analysis
