# George F. Smoot

**Born:** 20 February 1945

**Died / status:** 18 September 2025

**Fields:** Cosmology, Cosmic microwave background

## Biography

George F. Smoot was born in Yukon, FL, USA and conducted research in cosmology and cosmic microwave background. Nobel records list University of California as the affiliation at the time of the award. They led key work on COBE's DMR experiment. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2006.

## Major contributions to physics

- Led key work on COBE's DMR experiment
- Detected large-scale temperature anisotropies in the cosmic microwave background

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2006
