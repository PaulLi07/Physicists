# Johann Wilhelm Hittorf

**Born:** 27 March 1824

**Died / status:** 28 November 1914

**Fields:** Electrochemistry, Gas discharges, Spectroscopy

![Portrait of Johann Wilhelm Hittorf](../portraits/637fa51a935de827ce15-83fad3f19d.jpg)

For the image’s date, creator, form and attribution, see its source record.

## Biography

Hittorf was a German physicist and chemist who spent his career in Münster after training with Plücker. He made quantitative investigations of ionic transport and low-pressure discharges and helped build experimental physics teaching at his institution.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed methods for measuring ionic transport numbers in electrolytes
- Investigated straight-line cathode-ray propagation and the shadows cast by objects
- Studied gas spectra with Plücker and advanced discharge physics
