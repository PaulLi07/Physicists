# Marie Curie, née Skłodowska

**Born:** 7 November 1867

**Died / status:** 4 July 1934

**Fields:** Radioactivity, Nuclear physics

## Biography

Marie Curie, née Skłodowska was born in Warsaw, Russian Empire (now Poland) and conducted research in radioactivity and nuclear physics. They systematically investigated radioactivity and co-discovered polonium and radium with Pierre Curie. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1903.

## Major contributions to physics

- Systematically investigated radioactivity and co-discovered polonium and radium with Pierre Curie
- Advanced the isolation of radioactive elements and investigation of their physical and chemical properties

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1903
