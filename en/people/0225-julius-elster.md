# Julius Elster

**Born:** 24 December 1854

**Died / status:** 8 April 1920

**Fields:** Photoelectric effects, Atmospheric electricity, Radioactivity

![Julius Elster and Hans Geitel conducting an experiment](../portraits/cf1d3f302cfec4399fdf-854111e37f.jpg)

A historical photograph of Julius Elster and Hans Geitel conducting an experiment, extracted unchanged from Figure 1 of Fricke and Schlegel (2017), credited to Archiv Fricke. This is a two-person photograph rather than a solo headshot.

## Biography

Elster was a German physicist and schoolteacher who collaborated with his childhood friend Hans Geitel in Wolfenbüttel. Their research outside normal teaching duties addressed photoelectricity, atmospheric electricity and radioactivity, demonstrating the scientific reach of carefully organized independent experiments.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed sensitive photoelectric cells and measurements with Geitel
- Studied atmospheric ionization, electrical phenomena and precipitation charge
- Investigated radioactivity and its connection with energy within atoms

## Historical and source notes

- The death date follows Neue Deutsche Biographie: 8 April 1920. English Wikipedia and some derivative references give 6 April.
