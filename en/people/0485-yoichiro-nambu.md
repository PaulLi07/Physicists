# Yoichiro Nambu

**Born:** 18 January 1921

**Died / status:** 5 July 2015

**Fields:** Particle physics, Symmetry breaking

![Portrait of Yoichiro Nambu](../portraits/3a5863ef989ac4cfb39c.jpg)

## Biography

Yoichiro Nambu was born in Tokyo, Japan and conducted research in particle physics and symmetry breaking. Nobel records list Enrico Fermi Institute, University of Chicago as the affiliation at the time of the award. They systematically introduced spontaneous symmetry breaking into elementary-particle theory. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2008.

## Major contributions to physics

- Systematically introduced spontaneous symmetry breaking into elementary-particle theory
- Provided important ideas for understanding low-energy strong-interaction phenomena

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2008
