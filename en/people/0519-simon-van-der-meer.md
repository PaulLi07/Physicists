# Simon van der Meer

**Born:** 24 November 1925

**Died / status:** 4 March 2011

**Fields:** Accelerator physics

## Biography

Simon van der Meer was born in the Hague, the Netherlands and conducted research in accelerator physics. Nobel records list CERN as the affiliation at the time of the award. They invented stochastic cooling. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1984.

## Major contributions to physics

- Invented stochastic cooling
- Made intense antiproton beams and the collider experiments that discovered the W and Z bosons possible

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1984
