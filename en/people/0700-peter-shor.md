# Peter Shor

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

## Biography

An American mathematician and quantum-computing researcher, Shor worked at Bell Laboratories before joining MIT. His algorithms revealed quantum computing's potential impact on cryptography, while his later work advanced error correction.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed polynomial-time quantum factoring and discrete-logarithm algorithms.
- Constructed an early quantum error-correcting code.
- Advanced foundations of fault-tolerant quantum computing.
