# Mildred Dresselhaus

**Born:** 11 November 1930

**Died / status:** 20 February 2017

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Nanophysics

## Biography

An American condensed-matter physicist at MIT, Dresselhaus was among its early women tenured faculty. She used spectroscopy and transport to reveal the electronic properties of carbon materials and advocated women's participation in science.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated graphite, carbon fibres, nanotubes, and low-dimensional carbon electronic structure.
- Developed Raman-spectroscopic characterization of carbon nanomaterials.
- Investigated thermoelectric effects in low-dimensional systems.
