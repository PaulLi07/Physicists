# Ralph Alpher

**Born:** 3 February 1921

**Died / status:** 12 August 2007

**Fields:** Cosmology, Nuclear astrophysics

![Portrait of Ralph Alpher](../portraits/93bf377617d3aa63ed30-cef14ebec9.jpg)

Sourced portrait or photograph; see the image record for provenance and reuse conditions.

## Biography

An American theorist, Alpher studied early-universe nuclear reactions with Gamow during his doctorate and later worked in applied research and universities. His work with Herman anticipated the cosmic microwave background.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated light-element production in the early universe.
- Predicted relic thermal cosmic radiation with Herman.
- Studied cosmic thermal history and elemental abundances.
