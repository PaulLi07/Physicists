# Caroline Herschel

**Born:** 16 March 1750

**Died / status:** 9 January 1848

**Fields:** Observational astronomy, Astronomical catalogues

## Biography

Caroline Herschel was a Hanover-born astronomer who moved to Britain and worked for many years with her brother William on observations, calculations and catalogues. She also independently discovered comets and nebulae and later received the Royal Astronomical Society's gold medal, becoming a major early professional woman astronomer.

## Major contributions to physics

- Independently discovered several comets and observed nebulae.
- Compiled, corrected and extended stellar and nebular catalogues, improving the reliability of observational records.
