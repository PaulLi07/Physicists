# Andrei Sakharov

**Born:** 21 May 1921

**Died / status:** 14 December 1989

**Fields:** Particle physics, Cosmology

## Biography

A Soviet theoretical physicist, Sakharov contributed to thermonuclear research before becoming an advocate of human rights and nuclear disarmament. His scientific interests also included particle physics and cosmology; he received the 1975 Nobel Peace Prize.

## Major contributions to physics

- Formulated conditions for generating a cosmic baryon asymmetry.
- Investigated induced gravity and fundamental interactions.
- Contributed to early Soviet thermonuclear and magnetic-confinement-fusion theory.
