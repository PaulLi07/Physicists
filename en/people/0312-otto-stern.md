# Otto Stern

**Born:** 17 February 1888

**Died / status:** 17 August 1969

**Fields:** Atomic physics, Molecular beams

## Biography

Otto Stern was born in Sorau, Germany (now Zory, Poland) and conducted research in atomic physics and molecular beams. Nobel records list Carnegie Institute of Technology as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed molecular-beam experiments and measured the proton's magnetic moment. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1943.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed molecular-beam experiments and measured the proton's magnetic moment
- Used space-quantization experiments to investigate atomic magnetic moments

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1943
