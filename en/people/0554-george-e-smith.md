# George E. Smith

**Born:** 10 May 1930

**Died / status:** 28 May 2025

**Fields:** Semiconductor physics, Imaging devices

## Biography

George E. Smith was born in White Plains, NY, USA and conducted research in semiconductor physics and imaging devices. Nobel records list Bell Laboratories as the affiliation at the time of the award. They invented the CCD image sensor with Willard Boyle. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2009.

## Major contributions to physics

- Invented the CCD image sensor with Willard Boyle
- Developed a way to convert light into charge signals transferred and read out through a semiconductor device

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2009
