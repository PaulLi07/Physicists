## Biography

A Russian-born theorist, Kallosh worked at the Lebedev Institute before joining Stanford. Her research on supersymmetry, supergravity, and inflation connects particle theory with models of the early universe.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated quantum corrections and ultraviolet properties of supergravity.
- Constructed string-vacuum and cosmological models with collaborators.
- Developed supergravity inflation and alpha-attractor models.
