# Shoichi Sakata

**Born:** 18 January 1911

**Died / status:** 16 October 1970

**Fields:** Particle physics

## Biography

A Japanese theorist at Nagoya, Sakata used experimental regularities to develop models of particle structure. With colleagues, he helped build a postwar particle-theory community whose work influenced quark models and neutrino mixing.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed the Sakata model based on protons, neutrons, and Lambda particles.
- Contributed to neutral-meson and particle-classification theories.
- Developed an early lepton-mixing framework with Maki and Nakagawa.
