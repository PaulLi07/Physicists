# Sergio Ferrara

**Born:** 2 May 1945

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Supergravity, Quantum field theory

## Biography

An Italian theorist associated with Frascati, CERN, and UCLA, Ferrara investigates supersymmetry and gravitation, including extended supergravity, black holes, and unification.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established supergravity with Freedman and van Nieuwenhuizen.
- Developed supersymmetric Yang–Mills theories.
- Investigated extended supergravity and supersymmetric black holes.
