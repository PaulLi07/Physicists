# Amedeo Avogadro

**Born:** 9 August 1776

**Died / status:** 9 July 1856

**Fields:** Molecular physics, Gas physics

## Biography

Avogadro was an Italian scholar initially trained in law who turned to natural science and taught physics in Turin. He distinguished molecular and atomic levels of description and in 1811 proposed a relation between gas volume and particle number under equal conditions. Later reassessment established its importance to molecular science.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed that equal gas volumes at the same temperature and pressure contain equal numbers of molecules.
- Used molecular ideas to interpret combining gas volumes and relative molecular masses, supporting molecular theory and atomic-weight determination.
