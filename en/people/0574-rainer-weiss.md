# Rainer Weiss

**Born:** 29 September 1932

**Died / status:** 25 August 2025

**Fields:** Gravitational waves, Experimental physics, Relativity

## Biography

Rainer Weiss was born in Berlin, Germany and conducted research in gravitational waves, experimental physics and relativity. Nobel records list LIGO/VIRGO Collaboration and Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) as the affiliations at the time of the award. They made decisive contributions to the LIGO detector and the first direct observation of gravitational waves. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2017.

## Major contributions to physics

- Made decisive contributions to the LIGO detector and the first direct observation of gravitational waves
- Opened gravitational-wave astronomy through precision interferometry

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2017
