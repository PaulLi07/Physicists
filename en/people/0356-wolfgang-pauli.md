## Biography

Wolfgang Pauli was born in Vienna, Austria and conducted research in quantum mechanics and particle physics. Nobel records list Princeton University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They formulated the exclusion principle, explaining electronic shells and matter's structure. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1945.

## Major contributions to physics

- Formulated the exclusion principle, explaining electronic shells and matter's structure
- Proposed the neutrino to address energy conservation in beta decay

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1945
