## Biography

An American experimental physicist born in Shanghai, Ye trained with Hall at the University of Colorado and worked at JILA and NIST. His research connects metrology and quantum physics through ultrastable lasers, optical lattice clocks, and ultracold molecules.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed strontium optical lattice clocks and precision optical-frequency measurements.
- Investigated frequency combs and ultrastable lasers.
- Prepared and controlled ultracold molecules and quantum gases.
