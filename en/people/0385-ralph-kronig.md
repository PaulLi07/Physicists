# Ralph Kronig

**Born:** 10 March 1904

**Died / status:** 16 November 1995

**Fields:** Quantum theory, Condensed-matter physics, Optics

## Biography

Kronig was a Dresden-born theoretical physicist trained at Columbia who later worked in Groningen and Delft. He investigated atomic spectra, crystal electrons and linear response. He considered electron spin early but did not establish publication priority; his published work supplied lasting tools for quantum materials and optics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the Kramers–Kronig dispersion relations connecting dispersive and absorptive response
- Developed the Kronig–Penney model of electrons in a periodic potential with William Penney
- Investigated molecular spectra and electron spin during the development of early quantum theory
