## Biography

Aage Niels Bohr was born in Copenhagen, Denmark and conducted research in nuclear physics and nuclear structure. Nobel records list Niels Bohr Institute as the affiliation at the time of the award. They connected collective nuclear motion with individual-particle motion. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1975.

## Major contributions to physics

- Connected collective nuclear motion with individual-particle motion
- Developed a theory of the structure of deformed nuclei

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1975
