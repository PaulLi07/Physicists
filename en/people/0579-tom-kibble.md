# Tom Kibble

**Born:** 23 December 1932

**Died / status:** 2 June 2016

**Fields:** Particle physics, Cosmology

## Biography

Born in Madras, Kibble was a British theorist at Imperial College London. His work connected spontaneous symmetry breaking and gauge-field masses with phase transitions and defects in the early universe.

## Major contributions to physics

- Independently proposed a gauge-theory symmetry-breaking mechanism with Guralnik and Hagen.
- Developed the Kibble mechanism for topological-defect formation.
- Advanced the ideas underlying Kibble–Zurek nonequilibrium scaling.
