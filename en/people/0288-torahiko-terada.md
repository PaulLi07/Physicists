# Torahiko Terada

**Born:** 28 November 1878

**Died / status:** 31 December 1935

**Fields:** X-ray physics, Geophysics, Experimental physics

![Portrait of Torahiko Terada](../portraits/7ca16047d8f82a5d61a0.jpg)

## Biography

Terada was a Japanese physicist and essayist at Tokyo Imperial University. He investigated X-ray diffraction, geophysics and everyday natural phenomena, emphasizing close observation and experimental questions. His writing spread an experimental outlook and interest in complex natural processes.

## Major contributions to physics

- Conducted early crystal X-ray diffraction studies
- Investigated earthquakes, meteorology and complex natural phenomena
- Promoted observation-based physics education and public understanding of science
