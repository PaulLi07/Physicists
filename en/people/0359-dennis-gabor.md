# Dennis Gabor

**Born:** 5 June 1900

**Died / status:** 9 February 1979

**Fields:** Optics, Holography

![Portrait of Dennis Gabor](../portraits/2988f65347aeab75ef3d.jpg)

For the photographer or artist, date and type of image, consult the original image record.

## Biography

Dennis Gabor was born in Budapest, Hungary and conducted research in optics and holography. Nobel records list Imperial College as the affiliation at the time of the award. They invented and developed holography. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1971.

## Major contributions to physics

- Invented and developed holography
- Reconstructed wavefronts by recording both phase and amplitude information

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1971

## Historical and source notes

- The date of death follows Imperial College's archives, the Royal Society catalogue and the Hungarian Academy's memorial: 9 February 1979. Nobel structured data and some institutional pages give 8 February; that discrepancy is retained here.
