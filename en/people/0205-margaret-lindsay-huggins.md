# Margaret Lindsay Huggins

**Born:** 14 August 1848

**Died / status:** 24 March 1915

**Fields:** Astrophysics, Spectroscopy, Scientific photography

## Biography

Margaret Huggins was an Irish-born British astronomer who collaborated for decades with William Huggins at their private observatory. Skilled in photography and observational practice, she contributed to recording, interpreting and publishing stellar and nebular spectra, including ultraviolet observations.

## Major contributions to physics

- Helped develop astronomical spectral photography with William Huggins
- Participated in ultraviolet stellar spectroscopy and studies of celestial emission spectra
- Co-produced spectral atlases that made observations easier to compare
