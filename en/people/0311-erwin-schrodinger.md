**Fields:** Quantum mechanics, Mathematical physics

## Biography

Erwin Schrödinger was born in Vienna, Austria and conducted research in quantum mechanics and mathematical physics. Nobel records list Berlin University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed the Schrödinger equation and wave mechanics. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1933.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the Schrödinger equation and wave mechanics
- Described quantum states and atomic energy levels through the evolution of wavefunctions

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1933
