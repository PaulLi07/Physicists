# Friedrich Hund

**Born:** 4 February 1896

**Died / status:** 31 March 1997

**Fields:** Atomic physics, Molecular physics, Quantum mechanics

![Portrait of Friedrich Hund](../portraits/8b7a825d26ee666e246e-cb24399e04.jpg)

Consult the original image record for the creator, date and form of the image.

## Biography

Hund was a German theoretical physicist who taught in Leipzig, Jena, Frankfurt and Göttingen. He connected atomic and molecular spectra with quantum electronic states and shaped quantum physics through research, textbooks and teaching. He also studied the history of physics later in his career.

## Major contributions to physics

- Formulated Hund's rules for the ordering of atomic electronic energy levels
- Developed quantum descriptions of molecular orbitals and spectra with Mulliken and others
- Made early investigations of quantum tunnelling in molecules
