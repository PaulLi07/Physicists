# Nicholas Metropolis

**Born:** 11 June 1915

**Died / status:** 17 October 1999

**Fields:** Computational physics, Statistical physics

## Biography

An American physicist associated with wartime Los Alamos and early electronic computing, Metropolis helped combine random sampling with computers. His collaborations opened new methods in statistical mechanics and numerical simulation.

## Major contributions to physics

- Coauthored the equilibrium-sampling algorithm now called the Metropolis algorithm.
- Advanced Monte Carlo methods in physics.
- Led early scientific-computing work including the MANIAC computer.
