**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

## Biography

Takaaki Kajita was born in Higashimatsuyama, Japan and conducted research in particle physics and neutrinos. Nobel records list University of Tokyo as the affiliation at the time of the award. They led key analyses of atmospheric neutrinos in Super-Kamiokande. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2015.

## Major contributions to physics

- Led key analyses of atmospheric neutrinos in Super-Kamiokande
- Established oscillation evidence through neutrino deficits varying with propagation distance

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2015
