# Zhou Peiyuan

**Born:** 28 August 1902

**Died / status:** 24 November 1993

**Fields:** Fluid mechanics, General relativity

## Biography

Born in Yixing, Jiangsu, Zhou studied in Chicago, at Caltech, and in Europe before teaching at Tsinghua and Peking University. His research in turbulence and gravitation accompanied a major role in Chinese scientific education.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated Reynolds stresses and their transport equations in turbulent flow.
- Studied gravitational-field solutions in general relativity.
- Developed teaching and research communities in fluid mechanics and theoretical physics.
