# James Watson Cronin

**Born:** 29 September 1931

**Died / status:** 25 August 2016

**Fields:** Particle physics, CP symmetry

## Biography

James Watson Cronin was born in Chicago, IL, USA and conducted research in particle physics and CP symmetry. Nobel records list University of Chicago as the affiliation at the time of the award. They discovered CP violation in neutral-kaon decays. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1980.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered CP violation in neutral-kaon decays
- Showed that some processes violate the combined symmetry of charge conjugation and spatial reflection

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1980
