# Hantaro Nagaoka

**Born:** 19 August 1865

**Died / status:** 11 December 1950

**Fields:** Atomic physics, Magnetism, Spectroscopy

![Portrait of Hantaro Nagaoka](../portraits/a5afd16a0e6942a1b96d.jpg)

## Biography

Nagaoka was a Japanese physicist who studied in Europe and taught at Tokyo Imperial University. He investigated magnetism, spectroscopy and atomic structure, proposing a Saturnian atom before the nuclear model was established and helping build Japan's modern physics institutions.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed a historical atomic model with a massive positive centre and an electron ring
- Investigated magnetostriction, spectra and electromagnetic phenomena
- Helped develop modern physics research and higher education in Japan

## Historical and source notes

- The Saturnian atom is a historical model in the development of atomic structure, not a modern correct picture of the atom.
