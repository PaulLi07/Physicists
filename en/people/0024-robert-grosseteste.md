# Robert Grosseteste

**Born:** c. 1168–1175

**Died / status:** 9 October 1253

**Fields:** History of optics, Natural philosophy

## Biography

Robert Grosseteste was an English medieval scholar associated with Oxford and later bishop of Lincoln. He translated Greek works and studied natural philosophy. He emphasized geometry as a means of understanding nature and linked natural philosophy to mathematical reasoning through studies of light, colour and the rainbow.

## Major contributions to physics

- Tried to explain the structure of nature in terms of light in works including De luce.
- Studied reflection, refraction and the rainbow and emphasized geometrical reasoning in the investigation of nature.

## Historical and source notes

- Birth has been variously estimated around 1168, 1170 or 1175.
