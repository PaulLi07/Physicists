## Biography

Michel Mayor was born in Lausanne, Switzerland and conducted research in astrophysics and exoplanets. Nobel records list University of Geneva as the affiliation at the time of the award. They discovered the exoplanet 51 Pegasi b with Didier Queloz. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2019.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the exoplanet 51 Pegasi b with Didier Queloz
- Advanced high-precision radial-velocity searches for planets orbiting Sun-like stars

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2019
