# Erwin Hahn

**Born:** 9 June 1921

**Died / status:** 20 September 2016

**Fields:** Nuclear magnetic resonance, Quantum coherence, Experimental physics

## Biography

Hahn was an American experimental physicist who investigated nuclear magnetic resonance at Illinois and later taught for decades at Berkeley. His discovery of spin echoes and development of pulsed resonance methods made coherence and relaxation in spin systems accessible to precise experiments and provided important foundations for MRI.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered spin echoes, revealing coherent information recoverable from some apparent dephasing processes
- Developed pulsed nuclear magnetic resonance and free-induction-decay measurements
- Established experimental methods important to magnetic-resonance spectroscopy and later MRI
