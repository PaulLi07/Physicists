# Konstantin Petrzhak

**Born:** 4 September 1907

**Died / status:** 1998

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Experimental physics

## Biography

A Soviet experimental nuclear physicist of Polish origin, Petrzhak graduated from Leningrad University and worked for many years at the Radium Institute. He discovered spontaneous fission of uranium with Georgy Flerov, later studying neutron physics, fission and radioactivity measurements while training nuclear scientists.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered spontaneous fission of uranium with Flerov in 1940 and used underground measurements to test for cosmic-ray effects.
- Made precision measurements of neutron-induced fission cross sections and properties of heavy-nucleus fission.
- Developed radioactivity and neutron-measurement methods and built research groups in fission and neutron physics.

## Historical and source notes

- The day of death is disputed: it is commonly given as 10 October 1998, whereas the Rosatom history portal gives 19 November 1998. No contemporary original record resolving this discrepancy was obtained, so the canonical date retains year precision.
