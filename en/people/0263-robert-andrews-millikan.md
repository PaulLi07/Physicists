# Robert Andrews Millikan

**Born:** 22 March 1868

**Died / status:** 19 December 1953

**Fields:** Atomic physics, Precision measurement

## Biography

Robert Andrews Millikan was born in Morrison, IL, USA and conducted research in atomic physics and precision measurement. Nobel records list California Institute of Technology (Caltech) as the affiliation at the time of the award. They measured the elementary electric charge through oil-drop experiments. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1923.

## Major contributions to physics

- Measured the elementary electric charge through oil-drop experiments
- Experimentally investigated the photoelectric effect and tested Einstein's relation

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1923
