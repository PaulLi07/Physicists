# Leo Szilard

**Born:** 11 February 1898

**Died / status:** 30 May 1964

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Statistical physics

## Biography

A Hungarian-born physicist who worked in Germany and Britain before settling in the United States, Szilard recognized the possibility of nuclear chain reactions and participated in the Chicago reactor project. After the war he advocated international control of nuclear weapons.

## Major contributions to physics

- Conceived a self-sustaining nuclear chain reaction and contributed to reactor research.
- Used a single-molecule engine to analyze measurement, information, and entropy.
- Investigated neutron multiplication and nuclear-energy technology.
