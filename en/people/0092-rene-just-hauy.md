# René Just Haüy

**Born:** 28 February 1743

**Died / status:** 1 or 3 June 1822

**Fields:** Crystallography, Mineral physics

## Biography

Haüy was a French priest and mineralogist who taught and studied collections in Paris. From crystal cleavage and geometry he developed an account of external forms built from regularly arranged small units. His investigations of physical properties made him a founder of geometrical crystallography.

## Major contributions to physics

- Explained crystal forms through structural units and rational geometrical relationships, advancing geometrical crystallography.
- Investigated cleavage, symmetry and electrical and other physical properties of minerals, connecting mineralogy and physics.
