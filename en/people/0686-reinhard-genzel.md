**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

## Biography

Reinhard Genzel was born in Bad Homburg vor der Höhe, Germany and conducted research in astrophysics and Galactic Centre. Nobel records list Max Planck Institute for Extraterrestrial Physics and University of California as the affiliations at the time of the award. They led infrared observations tracking stars near the centre of the Milky Way. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2020.

## Major contributions to physics

- Led infrared observations tracking stars near the centre of the Milky Way
- Provided crucial evidence for a supermassive compact object at the Galactic Centre

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2020
