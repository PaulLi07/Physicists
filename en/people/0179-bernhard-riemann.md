# Bernhard Riemann

**Born:** 17 September 1826

**Died / status:** 20 July 1866

**Fields:** Mathematical physics, Geometry and gravitation

![Portrait of Bernhard Riemann](../portraits/49c360e7f7e47a6a1b81.jpeg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Riemann was a German mathematician who studied and worked mainly in Göttingen and died young from illness. His work in analysis, geometry and complex functions introduced the description of space through an intrinsic metric, later fundamental to general relativity and modern field theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Founded Riemannian geometry around intrinsic metrics and curvature
- Developed Riemann surfaces and important analytical tools
- Studied physical problems involving fluids and waves, while providing mathematics later used in gravitation

## Historical and source notes

- Riemann did not formulate general relativity.
- This entry records the foundational role of his mathematics in later physics.
