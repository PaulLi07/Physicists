# Johannes Diderik van der Waals

**Born:** 23 November 1837

**Died / status:** 8 March 1923

**Fields:** Thermodynamics, Statistical physics

## Biography

Johannes Diderik van der Waals was born in Leiden, the Netherlands and conducted research in thermodynamics and statistical physics. Nobel records list Amsterdam University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed an equation of state accounting for molecular size and intermolecular forces. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1910.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed an equation of state accounting for molecular size and intermolecular forces
- Explained the continuity of gaseous and liquid states and critical behaviour

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1910
