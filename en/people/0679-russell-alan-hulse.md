## Biography

Russell A. Hulse was born in New York, NY, USA and conducted research in astrophysics and gravitation. Nobel records list Princeton University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They discovered and investigated a binary pulsar system. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1993.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered and investigated a binary pulsar system
- Its orbital decay supplied indirect evidence consistent with gravitational-wave emission

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1993
