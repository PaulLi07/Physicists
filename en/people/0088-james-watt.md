# James Watt

**Born:** 30 January 1736

**Died / status:** 25 August 1819

**Fields:** Heat-engine engineering, Applied thermal physics

![James Watt — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/ea3d76712e6305fd52e7.jpg)

## Biography

Watt was a Scottish instrument maker and engineer who began improving steam engines after repairing a teaching model near Glasgow University. In partnership with Matthew Boulton he developed and marketed more efficient steam power, bringing together the study of heat, precision manufacture and industrial engineering.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the separate condenser, greatly reducing heat lost through repeatedly cooling the working cylinder.
- Improved steam-engine transmission, regulation and use of power, advancing practical heat-engine engineering.
