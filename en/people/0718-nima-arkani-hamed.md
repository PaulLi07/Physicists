## Biography

An American-born theorist of Iranian ancestry, Arkani-Hamed worked at Berkeley and Harvard before joining the Institute for Advanced Study. He investigates physics beyond the standard model and geometric structures in scattering amplitudes.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed large extra dimensions with Dimopoulos and Dvali.
- Contributed to little-Higgs and split-supersymmetry models.
- Developed geometric scattering-amplitude methods and the amplituhedron programme.
