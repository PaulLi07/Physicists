# Marshall Rosenbluth

**Born:** 5 February 1927

**Died / status:** 28 September 2003

**Fields:** Plasma physics, Computational physics

## Biography

An American theorist who worked at Los Alamos, General Atomics, and several universities, Rosenbluth became a major figure in fusion and plasma stability. He also helped develop early Monte Carlo sampling methods.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated plasma instabilities, transport, and magnetic confinement.
- Contributed to the sampling method known as the Metropolis algorithm.
- Developed Rosenbluth potentials for collision and transport theory.
