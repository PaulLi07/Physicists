# Al-Khazini

**Born:** c. 1077

**Died / status:** c. 1130

**Fields:** Hydrostatics, Metrology, Mechanics

![Related historical document for Al-Khazini; not a portrait](../portraits/13dae8a543f80e31d4d6-0a43b1f5a3.jpg)

A balance diagram from a manuscript of the Book of the Balance of Wisdom; this is a historical document illustration, not a portrait.

## Biography

Al-Khazini was an astronomer and investigator of mechanics active in twelfth-century Merv under the patronage of the Seljuq ruler Sanjar. His birth and death are not securely documented. His Book of the Balance of Wisdom systematically examined weighing instruments, specific gravities and hydrostatic equilibrium.

## Major contributions to physics

- Collected and developed methods of hydrostatic weighing and measurements of specific gravity.
- Designed and analyzed precision compound balances, considering equilibrium, centres of gravity and instrumental error.

## Historical and source notes

- Exact birth and death dates are unknown; approximately 1077–1130 is a common reconstruction, while documented activity is principally around 1115–1130.
- No reliable contemporary likeness has been identified.
