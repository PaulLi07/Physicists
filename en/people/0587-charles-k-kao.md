# Charles Kuen Kao

**Born:** 4 November 1933

**Died / status:** 23 September 2018

**Fields:** Optics, Optical-fibre communication

![Portrait of Charles Kuen Kao](../portraits/54422eeb5d383361ffd0.jpg)

For the photographer or artist, date and type of image, consult the original image record.

## Biography

Charles Kuen Kao was born in Shanghai, China and conducted research in optics and optical-fibre communication. Nobel records list Standard Telecommunication Laboratories and Chinese University of Hong Kong as the affiliations at the time of the award. They showed that high-purity glass could greatly reduce optical-fibre attenuation. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2009.

## Major contributions to physics

- Showed that high-purity glass could greatly reduce optical-fibre attenuation
- Established a physical and materials-research path to long-distance optical communication

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2009
