# James Bradley

**Born:** March 1693

**Died / status:** 13 July 1762

**Fields:** Astrometry, Optics, Earth's motion

## Biography

Bradley was an English astronomer who held Oxford's Savilian chair of astronomy and later became Astronomer Royal. While searching for annual stellar parallax, his sustained precision observations revealed other systematic effects. These discoveries improved understanding of Earth's motion and the measurement of celestial positions.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered stellar aberration, connecting Earth's orbital motion with the finite speed of light.
- Identified nutation of Earth's axis and improved the precision of positional astronomy.
