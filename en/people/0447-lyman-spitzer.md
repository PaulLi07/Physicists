# Lyman Spitzer

**Born:** 26 June 1914

**Died / status:** 31 March 1997

**Fields:** Plasma physics, Astrophysics

![Portrait of Lyman Spitzer](../portraits/3a7c23a5073bc04d0d74-3f862d2239.jpg)

## Biography

An American astrophysicist at Princeton, Spitzer studied interstellar matter, stellar dynamics, and fusion plasmas. He was an early advocate of observing the universe with space telescopes.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed the stellarator for magnetic-confinement fusion.
- Investigated interstellar matter and plasma transport.
- Advocated space telescopes and helped develop the later Hubble programme.
