# Laura Bassi

**Born:** 29 October 1711

**Died / status:** 20 February 1778

**Fields:** Experimental physics, Electrical science, Fluid mechanics

## Biography

Bassi was an Italian physicist who received a degree and a paid university teaching position in Bologna in 1732 and later held a chair in experimental physics. With her husband Veratti she maintained a home laboratory, investigated electricity, fluids and optics and taught Newtonian physics despite restrictions on women's education.

## Major contributions to physics

- Advanced Newtonian physics in Italy through teaching, experiments and scholarly exchange.
- Investigated electricity, fluid mechanics and optics and developed laboratory teaching and research in her home laboratory.
