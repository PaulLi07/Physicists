# Bruno Pontecorvo

**Born:** 22 August 1913

**Died / status:** 24 September 1993

**Fields:** Neutrino physics, Nuclear physics

## Biography

An Italian-born physicist in Fermi's Rome group, Pontecorvo worked in France, North America, and Britain before moving to the Soviet Union in 1950. His combination of experimental skill and theoretical insight made him a pioneer of neutrino physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed radiochemical neutrino detection through chlorine-to-argon conversion.
- Proposed oscillations involving neutrino and antineutrino states and later different neutrino types.
- Investigated lepton universality and neutrino species.
