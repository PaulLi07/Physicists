## Biography

A Soviet physicist, Budker founded and directed the Institute of Nuclear Physics in Novosibirsk. Combining theoretical ideas with machine development, he pioneered work on electron–positron storage rings, beam control, and controlled fusion.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed and developed electron cooling of heavy-particle beams.
- Advanced electron–positron colliders and storage rings.
- Investigated plasma confinement and collective acceleration.
