# Paul Corkum

**Born:** 30 October 1943

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Attosecond physics, Laser physics

## Biography

A Canadian experimental and theoretical laser physicist at the National Research Council and the University of Ottawa, Corkum connected strong-field ionization with electron recollision, establishing a central picture of high-harmonic generation and attosecond pulses.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed the three-step recollision model of strong-field harmonic generation.
- Developed attosecond-pulse and molecular-imaging methods.
- Investigated ultrafast laser control of electron motion.
