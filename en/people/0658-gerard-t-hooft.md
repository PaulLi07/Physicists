## Biography

Gerardus 't Hooft was born in Den Helder, the Netherlands and conducted research in particle physics and quantum field theory. Nobel records list Utrecht University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They established and developed the renormalizable treatment of non-Abelian gauge theories. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1999.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established and developed the renormalizable treatment of non-Abelian gauge theories
- Enabled precise calculations and experimental tests of electroweak theory

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1999
