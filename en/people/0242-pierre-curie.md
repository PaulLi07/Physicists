# Pierre Curie

**Born:** 15 May 1859

**Died / status:** 19 April 1906

**Fields:** Radioactivity, Magnetism, Piezoelectricity

## Biography

Pierre Curie was born in Paris, France and conducted research in radioactivity, magnetism and piezoelectricity. Nobel records list École municipale de physique et de chimie industrielles (Municipal School of Industrial Physics and Chemistry) as the affiliation at the time of the award. They investigated radioactivity with Marie Curie and co-discovered polonium and radium. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1903.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated radioactivity with Marie Curie and co-discovered polonium and radium
- Established Curie's law of magnetism and co-discovered piezoelectricity with Jacques Curie

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1903
