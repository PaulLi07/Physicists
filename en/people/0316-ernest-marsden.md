# Ernest Marsden

**Born:** 19 February 1889

**Died / status:** 15 December 1970

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Experimental physics

## Biography

Born in England, Marsden became a New Zealand experimental physicist after training with Rutherford's group in Manchester. He moved to Wellington to teach in 1915 and later led New Zealand's Department of Scientific and Industrial Research, developing experimental physics, radar and national research institutions.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered rare large-angle backscattering of alpha particles from thin metal foils with Geiger, helping establish the nuclear atom.
- Contributed to early experiments on radioactivity, alpha particles and atomic structure.
- Organized New Zealand's research system and promoted domestic radar research during the Second World War.
