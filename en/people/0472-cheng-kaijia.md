# Cheng Kaijia

**Born:** 3 August 1918

**Died / status:** 17 November 2018

**Fields:** Solid-state physics, Nuclear-test physics

## Biography

Born in Wujiang, Jiangsu, Cheng graduated from Zhejiang University and studied with Born in Edinburgh before returning to China in 1950. His early solid-state research was followed by leadership of physical measurements and experimental techniques for nuclear testing.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated solid-state theory and wrote textbooks.
- Led nuclear-test physics and diagnostic research in China.
- Established measurement methods and experimental systems for nuclear-explosion effects.
