## Biography

A Pakistan-born American experimental physicist with an MIT doctorate, Mavalvala participated in LIGO and later returned to MIT as a faculty member. Her work combines precision interferometry, quantum optics, and gravitational-wave detection.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed precision laser interferometry for gravitational-wave detectors.
- Investigated squeezed light and quantum-noise reduction.
- Advanced quantum measurement and control of macroscopic optomechanical systems.
