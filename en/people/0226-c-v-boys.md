# C. V. Boys

**Born:** 15 March 1855

**Died / status:** 30 March 1944

**Fields:** Precision measurement, Gravitational experiments, Surface physics

![Portrait of C. V. Boys](../portraits/07c57f98d43a28b469c1.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Boys was a British experimental physicist noted for sensitive instruments and precision measurement. His extremely fine quartz-fibre suspensions made tiny torques accessible to experiment. He also investigated soap films, radiation measurement and high-speed photography.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed quartz-fibre suspensions for highly sensitive torsion instruments
- Improved Cavendish-type measurements of the gravitational constant
- Developed radiation-measuring and high-speed photographic apparatus and studied surface-tension phenomena
