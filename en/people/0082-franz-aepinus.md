# Franz Aepinus

**Born:** 13 December 1724

**Died / status:** 10 August 1802

**Fields:** Electrostatics, Magnetism, Pyroelectricity

## Biography

Aepinus was a German-born physicist who worked in Berlin before serving for many years at the St Petersburg academy. He investigated electricity and magnetism within a common mathematical framework and studied the electrical effects of heating tourmaline, publishing an influential theoretical work in 1759.

## Major contributions to physics

- Used a mathematical one-fluid electrical theory to describe phenomena including electrostatic induction.
- Investigated pyroelectricity in tourmaline and sought common analytical treatments of electrical and magnetic interactions.

## Historical and source notes

- His electrical-fluid model is historical and must not be equated with modern electron or electromagnetic-field theories.
