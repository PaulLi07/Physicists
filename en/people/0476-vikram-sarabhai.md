# Vikram Sarabhai

**Born:** 12 August 1919

**Died / status:** 30 December 1971

**Fields:** Cosmic rays, Space physics

## Biography

An Indian physicist educated at Cambridge, Sarabhai established the Physical Research Laboratory in Ahmedabad after returning to India. His interests developed from cosmic-ray experiments to space science and satellite applications, making him a leading organizer of India's early space programme.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated tropical cosmic-ray variations and their geomagnetic connections.
- Built research facilities for cosmic rays and space physics.
- Organized early Indian space science and satellite-application research.
