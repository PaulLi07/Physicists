## Biography

An American theorist who studied and worked at Harvard, Princeton, and MIT, Randall is based at Harvard. She investigates extra dimensions, dark matter, and connections between particle physics and cosmology.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed warped-extra-dimension models with Raman Sundrum.
- Investigated the hierarchy problem, localized gravity, and testable new physics.
- Explored dark-matter and early-universe models.
