# Bruno Touschek

**Born:** 3 February 1921

**Died / status:** 25 May 1978

**Fields:** Accelerator physics

## Biography

An Austrian-born physicist, Touschek survived Nazi persecution and later worked in Britain and Italy. He combined particle theory with accelerator development and played a central role in establishing electron–positron storage-ring collisions at Frascati.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed and helped build AdA, an early electron–positron collider.
- Investigated intrabeam scattering now called the Touschek effect.
- Investigated collider-beam dynamics and radiative corrections.
