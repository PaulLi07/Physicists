# Hu Ning

**Born:** 11 February 1916

**Died / status:** 26 December 1997

**Fields:** Quantum field theory, Particle physics

![Portrait of Hu Ning](../portraits/1f322429d1ce9d3efa97-2469d70318.jpg)

Sourced portrait or photograph; see the image record for provenance and reuse conditions.

## Biography

Born in Suqian, Hu worked at Caltech, Princeton, Dublin, and Copenhagen before teaching at Peking University. He investigated nuclear forces, scattering matrices, and hadron structure and trained Chinese theorists.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated meson-mediated nuclear forces and relativistic field theory.
- Developed a hadron substructure model with Zhu Hongyuan and collaborators.
- Wrote electrodynamics and field-theory texts and built theoretical-physics research.
