# Hedwig Kohn

**Born:** 5 April 1887

**Died / status:** 26 November 1964

**Fields:** Spectroscopy, Experimental physics

![Portrait of Hedwig Kohn](../portraits/7e2da9f76ce2d0668287.jpg)

## Biography

A German-born experimental physicist, Kohn was among the few women to obtain the German university teaching qualification in physics before the Second World War. Driven from Germany by Nazi persecution, she taught and conducted research at American women's colleges and Duke University.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed quantitative measurements of flame radiation and spectral intensities.
- Used spectroscopy to investigate atomic absorption and emission.
- Wrote substantial reference treatments of radiation measurement.
