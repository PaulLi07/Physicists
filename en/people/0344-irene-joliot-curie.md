# Irène Joliot-Curie

**Born:** 12 September 1897

**Died / status:** 17 March 1956

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Radioactivity

## Biography

A French physical scientist and daughter of Marie and Pierre Curie, Joliot-Curie worked at the Radium Institute in Paris. Her nuclear-reaction research with Frédéric Joliot-Curie led to their joint 1935 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for artificial radioactivity.

## Major contributions to physics

- Co-discovered artificial radioactivity with Frédéric Joliot-Curie.
- Studied alpha-induced reactions in light nuclei and positron-emitting activity.
- Advanced radioisotope production and nuclear research in France.
