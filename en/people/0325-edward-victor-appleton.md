# Sir Edward Victor Appleton

**Born:** 6 September 1892

**Died / status:** 21 April 1965

**Fields:** Space physics, Ionospheric physics

![Portrait of Sir Edward Victor Appleton](../portraits/59c2a0d053123724148d.jpg)

## Biography

Sir Edward Victor Appleton was born in Bradford, United Kingdom and conducted research in space physics and ionospheric physics. Nobel records list Department of Scientific and Industrial Research as the affiliation at the time of the award. They used radio experiments to investigate the ionosphere. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1947.

## Major contributions to physics

- Used radio experiments to investigate the ionosphere
- Identified high-altitude ionized regions responsible for reflecting radio waves

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1947
