**Born:** c. 1706–1707

**Died / status:** 20 July 1751

**Fields:** Ballistics, Experimental mechanics

## Biography

Robins was an English mathematician and engineer from a Quaker family who studied Newtonian mathematics, gunnery and air resistance before working in India. His New Principles of Gunnery of 1742 brought experimental velocity measurements into ballistics, changing approaches based mainly on practical rules.

## Major contributions to physics

- Invented the ballistic pendulum to measure projectile speed through momentum transfer.
- Investigated air resistance on fast projectiles, combining experimental measurement and mathematics in ballistics.

## Historical and source notes

- Birth is often given as 1707, while MacTutor cites Quaker records for 16 April 1706; the approximate interval 1706–1707 is retained.
