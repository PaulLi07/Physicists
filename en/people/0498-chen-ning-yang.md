# Chen Ning Yang

**Born:** 1 October 1922

**Died / status:** 18 October 2025

**Fields:** Particle physics, Gauge theory, Statistical physics

## Biography

Chen Ning Yang was born in Hofei, Anhwei, China and conducted research in particle physics, gauge theory and statistical physics. Nobel records list Institute for Advanced Study as the affiliation at the time of the award. They proposed tests of parity conservation in weak interactions with Tsung-Dao Lee. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1957.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed tests of parity conservation in weak interactions with Tsung-Dao Lee
- Developed non-Abelian gauge theory with Robert Mills and made important contributions to statistical physics

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1957

## Historical and source notes

- Two dates occur in formal records: Tsinghua University's 2025 biographical announcement gives 1 October 1922, while Nobel records and his 1963 naturalization application give 22 September. This archive follows Tsinghua's later announcement and explicitly retains the alternative; it does not assume an unverified calendar conversion.
