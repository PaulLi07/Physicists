# Riccardo Giacconi

**Born:** 6 October 1931

**Died / status:** 9 December 2018

**Fields:** X-ray astronomy, Astrophysics

![Portrait of Riccardo Giacconi](../portraits/ceac011b91f40b9ae58b.jpg)

For the photographer or artist, date and type of image, consult the original image record.

## Biography

Riccardo Giacconi was born in Genoa, Italy and conducted research in X-ray astronomy and astrophysics. Nobel records list Associated Universities Inc. as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed space-based X-ray detection and discovered early cosmic X-ray sources. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2002.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed space-based X-ray detection and discovered early cosmic X-ray sources
- Helped establish X-ray astronomy as a major field for investigating high-energy celestial objects

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2002

## Historical and source notes

- The date of death is corrected to 9 December 2018, as recorded by ESO, ESA and the US National Academy of Sciences. The 16 December date in the original Nobel structured data is not used.
