# Charles Glover Barkla

**Born:** 7 June 1877

## Biography

Charles Glover Barkla was born in Widnes, United Kingdom and conducted research in X-rays and atomic physics. Nobel records list Edinburgh University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They investigated characteristic X-radiation from elements. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1917.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated characteristic X-radiation from elements
- Established that elements have identifiable characteristic X-ray spectra

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1917
