# Emmy Noether

**Born:** 23 March 1882

**Died / status:** 14 April 1935

**Fields:** Mathematical physics, Symmetry

## Biography

A German mathematician who worked with Hilbert and Klein in Göttingen, Noether faced persistent restrictions on women's academic employment. After the Nazi takeover she taught at Bryn Mawr College. Her work connected abstract mathematical structures with the symmetries underlying modern physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proved Noether's first theorem connecting continuous symmetries with conservation laws.
- Established a second theorem for symmetries involving arbitrary functions, foundational to gauge theories.
