# Robert Laurence Mills

**Born:** 15 April 1927

**Died / status:** 27 October 1999

**Fields:** Quantum field theory, Gauge theory, Many-body theory

## Biography

Mills was an American theoretical physicist educated at Columbia and Cambridge. He collaborated with Chen Ning Yang at Brookhaven and later taught for many years at Ohio State. His research concerned quantum field theory, alloys and many-body systems; the non-Abelian gauge theory published with Yang in 1954 became foundational to modern particle physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established non-Abelian gauge-field theory with Chen Ning Yang, now called Yang–Mills theory
- Investigated propagators and quantum methods for many-body systems
- Studied alloy theory and advanced modern physical methods through books and teaching
