# William Robert Grove

**Born:** 11 July 1811

**Died / status:** 1 August 1896

**Fields:** Electrochemistry, Energy conversion, Experimental physics

## Biography

Grove was a British lawyer and experimental scientist who combined a legal career with electrochemical research and teaching in London. His work on cells, electrolysis and the connections between natural forces helped shape early ideas about the transformation and conservation of energy.

## Major contributions to physics

- Constructed an early gas battery, a forerunner of the fuel cell
- Promoted the interconversion and connectedness of heat, light, electricity, magnetism and mechanical action
- Improved electrochemical cells used in electrical experiments
