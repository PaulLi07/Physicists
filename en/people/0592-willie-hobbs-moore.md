# Willie Hobbs Moore

**Born:** 23 May 1934

**Died / status:** 14 March 1994

**Fields:** Molecular spectroscopy, Experimental physics

![Portrait of Willie Hobbs Moore](../portraits/c949184e8d0a23e88a0f-02d1d3c47b.jpg)

Sourced portrait or photograph; see the image record for provenance and reuse conditions.

## Biography

An American experimental physicist, Moore earned her Michigan doctorate in 1972 and became the first Black American woman with a physics PhD. She studied molecular spectra before working in industrial engineering and quality systems.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated molecular vibrational spectra experimentally.
- Applied spectroscopy to molecular-structure analysis.
- Applied physics training to industrial engineering and technical management.
