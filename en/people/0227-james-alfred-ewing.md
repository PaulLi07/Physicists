# James Alfred Ewing

**Born:** 27 March 1855

**Died / status:** 7 January 1935

**Fields:** Magnetism, Geophysics, Engineering physics

![Portrait of James Alfred Ewing](../portraits/7e95284d8affd11c441d.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Ewing was a Scottish engineer and physicist who worked in Japan, Dundee, Cambridge and Edinburgh. His research on magnetic materials, seismic instruments and mechanical engineering joined laboratory physics with practical measurement and engineering education.

## Major contributions to physics

- Systematically investigated magnetic hysteresis in ferromagnets
- Studied models of magnetic materials and their energy losses
- Helped develop early seismographs and engineering-physics education
