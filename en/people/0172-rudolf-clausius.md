# Rudolf Clausius

**Born:** 2 January 1822

**Died / status:** 24 August 1888

**Fields:** Thermodynamics, Statistical physics, Kinetic theory of gases

![Portrait of Rudolf Clausius](../portraits/760d65f5a0f64c37a6a7.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Clausius was a German theoretical physicist who taught in Berlin, Zürich, Würzburg and Bonn. Re-examining Carnot's heat-engine theory, he distinguished energy conservation from the direction of thermal processes and introduced entropy, becoming a principal founder of thermodynamics and kinetic theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Formulated a major statement of the second law of thermodynamics
- Introduced entropy and its mathematical definition
- Developed gas kinetic theory, the mean-free-path concept and the virial theorem
