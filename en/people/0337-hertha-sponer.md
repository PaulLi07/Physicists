# Hertha Sponer

**Born:** 1 September 1895

**Died / status:** 17 February 1968

**Fields:** Molecular spectroscopy, Chemical physics

## Biography

A German-born experimental physicist, Sponer worked with Franck in Göttingen before leaving Nazi Germany and becoming the first woman on Duke University's physics faculty. She applied quantum mechanics to molecular spectra and trained generations of spectroscopists.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated electronic and vibrational spectra of diatomic and polyatomic molecules.
- Developed methods for extracting molecular dissociation energies from spectra.
- Wrote influential molecular-spectroscopy texts and advanced applications of quantum chemistry.

## Historical and source notes

- The English Wikipedia article gives 27 February 1968, while Neue Deutsche Biographie and German historical sources give 17 February. The archive adopts 17 February 1968 from the specialist biography and preserves this source discrepancy.
