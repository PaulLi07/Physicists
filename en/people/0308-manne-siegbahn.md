# Karl Manne Georg Siegbahn

**Born:** 3 December 1886

**Died / status:** 24 September 1978

**Fields:** X-rays, Spectroscopy

## Biography

Karl Manne Georg Siegbahn was born in Örebro, Sweden and conducted research in X-rays and spectroscopy. Nobel records list Uppsala University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed high-precision methods of X-ray spectroscopy. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1924.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed high-precision methods of X-ray spectroscopy
- Used accurate spectral measurements to investigate atomic structure

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1924

## Historical and source notes

- The Swedish National Archives' Svenskt biografiskt lexikon records his death on 24 September 1978. Some Nobel and Royal Society records give 26 September. This archive adopts 24 September while preserving the discrepancy.
