# George Biddell Airy

**Born:** 27 July 1801

**Died / status:** 2 January 1892

**Fields:** Optics, Elasticity, Geophysics

![Portrait of George Biddell Airy](../portraits/ca02f6137a1e38b2e0ed.jpg)

## Biography

Airy was a British astronomer and mathematical physicist. After teaching at Cambridge, he served as Astronomer Royal and directed the Greenwich Observatory. Alongside precision astronomy, he investigated optics, elasticity and geophysics, helping to standardize nineteenth-century observational practice.

## Major contributions to physics

- Calculated the diffraction pattern of a circular aperture, including the Airy disk
- Introduced the Airy stress function for two-dimensional elasticity
- Proposed an isostatic model of the crust and used gravity measurements to investigate Earth's density
