# Pierre Prévost

**Born:** 3 March 1751

**Died / status:** 8 April 1839

**Fields:** Thermal radiation, History of thermal physics

## Biography

Prévost was a Genevan philosopher and physicist trained initially in law who taught philosophy in Berlin and Geneva before becoming a professor of general physics. He investigated magnetism and heat and in 1791 proposed explaining thermal equilibrium through the continuing emission and absorption of radiation.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed the exchange theory of thermal radiation, treating equilibrium as a dynamic balance of emission and absorption.
- Opposed treating cold as a separately radiated substance, advancing concepts used in the study of thermal radiation.
