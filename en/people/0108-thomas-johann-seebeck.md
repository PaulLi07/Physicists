# Thomas Johann Seebeck

**Born:** 9 April 1770

**Died / status:** 10 December 1831

**Fields:** Thermoelectricity, Electromagnetism, Thermometry

![Thomas Johann Seebeck — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/3d899a35268cad54a844.jpg)

## Biography

Seebeck was a Baltic German experimental investigator with medical training who studied colours, magnetism and temperature. In circuits of dissimilar metals he observed a magnetic-needle deflection produced by a temperature difference. He initially interpreted the phenomenon in thermomagnetic terms.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the temperature-driven electrical effect in a circuit of dissimilar conductors now called the Seebeck effect.
- Opened experimental research on thermoelectric conversion, providing a basis for thermocouples and thermoelectric devices.
