# Christian Doppler

**Born:** 29 November 1803

**Died / status:** 17 March 1853

**Fields:** Wave physics, Acoustics, Optics

![Portrait of Christian Doppler](../portraits/e7e5227f7bcf823ff326.jpg)

## Biography

Doppler was an Austrian physicist and mathematician who taught in Prague, Banská Štiavnica and Vienna. He considered how motion changes the waves received by an observer, introducing a frequency-shift principle that later became central to acoustics, optics and astronomy.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed the change in observed wave frequency caused by relative motion of source and observer
- Applied the idea of wave-frequency shifts to the study of starlight
- Provided a theoretical basis for later spectroscopic and other velocity measurements
