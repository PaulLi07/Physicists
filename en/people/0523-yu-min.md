# Yu Min

**Born:** 16 August 1926

**Died / status:** 16 January 2019

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Theoretical physics

## Biography

Born in Tianjin, Yu graduated from Peking University and joined the Institute of Modern Physics. He investigated nuclear theory and worked under extensive secrecy on the principles and design of China's hydrogen bomb.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated nuclear structure and light-nuclear reaction mechanisms.
- Played a central role in developing China's hydrogen-bomb principles and theoretical scheme.
- Advanced nuclear-weapons calculations and physical design.
