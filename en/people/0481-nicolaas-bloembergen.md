# Nicolaas Bloembergen

**Born:** 11 March 1920

**Died / status:** 5 September 2017

**Fields:** Nonlinear optics, Laser spectroscopy

## Biography

Nicolaas Bloembergen was born in Dordrecht, the Netherlands and conducted research in nonlinear optics and laser spectroscopy. Nobel records list Harvard University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed nonlinear optics and methods of laser spectroscopy. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1981.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed nonlinear optics and methods of laser spectroscopy
- Investigated nonlinear interactions between intense light fields and matter

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1981
