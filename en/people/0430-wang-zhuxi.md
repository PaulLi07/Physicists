# Wang Zhuxi

**Born:** 7 June 1911

**Died / status:** 30 January 1983

**Fields:** Statistical physics, Theoretical physics

## Biography

Born in Gong'an, Hubei, Wang graduated from Tsinghua, earned his doctorate at Cambridge, and taught at Tsinghua and Peking University. His statistical-physics research, textbooks, and teaching influenced Chinese theoretical physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated statistical physics, cooperative phenomena, and phase transitions.
- Wrote influential texts on thermodynamics, statistical physics, and special functions.
- Built theoretical-physics teaching and research in China.
