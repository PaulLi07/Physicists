# Igor Kurchatov

**Born:** 12 January 1903

**Died / status:** 7 February 1960

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Nuclear energy

## Biography

A Soviet experimental physicist, Kurchatov initially studied dielectrics and nuclei before leading the Soviet nuclear programme during and after the war. He organized reactor, weapons, and nuclear-energy research and helped establish its scientific institutions.

## Major contributions to physics

- Organized the construction of early Soviet nuclear reactors.
- Led research on fission, chain reactions, and nuclear-energy technology.
- Built institutions for nuclear power and controlled-fusion research.
