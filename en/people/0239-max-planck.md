## Biography

Max Karl Ernst Ludwig Planck was born in Kiel, Schleswig (now Germany) and conducted research in quantum theory and thermal radiation. Nobel records list Berlin University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They introduced energy quantization and formulated the black-body radiation law. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1918.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced energy quantization and formulated the black-body radiation law
- Introduced Planck's constant and helped initiate quantum physics

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1918
