# Oliver Lodge

**Born:** 12 June 1851

**Died / status:** 22 August 1940

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Radio physics, Experimental physics

![Portrait of Oliver Lodge](../portraits/3a26e8279e4df13ce088.jpg)

## Biography

Lodge was a British physicist who taught in Liverpool and became the first principal of the University of Birmingham. He investigated electromagnetic waves and radio reception after Hertz's experiments, while also remaining publicly associated with ether theories and psychical research.

## Major contributions to physics

- Demonstrated electromagnetic waves and improved radio detection and reception
- Developed methods of tuned radio communication
- Investigated electromagnetic propagation and moving-media tests relevant to ether theories
