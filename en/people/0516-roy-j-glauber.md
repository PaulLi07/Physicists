# Roy J. Glauber

**Born:** 1 September 1925

**Died / status:** 26 December 2018

**Fields:** Quantum optics

## Biography

Roy J. Glauber was born in New York, NY, USA and conducted research in quantum optics. Nobel records list Harvard University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed the quantum theory of optical coherence. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2005.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the quantum theory of optical coherence
- Used quantum correlation functions to describe photon statistics and coherence

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2005
