# Johann Wilhelm Ritter

**Born:** 16 December 1776

**Died / status:** 23 January 1810

**Fields:** Ultraviolet optics, Electrochemistry

## Biography

Ritter was a German experimental investigator trained initially in pharmacy who worked on electrochemistry and optics in Jena and elsewhere and later joined the Bavarian academy. He used chemical reactions to detect radiation beyond visible violet and studied chemical and physiological effects of electricity.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered chemically active invisible radiation beyond the violet end of the solar spectrum, now called ultraviolet radiation.
- Investigated electrolysis, polarization and electrical storage, advancing early electrochemistry.
