# Ptolemy

**Born:** c. 85–100 CE

**Died / status:** c. 165–170 CE

**Fields:** Astronomy, Geometrical optics

## Biography

Ptolemy was an astronomer, geographer and author on optics active in Alexandria under Roman rule. His Almagest systematized geocentric astronomy, and its models were used for centuries to calculate celestial positions. His Optics preserved quantitative investigations of vision, reflection and refraction.

## Major contributions to physics

- Organized mathematical models of planetary positions using devices such as epicycles and eccentric circles.
- Investigated reflection and refraction with experimental measurements, advancing ancient geometrical optics.
