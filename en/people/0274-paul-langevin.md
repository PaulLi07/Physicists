# Paul Langevin

**Born:** 23 January 1872

**Died / status:** 19 December 1946

**Fields:** Statistical physics, Magnetism, Ultrasonics

![Portrait of Paul Langevin](../portraits/6522a2932306c6a951d4.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Langevin was a French physicist who worked in Paris and spent time at Cambridge's Cavendish Laboratory. He studied magnetism, Brownian motion and relativity, developed ultrasonic detection during the First World War, and played a prominent role in scientific education.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed electron-based theories of paramagnetism and diamagnetism
- Introduced a stochastic equation of motion for Brownian particles
- Applied piezoelectricity to underwater ultrasonic detection and promoted relativity
