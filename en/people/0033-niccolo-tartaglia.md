# Niccolò Tartaglia

**Born:** c. 1499–1500

**Died / status:** 13 December 1557

**Fields:** History of ballistics, Applied mechanics

![Niccolò Tartaglia — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/b4792e66cc83e1162710.jpg)

## Biography

Tartaglia was an Italian Renaissance mathematician who was injured during warfare in childhood, learned largely independently and earned his living by teaching. Alongside his work on cubic equations, he applied mathematics to gunnery, surveying and military technology. His Nova scientia was an important early work on ballistics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Brought projectile motion and gunnery into systematic mathematical investigation.
- Examined relationships between firing angle and range, helping develop quantitative approaches to artillery.

- Sources place his birth in 1499 or 1500; no exact day is established.
