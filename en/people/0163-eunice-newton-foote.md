# Eunice Newton Foote

**Born:** 17 July 1819

**Died / status:** 30 September 1888

**Fields:** Atmospheric physics, Thermal physics, Experimental physics

## Biography

Foote was an American experimental researcher, inventor and women's-rights advocate with training in science. Her home experiments compared the solar heating of gases. In 1856 she proposed that a more carbon-dioxide-rich atmosphere could warm Earth, work long overlooked in accounts of climate science.

## Major contributions to physics

- Compared solar heating and cooling in different gas samples
- Proposed an early connection between atmospheric carbon dioxide abundance and Earth's temperature
- Investigated electrical properties of air and published pioneering work by an American woman in physics

## Historical and source notes

- Her apparatus compared solar heating and cooling of gases; it was not a direct measurement of the complete atmospheric greenhouse process or an infrared absorption spectrum.
