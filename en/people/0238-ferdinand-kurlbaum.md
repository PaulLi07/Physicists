# Ferdinand Kurlbaum

**Born:** 4 October 1857

**Died / status:** 29 July 1927

**Fields:** Thermal radiation, Radiometry, Experimental techniques

![Portrait of Ferdinand Kurlbaum](../portraits/306832212ccb14f911cc-5820a8f343.jpg)

For the image’s date, creator, form and attribution, see its source record.

## Biography

Kurlbaum was a German experimental physicist at the Physikalisch-Technische Reichsanstalt and technical institutions in Berlin. He improved thermal-radiation detectors and absolute measurements, collaborating with Rubens on data crucial to testing radiation theories around 1900.

## Major contributions to physics

- Improved bolometers and absolute radiation measurements
- Investigated long-wavelength black-body radiation with Rubens
- Studied radiated power and high-temperature measurement standards
