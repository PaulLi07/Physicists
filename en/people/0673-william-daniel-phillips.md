## Biography

William D. Phillips was born in Wilkes-Barre, PA, USA and conducted research in atomic physics and laser cooling. Nobel records list National Institute of Standards and Technology as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed methods to cool and trap atoms with laser light. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1997.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed methods to cool and trap atoms with laser light
- Provided foundations for ultracold-atom experiments, precision measurements and quantum gases

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1997
