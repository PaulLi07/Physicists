## Biography

Theodor W. Hänsch was born in Heidelberg, Germany and conducted research in precision spectroscopy and laser physics. Nobel records list Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics and Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München as the affiliations at the time of the award. They developed high-precision laser spectroscopy and optical-frequency combs. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2005.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed high-precision laser spectroscopy and optical-frequency combs
- Connected optical frequencies precisely with countable radio frequencies

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2005
