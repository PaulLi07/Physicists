# John D. Kraus

**Born:** 28 June 1910

**Died / status:** 18 July 2004

**Fields:** Radio astronomy, Electromagnetism

![Portrait of John D. Kraus](../portraits/aa18770af89fc8ad20fa-e17db985c1.jpg)

Sourced portrait or photograph; see the image record for provenance and reuse conditions.

## Biography

An American physicist and antenna researcher educated at Michigan, Kraus taught at Ohio State. He developed antennas, radio telescopes, and astronomical surveys and wrote influential electromagnetics and antenna texts.

## Major contributions to physics

- Invented and developed helical and other radio antennas.
- Designed and built the Big Ear radio telescope and conducted radio surveys.
- Advanced radio astronomy and electromagnetic education.
