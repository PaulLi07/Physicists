# Ben Roy Mottelson

**Born:** 9 July 1926

**Died / status:** 13 May 2022

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Nuclear structure

## Biography

Ben Roy Mottelson was born in Chicago, IL, USA and conducted research in nuclear physics and nuclear structure. Nobel records list Nordita as the affiliation at the time of the award. They connected collective nuclear motion with individual-particle motion. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1975.

## Major contributions to physics

- Connected collective nuclear motion with individual-particle motion
- Developed a theory of the structure of deformed nuclei

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1975
