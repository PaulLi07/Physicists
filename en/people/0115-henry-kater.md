## Biography

Kater was a British soldier, surveyor and experimental investigator who worked on triangulation in India before studying instruments in Britain after retirement. His reversible compound pendulum improved gravity measurements. He also investigated length and mass standards and optical collimating devices.

## Major contributions to physics

- Invented Kater's reversible pendulum for precise measurements of local gravitational acceleration.
- Improved surveying, collimation and metrological instruments and compared standards of length and mass.
