# William John Macquorn Rankine

**Born:** 5 July 1820

**Died / status:** 24 December 1872

**Fields:** Thermodynamics, Engineering physics, Mechanics

![Portrait of William John Macquorn Rankine](../portraits/545af70ed1ebb52cd66f.jpg)

## Biography

Rankine was a Scottish engineer and physicist who worked in railway engineering before becoming professor of engineering at Glasgow. He systematically connected heat, mechanics and design and produced influential textbooks. His name is associated with a steam-power cycle and an absolute temperature scale.

## Major contributions to physics

- Helped develop thermodynamics and the analysis of heat-engine efficiency
- Developed the steam-power cycle associated with his name
- Advanced engineering mechanics and wave studies, linking physical laws with engineering practice
