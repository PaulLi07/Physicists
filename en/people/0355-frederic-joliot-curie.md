# Frédéric Joliot-Curie

**Born:** 19 March 1900

**Died / status:** 14 August 1958

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Radioactivity

## Biography

A French experimental physicist at the Radium Institute in Paris, Joliot-Curie shared the 1935 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Irène Joliot-Curie. After the war he helped establish France's atomic-energy organization and its early reactor programme.

## Major contributions to physics

- Co-discovered artificial radioactivity with Irène Joliot-Curie.
- Investigated neutron release in fission and the possibility of chain reactions.
- Helped develop early French nuclear reactors.
