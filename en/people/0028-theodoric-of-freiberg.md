# Theodoric of Freiberg

**Born:** c. 1250

**Died / status:** after 1310; exact year unknown

**Fields:** Geometrical optics, Physics of the rainbow

![Image associated with Theodoric of Freiberg](../portraits/5beb376782d3087bf94b-c6f3679818.jpg)

Detail of a later commemorative sculpture representing Theodoric on the Fortuna fountain in Freiberg, Germany, photographed by Unukorno in 2012. It is not a verified medieval likeness.

## Biography

Theodoric, or Dietrich, of Freiberg was a German Dominican scholar who studied in Paris and held offices in his order. He investigated knowledge, natural philosophy and optics and analyzed the rainbow through experiment and geometry. His exact birth and death years are not established.

## Major contributions to physics

- Used models such as water-filled glass spheres to analyze refraction and internal reflection in primary and secondary rainbows.
- Connected ray paths in individual droplets to the rainbow seen by an observer, advancing its quantitative explanation.

## Historical and source notes

- Exact life dates are unknown; birth is estimated around 1250, and he may have lived beyond 1310.
