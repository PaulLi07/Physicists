# Loránd Eötvös

**Born:** 27 July 1848

**Died / status:** 8 April 1919

**Fields:** Gravitational experiments, Geophysics, Surface physics

![Portrait of Loránd Eötvös](../portraits/f5ef9f5acbba1d85ba73.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Eötvös was a Hungarian physicist who worked in Budapest and also held public and academic offices. His highly sensitive torsion balances investigated gravitational fields and the relation between inertial and gravitational mass, while his earlier research addressed liquid surfaces.

## Major contributions to physics

- Conducted precision tests of the proportionality of inertial and gravitational mass
- Developed torsion-balance methods for measuring gravity gradients
- Established an empirical relation between liquid surface tension and temperature
