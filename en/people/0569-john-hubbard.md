# John Hubbard

**Born:** 27 October 1931

**Died / status:** 27 November 1980

**Fields:** Condensed-matter theory, Strongly correlated electrons, Statistical physics

![Portrait of John Hubbard](../portraits/b6000d4602a2d406dbf3-8664b1409b.jpg)

Historical photograph of the physicist John Hubbard, retaining the original memorial heading and the caption “Susono, November, 1980”; from the JINR-hosted scientific biography.

## Biography

Hubbard was a British theoretical physicist trained at Imperial College who worked at Harwell and later IBM in the United States. He studied solids through simplified models retaining essential electronic interactions. His work shaped strongly correlated electron physics and many-body theory before his early death.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced the Hubbard model combining electron hopping with local interactions
- Developed theoretical methods and operator descriptions for electronic correlations
- Investigated electron gases, magnetism and metal–insulator behaviour
