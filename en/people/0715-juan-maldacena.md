## Biography

An Argentine-born theorist with a Princeton doctorate, Maldacena taught at Harvard before joining the Institute for Advanced Study. His research connects black holes, quantum field theory, and quantum gravity.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed the AdS/CFT correspondence between certain gravitational theories and lower-dimensional quantum field theories.
- Investigated black-hole thermodynamics, entanglement, and holography.
- Advanced calculations in strongly coupled quantum systems.
