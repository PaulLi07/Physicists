# Archimedes

**Born:** c. 287 BCE

**Died / status:** c. 212 BCE

**Fields:** Statics, Hydrostatics, Mathematical physics

![Archimedes — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/856ce534714aae611fce.jpg)

A later painting, sculpture or traditional representation; it is not a verified contemporary likeness.

## Biography

Archimedes was a mathematician, engineer and investigator of mechanics in Syracuse. He communicated with scholars in Alexandria and combined rigorous geometrical demonstrations with mechanical reasoning. His work addressed equilibrium, floating bodies and machines. He was killed when Roman forces captured Syracuse.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed mathematical treatments of levers and centres of gravity, advancing statics.
- Established principles of buoyancy and equilibrium in On Floating Bodies, a foundational work of hydrostatics.
- Used mechanical reasoning to assist geometrical quadrature and investigated devices such as the water-raising screw.
