# Nicolay Gennadiyevich Basov

**Born:** 14 December 1922

**Died / status:** 1 July 2001

**Fields:** Quantum electronics, Lasers

## Biography

Nicolay Gennadiyevich Basov was born in Usman, USSR (now Russia) and conducted research in quantum electronics and lasers. Nobel records list P.N. Lebedev Physical Institute as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed microwave amplification and oscillation based on stimulated emission. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1964.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed microwave amplification and oscillation based on stimulated emission
- Established important theoretical and experimental foundations for masers and lasers

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1964
