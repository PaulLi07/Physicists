# Kristian Birkeland

**Born:** 13 December 1867

**Died / status:** 15 June 1917

**Fields:** Space physics, Plasma physics, Applied physics

![Portrait of Kristian Birkeland](../portraits/c5fd51590b2150ebdbf4-0e1b2eb37b.jpg)

## Biography

Birkeland was a Norwegian physicist at the University of Christiania. He combined Arctic observations with magnetized-sphere and electron-beam experiments to investigate aurorae, while also developing an industrial electric-arc process for nitrogen fixation.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed an influential model attributing aurorae to charged particles arriving from the Sun
- Investigated auroral and space-current processes through magnetic observations and terrella experiments
- Developed electric-arc nitrogen fixation with Eyde
