# Stanley Deser

**Born:** 19 March 1931

**Died / status:** 21 April 2023

**Fields:** General relativity, Supergravity

## Biography

A Polish-born American theorist at Brandeis, Deser studied canonical gravity, supergravity, and consistency in field theory. His work connected general relativity with quantum-field methods.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the ADM canonical formulation with Arnowitt and Misner.
- Independently constructed supergravity with Zumino.
- Investigated consistency of gravitational and higher-spin theories.
