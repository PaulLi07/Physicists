# Martin L. Perl

**Born:** 24 June 1927

**Died / status:** 30 September 2014

**Fields:** Particle physics, Leptons

## Biography

Martin L. Perl was born in New York, NY, USA and conducted research in particle physics and leptons. Nobel records list Stanford University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They discovered the tau lepton in electron-positron collision experiments. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1995.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the tau lepton in electron-positron collision experiments
- Established the charged member of the third lepton generation

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1995
