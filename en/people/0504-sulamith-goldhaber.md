# Sulamith Goldhaber

**Born:** 4 November 1923

**Died / status:** 11 December 1965

**Fields:** Particle physics, Particle detection

## Biography

An Austrian-born experimental physicist educated in Israel, Goldhaber worked at Berkeley. She developed emulsion and bubble-chamber experiments to investigate energetic mesons and strange particles.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed nuclear-emulsion experiments for high-energy particles.
- Investigated kaon–nucleon interactions.
- Analyzed bubble-chamber data and hadronic resonances.
