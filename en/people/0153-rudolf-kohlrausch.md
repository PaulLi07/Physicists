# Rudolf Kohlrausch

**Born:** 6 November 1809

**Died / status:** March 1858 (sources differ between 8 and 9 March)

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Dielectric physics, Metrology

![Portrait of Rudolf Kohlrausch](../portraits/70173761948973d40e42-8b4981fbdb.jpg)

For the image’s date, creator, form and attribution, see its source record.

## Biography

Rudolf Kohlrausch was a German physicist who taught in schools and universities and specialized in electrical measurement. His comparison of electrical unit systems with Wilhelm Weber became important to electromagnetic theory. His son Friedrich also became a leading experimental physicist.

## Major contributions to physics

- Measured the relation between electrostatic and electromagnetic units with Weber
- Investigated dielectric relaxation and introduced a stretched-exponential decay form
- Advanced electrostatic measurement and electrical metrology

## Historical and source notes

- Sources disagree between 8 and 9 March 1858, so the date is recorded only to the month. The Erlangen professors catalogue gives 8 March; the Marburg catalogue and Allgemeine Deutsche Biographie text give 9 March. Deutsche Biographie’s catalogue year 1859 conflicts with its own article text and is not adopted.
