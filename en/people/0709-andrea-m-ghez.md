## Biography

Andrea Ghez was born in New York, NY, USA and conducted research in astrophysics and Galactic Centre. Nobel records list University of California as the affiliation at the time of the award. They led high-resolution infrared imaging and orbit measurements of stars at the Galactic Centre. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2020.

## Major contributions to physics

- Led high-resolution infrared imaging and orbit measurements of stars at the Galactic Centre
- Made decisive observational contributions to establishing the Milky Way's central supermassive black hole

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2020
