# Raymond Davis Jr.

**Born:** 14 October 1914

**Died / status:** 31 May 2006

**Fields:** Neutrino astronomy, Experimental physics

## Biography

Raymond Davis Jr. was born in Washington, D.C., USA and conducted research in neutrino astronomy and experimental physics. Nobel records list University of Pennsylvania as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed an underground chlorine detector to measure solar neutrinos. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2002.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed an underground chlorine detector to measure solar neutrinos
- Observed a deficit in the solar-neutrino flux, establishing the solar-neutrino problem

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2002
