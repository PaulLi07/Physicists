## Biography

Samuel Chao Chung Ting was born in Ann Arbor, MI, USA and conducted research in particle physics and experimental physics. Nobel records list Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) as the affiliation at the time of the award. They led the Brookhaven experiment that discovered the J particle, identical to the psi found by Richter's group. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1976.

## Major contributions to physics

- Led the Brookhaven experiment that discovered the J particle, identical to the psi found by Richter's group
- Advanced precision particle spectroscopy and space-based particle detection

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1976
