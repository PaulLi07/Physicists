# Peter Mansfield

**Born:** 9 October 1933

**Died / status:** 8 February 2017

**Fields:** Nuclear magnetic resonance, Magnetic resonance imaging, Experimental physics

## Biography

Mansfield was a British physicist educated in London who spent most of his academic career at Nottingham. Moving from solid-state NMR to spatial imaging, he developed methods for analysing gradient-encoded signals and acquiring images rapidly. He shared the 2003 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Paul Lauterbur.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed spatial encoding and mathematical analysis of NMR signals in magnetic-field gradients
- Proposed and developed rapid MRI methods including echo-planar imaging
- Helped turn the physical principles of NMR into spatial imaging for medicine
