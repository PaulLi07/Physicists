## Biography

Carl E. Wieman was born in Corvallis, OR, USA and conducted research in atomic physics and quantum gases. Nobel records list University of Colorado, JILA as the affiliation at the time of the award. They realized Bose-Einstein condensation in dilute atomic gases. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2001.

## Major contributions to physics

- Realized Bose-Einstein condensation in dilute atomic gases
- Opened experimental research on controllable macroscopic quantum states in atomic gases

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2001
