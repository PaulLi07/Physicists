# Walter Kohn

**Born:** 9 March 1923

**Died / status:** 19 April 2016

**Fields:** Condensed-matter theory, Density-functional theory, Electronic structure

## Biography

Kohn was an Austrian-born American theoretical physicist who escaped Nazi persecution as a teenager and studied in Canada and the United States. He later worked at UC San Diego and UC Santa Barbara, where he became the founding director of the Institute for Theoretical Physics. His development of density-functional theory earned the 1998 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established foundational theorems of ground-state density-functional theory with Pierre Hohenberg
- Developed the self-consistent Kohn–Sham equations for electronic-structure calculations with Lu Jeu Sham
- Made major contributions to semiconductor physics, metallic electronic structure and surface physics
