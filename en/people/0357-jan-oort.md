# Jan Oort

**Born:** 28 April 1900

**Died / status:** 5 November 1992

**Fields:** Galactic astronomy, Radio astronomy

## Biography

A Dutch astronomer and director of Leiden Observatory, Oort began with stellar motions and later helped build Dutch radio astronomy. His observations revealed the structure and differential rotation of the Milky Way.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established differential Galactic rotation and the Oort constants.
- Advanced the use of the 21-centimetre hydrogen line to map Galactic structure.
- Proposed the distant comet reservoir now called the Oort cloud.
