## Biography

Michel H. Devoret was born in Paris, France and conducted research in condensed-matter physics, superconducting circuits and quantum mechanics. Nobel records list Yale University, University of California and Google Quantum AI as the affiliations at the time of the award. They discovered macroscopic quantum tunnelling and energy quantization in electrical circuits. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2025.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered macroscopic quantum tunnelling and energy quantization in electrical circuits
- Established foundations for controllable quantum behaviour in superconducting circuits

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2025
