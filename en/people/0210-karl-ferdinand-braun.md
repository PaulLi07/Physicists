## Biography

Karl Ferdinand Braun was born in Fulda, Hesse-Kassel (now Germany) and conducted research in electromagnetism and electronic instruments. Nobel records list Strasbourg University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They improved radio-transmission circuits and coupled tuning methods. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1909.

## Major contributions to physics

- Improved radio-transmission circuits and coupled tuning methods
- Developed the cathode-ray oscillograph, an important foundation for electronic measurement instruments

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1909
