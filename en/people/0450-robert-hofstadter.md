# Robert Hofstadter

**Born:** 5 February 1915

**Died / status:** 17 November 1990

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Scattering experiments

## Biography

Robert Hofstadter was born in New York, NY, USA and conducted research in nuclear physics and scattering experiments. Nobel records list Stanford University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They used high-energy electron scattering to measure nuclear charge distributions. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1961.

## Major contributions to physics

- Used high-energy electron scattering to measure nuclear charge distributions
- Investigated the electromagnetic structure of protons and neutrons

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1961
