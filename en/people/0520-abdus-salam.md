# Abdus Salam

**Born:** 29 January 1926

**Died / status:** 21 November 1996

**Fields:** Particle physics, Electroweak theory

![Portrait of Abdus Salam](../portraits/62c91d4e6bdb9f872406.jpg)

For the photographer or artist, date and type of image, consult the original image record.

## Biography

Abdus Salam was born in Jhang Maghiāna, India (now Pakistan) and conducted research in particle physics and electroweak theory. Nobel records list International Centre for Theoretical Physics and Imperial College as the affiliations at the time of the award. They developed a unified theory of electromagnetic and weak interactions. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1979.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed a unified theory of electromagnetic and weak interactions
- Predicted and explained phenomena including weak neutral currents

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1979
