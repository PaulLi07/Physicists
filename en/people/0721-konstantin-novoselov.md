## Biography

Konstantin Novoselov was born in Nizhny Tagil, Russia and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and two-dimensional materials. Nobel records list University of Manchester as the affiliation at the time of the award. They isolated and experimentally investigated graphene. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2010.

## Major contributions to physics

- Isolated and experimentally investigated graphene
- Revealed distinctive electronic and quantum-transport properties of a two-dimensional carbon material

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2010
