# Ernest Orlando Lawrence

**Born:** 8 August 1901

**Died / status:** 27 August 1958

**Fields:** Accelerator physics, Nuclear physics

## Biography

Ernest Orlando Lawrence was born in Canton, SD, USA and conducted research in accelerator physics and nuclear physics. Nobel records list University of California as the affiliation at the time of the award. They invented and developed the cyclotron. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1939.

## Major contributions to physics

- Invented and developed the cyclotron
- Made higher-energy charged-particle beams available for nuclear research

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1939
