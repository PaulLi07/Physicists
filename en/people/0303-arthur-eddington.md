# Arthur Eddington

**Born:** 28 December 1882

**Died / status:** 22 November 1944

**Fields:** Stellar physics, General relativity

## Biography

Eddington was a British astrophysicist at Cambridge who combined research on stellar interiors with advocacy of general relativity. He helped organize the 1919 eclipse observations that brought Einstein's gravitational theory international attention.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed theories of stellar structure and radiative equilibrium.
- Formulated the luminosity limit associated with the balance of radiation pressure and gravity.
- Participated in the 1919 observations of gravitational light deflection.
