# Michael Faraday

**Born:** 22 September 1791

**Died / status:** 25 August 1867

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Electrochemistry, History of field theory

## Biography

Faraday was a British experimental physicist and chemist who trained as a bookbinder before joining the Royal Institution. Without extensive higher mathematical training, he explored relationships among electricity, magnetism, chemistry and light through ingenious experiments. His field-based insights strongly influenced Maxwell's theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered electromagnetic induction, providing the physical basis of generators and transformers.
- Established the laws of electrolysis connecting transferred charge to chemical change.
- Discovered magnetically induced optical rotation and developed lines-of-force ideas that advanced field concepts.
