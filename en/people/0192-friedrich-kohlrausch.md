# Friedrich Kohlrausch

**Born:** 14 October 1840

**Died / status:** 17 January 1910

**Fields:** Electrochemistry, Precision measurement, Metrology

## Biography

Friedrich Kohlrausch was a German experimental physicist who taught in Würzburg and Strasbourg and later headed the Physikalisch-Technische Reichsanstalt. He combined precision measurement with electrolyte research and laboratory teaching, producing an influential practical physics textbook.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established independent ionic migration at infinite dilution
- Developed precision conductivity measurements, including alternating-current bridge methods
- Advanced electrical standards, metrology and systematic laboratory teaching
