# Evgeny Lifshitz

**Born:** 21 February 1915

**Died / status:** 29 October 1985

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Theoretical physics

## Biography

A Soviet theorist and long-term collaborator of Landau, Lifshitz coauthored the Course of Theoretical Physics. His research addressed magnetism, intermolecular interactions, and cosmology, alongside a major contribution to theoretical-physics education.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the Landau–Lifshitz equation for magnetization dynamics with Landau.
- Established the macroscopic theory of dispersion forces between media.
- Investigated dynamics near cosmological singularities.
