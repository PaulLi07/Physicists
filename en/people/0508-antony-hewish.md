# Antony Hewish

**Born:** 11 May 1924

**Died / status:** 13 September 2021

**Fields:** Radio astronomy, Pulsars

## Biography

Antony Hewish was born in Fowey, United Kingdom and conducted research in radio astronomy and pulsars. Nobel records list University of Cambridge as the affiliation at the time of the award. They led construction of the interplanetary-scintillation radio array and contributed to the discovery and interpretation of pulsars. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1974.

## Major contributions to physics

- Led construction of the interplanetary-scintillation radio array and contributed to the discovery and interpretation of pulsars
- Jocelyn Bell Burnell first recognized the unusual pulsed signals in the array's data

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1974
