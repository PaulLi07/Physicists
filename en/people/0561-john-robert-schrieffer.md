# John Robert Schrieffer

**Born:** 31 May 1931

**Died / status:** 27 July 2019

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Superconductivity

## Biography

John Robert Schrieffer was born in Oak Park, IL, USA and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and superconductivity. Nobel records list University of Pennsylvania as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed the BCS microscopic theory of superconductivity. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1972.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the BCS microscopic theory of superconductivity
- Explained conventional superconductivity through electron pairing and condensation

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1972
