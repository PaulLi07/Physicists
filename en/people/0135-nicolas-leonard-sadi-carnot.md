# Nicolas Léonard Sadi Carnot

**Born:** 1 June 1796

**Died / status:** 24 August 1832

**Fields:** Thermodynamics, Heat-engine theory

## Biography

Sadi Carnot was a French military engineer educated at the École Polytechnique who also pursued independent research. His short book of 1824 analyzed heat-engine efficiency through ideal engines and reversible processes. His ideas received their full development only after his death.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced the ideal reversible heat engine and the cycle bearing his name.
- Identified general limits on engine efficiency set by hot and cold reservoirs, laying foundations for the second law of thermodynamics.

## Historical and source notes

- His 1824 theory still used caloric terminology; Clapeyron, Clausius, Kelvin and others later developed its ideas about reversibility and efficiency.
