# Robert Brout

**Born:** 14 June 1928

**Died / status:** 3 May 2011

## Biography

An American-born Belgian theorist, Brout worked at Cornell before joining the Free University of Brussels. His collaboration with Englert on spontaneous symmetry breaking made him a co-originator of a mechanism for gauge-boson masses.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed spontaneous symmetry breaking in gauge theory with Englert.
- Showed how scalar fields can generate gauge-field masses.
- Investigated phase transitions, statistical physics, and cosmology.
