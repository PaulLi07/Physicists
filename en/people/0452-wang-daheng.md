# Wang Daheng

**Born:** 26 February 1915

**Died / status:** 21 July 2011

**Fields:** Optics, Optical engineering

## Biography

Born in Tokyo to a family from Jiangsu, Wang studied at Tsinghua and investigated optical glass in Britain before returning to China in 1948. He helped establish the Changchun optics institute and develop precision optical instruments.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated optical glass and materials.
- Organized precision optical instrumentation, testing, and defence-optics research.
- Helped establish China's optics community and advocate its high-technology programme.
