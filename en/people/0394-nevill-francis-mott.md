# Sir Nevill Francis Mott

**Born:** 30 September 1905

**Died / status:** 8 August 1996

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Electron correlations

## Biography

Sir Nevill Francis Mott was born in Leeds, United Kingdom and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and electron correlations. Nobel records list University of Cambridge as the affiliation at the time of the award. They investigated the role of electron correlations in metal-insulator transitions. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1977.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated the role of electron correlations in metal-insulator transitions
- Developed theories of electronic behaviour in amorphous and disordered solids

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1977
