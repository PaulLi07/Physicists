# Joseph Boussinesq

**Born:** 13 March 1842

**Died / status:** 19 February 1929

**Fields:** Fluid mechanics, Waves, Continuum mechanics

## Biography

Boussinesq was a French mathematician and physicist who taught in Lille and Paris. He developed mathematical descriptions of fluids, waves, heat and elasticity. His approximations made complex continuum problems more tractable while retaining the physical effects important to each regime.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy-driven flow
- Developed the Boussinesq equations for nonlinear shallow-water long waves
- Developed the eddy-viscosity concept for turbulence and the theory of elastic contact problems
