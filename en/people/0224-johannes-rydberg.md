# Johannes Rydberg

**Born:** 8 November 1854

**Died / status:** 28 December 1919

**Fields:** Atomic spectroscopy, Atomic structure, Mathematical physics

![Portrait of Johannes Rydberg](../portraits/36a1f99b61411b86323d-98dbdfeb75.jpg)

## Biography

Rydberg was a Swedish physicist at Lund whose early training was in mathematics. By comparing spectra of different elements and expressing their patterns in wavenumbers, he organized complicated observations into regularities that became crucial to quantum atomic theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Formulated spectral-series relations in wavenumber form
- Established systematic comparisons of spectral lines and the Rydberg constant
- Provided empirical foundations for energy-level descriptions, the Ritz combination principle and later quantum atomic models
