# Klaus von Klitzing

**Born:** 28 June 1943

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Quantum Hall effect

## Biography

Klaus von Klitzing was born in Schroda, German-occupied Poland (now Poland) and conducted research in condensed-matter physics and quantum Hall effect. Nobel records list Max-Planck-Institut für Festkörperforschung as the affiliation at the time of the award. They discovered the integer quantum Hall effect. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1985.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the integer quantum Hall effect
- Established precise quantization of Hall conductance in a two-dimensional electron system

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1985
