# James Clerk Maxwell

**Born:** 13 June 1831

**Died / status:** 5 November 1879

**Fields:** Electromagnetism, Statistical physics, Optics

![Portrait of James Clerk Maxwell](../portraits/3afa3f9d758d97952f52.jpg)

## Biography

Maxwell was a Scottish physicist who taught in Aberdeen and London before becoming Cambridge's first Cavendish Professor and organizing its laboratory. He translated Faraday's field ideas into mathematical theory and also investigated molecules, colour vision and the stability of Saturn's rings.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established classical electromagnetic field theory, predicted electromagnetic waves and identified light as an electromagnetic phenomenon
- Derived a molecular speed distribution and advanced kinetic and transport theory
- Studied trichromatic colour vision and the stability of Saturn's rings, and helped establish the Cavendish Laboratory
