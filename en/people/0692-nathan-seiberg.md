**Born:** 22 September 1956

## Biography

An Israeli-born American theorist, Seiberg worked at the Weizmann Institute and Rutgers before joining the Institute for Advanced Study. He uses duality, symmetry, and exact results to investigate strongly coupled quantum fields.

## Major contributions to physics

- Solved important supersymmetric gauge-theory low-energy dynamics with Witten.
- Proposed Seiberg duality.
- Advanced nonperturbative field theory and generalized-symmetry research.
