# Benjamin Franklin

**Born:** 17 January 1706

**Died / status:** 17 April 1790

**Fields:** Electrostatics, Atmospheric electricity, Experimental physics

![Benjamin Franklin — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/a3e473bb4da281fc3b76.jpg)

The creator, date and form of this likeness are documented at the image source.

## Biography

Franklin was an American scientist, printer and statesman, born in Boston and active mainly in Philadelphia before undertaking diplomatic service. Experiments and correspondence brought him into European scientific networks. He gained an international reputation in electricity and also investigated weather, ocean currents and practical technologies.

## Major contributions to physics

- Helped establish the electrical nature of lightning and promoted the lightning conductor.
- Developed a one-fluid account of positive and negative electricity that emphasized conservation during charge transfer.
