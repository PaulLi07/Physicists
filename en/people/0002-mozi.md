# Mozi

**Born:** c. 470 BCE

**Died / status:** c. 391 BCE

**Fields:** Early Chinese physical science, History of optics, History of mechanics

![Mozi — portrait or historical likeness](../portraits/8d4f0be283f19c21c600.jpg)

A later painting, sculpture or traditional representation; it is not a verified contemporary likeness.

## Biography

Mozi was a Chinese thinker of the Warring States period and the founder of the Mohist school. He valued practical techniques and reasoned argument. The surviving Mozi contains material from different periods: its technical discussions of optics, mechanics and geometry in the Mohist Canons largely belong to later Mohists, rather than to Mozi alone.

## Major contributions to physics

- His school preserved early Chinese discussions of shadows, reflection and pinhole images.
- Mohist writings examined force, motion, balance and space, providing important evidence for the history of Chinese physical thought.

## Historical and source notes

- The optical and mechanical discussions in the Mohist Canons are collective achievements of later Mohists and must not all be attributed to Mozi personally.
- The birth and death years are disputed estimates; this archive uses approximately 470–391 BCE.
