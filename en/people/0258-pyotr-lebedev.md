# Pyotr Lebedev

**Born:** 8 March 1866

**Died / status:** 14 March 1912

**Fields:** Radiation physics, Electromagnetism, Precision measurement

![Portrait of Pyotr Lebedev](../portraits/4d54a8d403a91866e7a4.jpg)

## Biography

Lebedev was a Russian experimental physicist who trained in Germany and established a research laboratory at Moscow University. With extremely sensitive torsion balances, he measured radiation pressure to test the momentum carried by electromagnetic fields and helped develop Russian experimental physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Measured the pressure of light on solids
- Extended radiation-pressure measurements to gases
- Developed techniques for detecting very weak forces
