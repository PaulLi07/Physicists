# Gérard Mourou

**Born:** 22 June 1944

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Laser physics, Ultrafast optics

## Biography

Gérard Mourou was born in Albertville, France and conducted research in laser physics and ultrafast optics. Nobel records list École Polytechnique and University of Michigan as the affiliations at the time of the award. They proposed chirped-pulse amplification with Donna Strickland. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2018.

## Major contributions to physics

- Proposed chirped-pulse amplification with Donna Strickland
- Enabled intense ultrashort pulses by stretching, amplifying and recompressing laser pulses

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 2018
