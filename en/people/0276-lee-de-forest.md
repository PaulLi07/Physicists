# Lee de Forest

**Born:** 26 August 1873

**Died / status:** 30 June 1961

**Fields:** Electronic devices, Radio physics, Applied physics

## Biography

De Forest was an American inventor and electronics pioneer trained in physics and electricity at Yale. His work on radio devices introduced the control-grid Audion tube, an important step toward electronic amplification and oscillation; he also investigated sound-on-film systems.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed the control-grid Audion triode
- Advanced the use of vacuum tubes in amplification, modulation and oscillation
- Investigated radio broadcasting and electronic sound-on-film recording
