# Leo Esaki

**Born:** 12 March 1925

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Semiconductor physics, Quantum tunnelling

## Biography

Leo Esaki was born in Osaka, Japan and conducted research in semiconductor physics and quantum tunnelling. Nobel records list IBM Thomas J. Watson Research Center as the affiliation at the time of the award. They discovered electron tunnelling in heavily doped semiconductor junctions. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1973.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered electron tunnelling in heavily doped semiconductor junctions
- Developed the Esaki tunnel diode

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1973
