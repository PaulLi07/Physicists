# Immanuel Bloch

**Born:** 16 November 1972

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** Ultracold atoms, Quantum many-body physics

## Biography

A German experimental physicist trained in Hänsch's group, Bloch worked in Mainz, Munich, and the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics. He uses ultracold atoms in optical lattices to build controllable many-body quantum simulators.

## Major contributions to physics

- Helped realize a superfluid-to-Mott-insulator quantum transition.
- Developed control and imaging of optical-lattice quantum gases.
- Simulated strongly correlated many-body models with ultracold atoms.
