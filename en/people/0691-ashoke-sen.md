# Ashoke Sen

**Born:** 15 July 1956

**Died / status:** Living (as of 2026-10-26)

**Fields:** String theory, Quantum gravity

![Portrait of Ashoke Sen](../portraits/676445beed52b087c0f4-1ce5b37d11.jpg)

## Biography

An Indian theorist who worked in the United States and at the Harish-Chandra Research Institute before joining ICTS, Sen made foundational contributions to string duality, black-hole microstates, and string field theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established key evidence for strong–weak string dualities.
- Investigated extremal black-hole entropy and microstate counting.
- Developed tachyon-condensation and string-field-theory methods.
