# Jack Steinberger

**Born:** 25 May 1921

**Died / status:** 12 December 2020

**Fields:** Particle physics, Neutrinos

## Biography

Jack Steinberger was born in Bad Kissingen, Germany and conducted research in particle physics and neutrinos. Nobel records list CERN as the affiliation at the time of the award. They used a high-energy neutrino beam to discover the muon neutrino. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1988.

## Major contributions to physics

- Used a high-energy neutrino beam to discover the muon neutrino
- Established that electron and muon neutrinos are distinct

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1988
