# Jean le Rond d'Alembert

**Born:** 16 November 1717

**Died / status:** 29 October 1783

**Fields:** Analytical mechanics, Wave equations, Fluid mechanics

## Biography

D'Alembert was a French mathematician and Enlightenment thinker, abandoned as an infant and educated in Paris, who later co-edited the Encyclopédie. He translated mechanical problems into mathematical relationships and investigated vibrations, fluids and celestial motion, strongly influencing differential equations and analytical mechanics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Formulated d'Alembert's principle, connecting dynamics to an equilibrium formulation using virtual work.
- Established and solved the wave equation for a vibrating string and investigated fluid motion and resistance.
