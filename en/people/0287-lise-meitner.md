# Lise Meitner

**Born:** 7 November 1878

**Died / status:** 27 October 1968

**Fields:** Nuclear physics, Radioactivity, Nuclear fission

![Portrait of Lise Meitner](../portraits/0ccef9d10cf72460bd73.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Meitner was an Austrian-born nuclear physicist who later became a Swedish citizen. She worked for many years with Hahn in Berlin, fled Nazi persecution in 1938, and then interpreted uranium fission with Frisch. Her career also broke major barriers facing women in physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Investigated radioactive nuclides with Hahn and helped discover the long-lived isotope of protactinium
- Explained uranium fission with Frisch and estimated its energy release
- Studied beta spectra and nuclear radiation
