# Jožef Stefan

**Born:** 24 March 1835

**Died / status:** 7 January 1893

**Fields:** Thermal radiation, Heat conduction, Phase transitions

## Biography

Stefan was a Slovenian-speaking Austrian physicist from Carinthia who worked at Vienna and taught Boltzmann. He drew quantitative laws from thermal experiments and investigated radiation, transport and phase changes, leaving lasting contributions to heat physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Inferred the fourth-power temperature law of thermal radiation from experimental data
- Investigated diffusion and heat conduction
- Studied moving freezing and melting boundaries, associated with the Stefan problem
