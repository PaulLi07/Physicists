# Pascual Jordan

**Born:** 18 October 1902

**Died / status:** 31 July 1980

**Fields:** Quantum mechanics, Quantum field theory

![Portrait of Pascual Jordan](../portraits/866a3101429c843e301d-0aec98d2ea.jpg)

Sourced portrait or photograph; see the image record for provenance and reuse conditions.

## Biography

A German theorist who collaborated with Born and Heisenberg in Göttingen, Jordan later taught in Rostock, Berlin, and Hamburg. He helped establish quantum mechanics and field quantization and investigated algebraic and gravitational theories.

## Major contributions to physics

- Helped establish matrix mechanics and canonical quantization.
- Developed fermionic anticommutation relations and field quantization.
- Introduced Jordan algebras and investigated scalar–tensor gravitation.

## Historical and source notes

- Jordan joined the Nazi Party in 1933 and later entered postwar politics; his scientific achievements should be presented together with this historical context.
