# Pieter Zeeman

**Born:** 25 May 1865

**Died / status:** 9 October 1943

**Fields:** Spectroscopy, Electromagnetism

## Biography

Pieter Zeeman was born in Zonnemaire, the Netherlands and conducted research in spectroscopy and electromagnetism. Nobel records list Amsterdam University as the affiliation at the time of the award. They discovered the splitting of atomic spectral lines in a magnetic field, the Zeeman effect. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1902.

## Major contributions to physics

- Discovered the splitting of atomic spectral lines in a magnetic field, the Zeeman effect
- Used spectroscopy to reveal the relation between atomic radiation and magnetic fields

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1902
