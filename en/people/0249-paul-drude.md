# Paul Drude

**Born:** 12 July 1863

**Died / status:** 5 July 1906

**Fields:** Condensed-matter physics, Electronic transport, Optics

## Biography

Drude was a German theoretical and experimental physicist who worked in Göttingen, Leipzig, Giessen and Berlin and edited an important physics journal. He used mobile electrons to explain metallic transport and also investigated optics, helping establish classical electron theory.

## Major contributions to physics

- Introduced the Drude free-electron model for electrical and thermal conduction in metals
- Advanced metallic optics and electromagnetic propagation studies
- Developed a classical collision-based transport picture later refined by quantum theory
