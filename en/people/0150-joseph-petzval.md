# Joseph Petzval

**Born:** 6 January 1807

**Died / status:** 17 September 1891

**Fields:** Applied optics, Imaging physics

![Portrait of Joseph Petzval](../portraits/1348b3cc2e34ecd8dcc0.jpg)

For the date, creator, form and attribution of this image, see its source record.

## Biography

Petzval was a mathematician and optical researcher born in what is now Slovakia who taught at the University of Vienna. He applied mathematical calculation to photographic lenses, working with instrument makers to obtain brighter images and much shorter portrait exposures.

## Major contributions to physics

- Designed the high-aperture Petzval portrait lens
- Investigated optical aberrations and the Petzval field-curvature condition
- Helped move lens design from empirical trial toward systematic mathematical calculation
