# Isaak Khalatnikov

**Born:** 17 October 1919

**Died / status:** 9 January 2021

**Fields:** Low-temperature physics, General relativity

## Biography

A Soviet and Russian theorist, Khalatnikov collaborated with Landau and directed the Landau Institute. He investigated superfluids, quantum liquids, and cosmology, extending many-body methods to relativistic dynamics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed Landau–Khalatnikov dynamics of superfluid helium.
- Investigated cosmological singularities with Belinski and Lifshitz, leading to BKL behaviour.
- Studied quantum liquids and relativistic fluids.
