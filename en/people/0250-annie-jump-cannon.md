# Annie Jump Cannon

**Born:** 11 December 1863

**Died / status:** 13 April 1941

**Fields:** Stellar physics, Spectroscopy, Observational astronomy

## Biography

Cannon was an American astronomer who devoted her career at Harvard to stellar classification and catalogue work. Her skilled examination of hundreds of thousands of spectra organized earlier systems into the familiar spectral sequence, providing a major empirical foundation for stellar physics.

## Major contributions to physics

- Established and extensively applied the O–B–A–F–G–K–M spectral sequence
- Led or contributed to the Henry Draper Catalogue and its extensions
- Discovered and investigated variable stars and enabled comparisons across stellar populations
