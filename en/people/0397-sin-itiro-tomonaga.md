# Sin-Itiro Tomonaga

**Born:** 31 March 1906

**Died / status:** 8 July 1979

**Fields:** Quantum electrodynamics, Particle physics

## Biography

Sin-Itiro Tomonaga was born in Kyoto, Japan and conducted research in quantum electrodynamics and particle physics. Nobel records list Tokyo University of Education as the affiliation at the time of the award. They developed renormalized quantum electrodynamics. They were awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1965.

## Major contributions to physics

- Developed renormalized quantum electrodynamics
- Enabled highly precise calculations of interactions between electrons and electromagnetic fields

**Nobel Prize in Physics:** 1965
